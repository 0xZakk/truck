Bolt Science — Short Bolting
Source: https://www.boltscience.com/pages/shortbolting.htm
Reviewed: 2026-09-26
Record type: research summary, not a raw page copy.

The article explains that incomplete threads near a bolt tip can reduce the effective engagement of a nut. It recommends accounting for those incomplete threads when selecting protrusion, matching nut and bolt strength, and distinguishing nut joints from tapped holes in other materials.

Application limit: general fastener guidance only. It does not identify Ford bracket hardware, establish the truck’s thread pitch or length, or validate the modeled joint’s load capacity.
