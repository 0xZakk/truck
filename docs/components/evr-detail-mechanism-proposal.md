## Complete comparative mechanism

Actual section drawing and text of Canadian Fram patentEP0124399A2 were inspected. Its positive-gain Fig2 arrangement provides a source restrictor, shared source/output chamber, disc-controlled atmospheric opening, bias spring, stator/coil, magnetic shell and filter. Fig3 is a different negative-gain arrangement and must not be combined with Fig2. The patent does not establish Ford or VS52 identity, dimensions, materials or calibration. Its adjustable stator is not evidence that the truck's part has an adjustment feature.

Combined with exact1994PCED coil/armature/disc/orifice prose and DN filtered-vent service evidence, this is sufficient to design a **clearly illustrative complete topology**, not a hidden factory replica:

1. Lower source nipple feeds an explicitly estimated restriction and a common chamber connected to the upper EGR nipple.
2. Atmospheric air enters the cap, crosses the filter and hollow core, then meets the disc-controlled opening to that chamber.
3. Moving the disc away from the core opens the atmospheric path. Seating it closes that path while preserving source-to-output continuity.
4. A comparison-supported bias spring holds the disc toward its seat; its rate/preload remain unknown. Coil envelope, insulating bobbin, hollow magnetic core, return shell and isolated terminal connections complete the explanatory decomposition.
5. An illustrative snap capture may use the source-observed slotted cap skirt. Any groove, bead, flexibility or force is estimated and must be labeled accordingly; actual cap retention remains unidentified.

The current vent-only body's direct upper-port/vent path must therefore be replaced by a chamber with a real controllable seat. Simply drilling the blind source nipple into the existing passage would create an uncontrolled bleed, not the requested explanatory mechanism. Protect mounting ears/holes, connector exterior, hose ends and occurrence frames. Internal clearances and seats should derive from the existing provisional envelope, explicitly identified as educational dimensions. No inferred production measurement, pressure curve or winding design is allowed.
