## Results and physical interpretation

All six actual canonical FS10 neighbors clear both the canonical block and pre-front combined block. The original front-joint future land alone introduces five intersections: rear cylinder 3773.224469 mm³; swashplate shaft 3184.202985 mm³; rear shoe 5 349.306661 mm³; front shoe 5 34.180035 mm³; case bolt 5 282.743339 mm³. These persist through attachment-v2, the first block adapter and expanded-seat V3. The pan transition revision is not their onset.

The seven-support revision adds a distinct station-6 fault: piston 5 overlap 312.432080 mm³. The frozen earlier decomposition also identifies 149.711263 mm³ of new shaft overlap and 133.620745 mm³ of old shaft overlap removed by the fastener drilling; net shaft overlap becomes 3200.293502 mm³. It would be misleading to use only the net increase. Exact first-land witnesses are exported, and actual X360 / X368 sections distinguish new rearward support from the original front rail.

At fixed compressor Z100, the estimated R65 barrel and R8 support require axis separation 73 mm; current separation is 37.984383 mm. A zero-margin illustration requires compressor Y≥320.289699, a +40.289699 mm shift. Holding the compressor fixed and using an estimated cam pocket R84.947 plus 6 mm wall suggests only a station-6 Y interval 194.042825..211.627108 at its current Z. That means an inward axis change of 40.289699..57.873981 mm. These are incomplete envelope bounds, not acceptable clearance specifications, and do not test the full gasket rail, carrier, belt, cover or compressor mechanism. Neither local change can be justified by the photo point-pick uncertainty. Moving one hole alone would leave the original rail conflicts unresolved.
