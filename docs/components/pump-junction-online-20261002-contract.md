2. **Positive backing.** Propose `seal_backing = extrude(seal_land_face,+X,3mm)` as a new *estimated* backing-depth requirement, not a factory wall specification. Root must approve this explicit revised scope before modeling. It preserves the entire mating face and a finite positive backing rather than assuming a14mm slab is source-exact. 3. **Dry bolt regions.** Preserve all four existing axes and actual head-seat faces. 4. **Fifth aperture.** Preserve the identified larger aperture at the mounting face, its sealing separation and a connection to the existing declared pump cavity. Its production function and detailed routing remain unknown; do not rename it a specific bypass or drill a new channel based on appearance alone.

Prior elliptical/round junction dimensions remain rejected estimates, not mandatory source geometry.
