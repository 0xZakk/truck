## Smallest actionable external input packages

- **Rod collision:** one identified rod/cap/bolt/nut set. Scaled head plan plus orthogonal sides; under-head radius and press shoulder; grip/thread and bore/nut-plane dimensions; rod boss and bolt centers tied to big-end axis; local block crankcase surface near the frozen witness in crank/cylinder coordinates. Correct orientation must accompany the data. Applicable drawings may replace measurements.
- **Oil-pump installation:** C5AZ6600A-applicable mount face relative to drive axis; bolt/dowel axes; discharge aperture distinct from shaft bore; block seat/gallery receiver; both gasket outlines/thicknesses; E3TZ6622F pickup flange and support/route. One source image without hidden-face geometry cannot close this package.
- **Water-pump tube/bearing:** identified Ford/Gates44009-applicable bearing marking or section with ring/shaft/row/seal geometry; calibrated root/end and intermediate tube coordinates tied to pump flange/shaft. Current photographs do not supply a conservative3D tube envelope. Retain separate access-procedure uncertainty.
- **Airbox/ducts:** vehicle-registered six-degree-of-freedom box frame, paired outlet identities, full-circumference hose-stop and insertion datums, surrounding body/hood boundaries and declared relative engine motion. Frame absence is exact and unavoidable for installed routing.
- **ACT:** F2DZ12A697A-tagged or explicitly cross-referenced specimen/drawing. Probe reach from an explicit threaded gauge datum, threaded length/installed depth and connector envelope/key/terminal geometry. MTE5041's3/8-18NPTF and25mm hex do not complete these dimensions.
- **Casting/attachments:** targeted sections and anchor/seat surveys for the intended next component, not necessarily a complete truck scan. Examples: intake-support lower anchor, EGR neck takeoff or a specific core-plug seat. A part identity and common datum are required before converting photographs into CAD.

No additional unqualified standalone fastener, seal, dowel, bearing or sensor completion is supported by the present handoffs. Common nominal sizes alone omit required profile, class, retention or mating data. This conclusion is limited to the reviewed evidence, not a claim that future manufacturer drawings cannot unblock work.
