Some child safety seats use a tether strap that passes over the back of the vehicle seat and clips to an
anchoring point behind it. Ford recommends placing tethered child seats in a rear seating position with the
tether attached to the tether anchoring point. The right front seating position should be used only when it
is the only seating position available.

For an air-bag-equipped truck this guidance dovetails with the SRS: the driver bag deploys with force in the
`interior`, so keeping children in the rear and reserving the right front for last resort reduces exposure to
front-impact restraint forces. The tether anchor and child-seat locking clip provisions give the rear
position a secure mounting path.

> "Ford recommends the placement of tethered safety seats in a rear seating position with the tether strap
> attached to the tether anchoring point. The right front seating position may be used if it is the only
> seating position available."
