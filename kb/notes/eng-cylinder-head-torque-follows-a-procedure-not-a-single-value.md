Unlike the other fasteners in the 4.9L torque table, the cylinder head bolts are not given a
single torque figure. The table entry for "Cylinder Head" points the reader to the Cylinder
Head section for the tightening procedure — meaning the head requires a staged, sequenced
torque-up rather than a one-shot value.

This matters on the `engine` inventory system: anyone reassembling the head must follow the
multi-step sequence and pattern from the Cylinder Head pages, not just pick a number from the
torque chart. Using a flat value risks an uneven clamp load and a head-gasket leak.

> "Cylinder Head [01] -- Refer to Cylinder Head for tightening procedure."
