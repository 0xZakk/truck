The 1994 F-150's factory radio head unit is held in the instrument panel by internal spring
clips, so it is removed with DIN-style radio removal tools rather than fasteners. Ford's
sequence: disconnect the battery ground cable, remove the bezel, then install Radio Removal
tool No. T87P-19061-A (or equivalent) into the radio faceplate. Depress the tool about one inch
to release the clips and pull the radio straight out by the tool, then disconnect the antenna
lead and the electrical connectors. Installation reverses the procedure.

The practical point is the one-inch push: the clips do not let go until the tools are seated to
that depth, and forcing the radio without the tools risks bending the faceplate or breaking the
clips. Because the procedure starts with a battery disconnect, record the radio presets first —
the FSM's accessory service precautions call this out explicitly. This sits in the truck
inventory `interior` system.
