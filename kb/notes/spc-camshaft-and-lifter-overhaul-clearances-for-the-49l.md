The 4.9L inline-six (inventory id `engine`) is a cam-in-block, pushrod engine, so the
camshaft rides in block-mounted bearings. At overhaul: **camshaft journal diameter
2.0170–2.0180 in**, camshaft bearing inside diameter 2.0190–2.0200 in, giving a **bearing
clearance of 0.0010–0.0030 in**. Camshaft **endplay is 0.0010–0.0070 in** (set by the cam
thrust plate) and runout must not exceed 0.008 in.

The hydraulic lifters (tappets) run in machined bores: **lifter bore 0.8752–0.8767 in**,
**lifter diameter 0.8740–0.8745 in**, for a **lifter-to-bore clearance of 0.0007–0.0027
in**. Worn lifter bores or excessive clearance cause noisy lifters and lost oil pressure to
the top end. These dimensions are the build targets behind the assembled valve lash and the
rocker-shaft torque used at reassembly.
