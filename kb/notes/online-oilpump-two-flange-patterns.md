The replacement catalog separates an elongated four-aperture pattern from a three-aperture pattern. Treat these as independent interface templates, not one generic ring around the current mounting feet.

Combined with the Ford raised-neck and pickup-flange evidence, this enables a better modeling contract: declare two face frames, separate gasket ownership, and aperture correspondence before sizing either flange. Test both candidate assignments against the Ford exploded view; retain ambiguity until correspondence is resolved. Unknown thickness or bolt spacing should remain explicit parameters, not inferred production dimensions.
