The 1994 F-150 front wheel alignment specifications, as optimum-plus-range values:

- Camber: .25 deg, +/- .5 deg
- Caster: minimum 2.0 deg, maximum 6.0 deg
- Total toe: .06 deg, +/- .25 deg
- Camber split (side-to-side difference): .0 deg, +/- .4 deg
- Caster split: -.5 deg, +/- .4 deg

The positive camber and modest toe-in are typical for a Twin I-Beam truck. The "split" figures
limit how different the left and right wheels may be, which is what keeps the truck from pulling.
Use these as the pass/fail targets when aligning the `suspension` system, and remember they are
only valid once ride height and frame level have been verified.
