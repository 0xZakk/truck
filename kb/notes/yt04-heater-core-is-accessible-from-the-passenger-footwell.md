On the OBS F-150 (1980–1996, including the 1994), the heater core lives in a plastic housing
on the passenger side of the firewall and is serviced from inside the cab through the
footwell — the full dash does not have to come out. The procedure in the video is: work the
**two heater hoses** off their tubes at the firewall with pliers, drop the **glove box**
(its hinge is a simple hook), unbolt the blend/control module, then remove the perimeter
screws that hold the heater-core cover. With a wire and a cable hook released, the cover comes
off as one piece and the core **slides straight down and out**.

Reassembly reverses the steps: fit the new core and cover, reinstall the screws and modules,
push the heater hoses back on, then **refill and bleed the cooling system** and confirm warm
air at the vents. This is `hvac` work that also touches the `cooling` system because the heater
core is plumbed into engine coolant.
