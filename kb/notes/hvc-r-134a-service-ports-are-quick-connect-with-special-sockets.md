The A/C service-port valves on the 1994 F-150's R-134a system (inventory id `hvac`) are a
one-piece design: an aluminum top threaded into a steel body on the suction accumulator/drier
or refrigerant line and sealed with an O-ring. The high-side fitting is the larger of the two
valves, and both require special adapters to connect service equipment.

Servicing the valves uses dedicated sockets — high-side A/C fitting socket D94L-19703-A and
low-side D94L-19703-B (or equivalents). These quick-connect-style R-134a fittings are
deliberately different from R-12 ports so R-12 equipment cannot be attached.
