A shared-circle fit to three actual views of RF-E7TE-9K461-A5E consistently places the first upper-port interval shorter than the remaining four. Under three contour thresholds, its ratio to the other intervals' mean is approximately0.86–0.89. This is a conditional photographic estimate; the fits assume circular ports and simplified cameras and do not establish factory dimensions.

Freeing upper station positions improves the independent flange-hole prediction, but head and sensor projection errors still exceed the declared8px budget. Cylinder/head pitch therefore cannot be transferred to the upper row or used to certify an ACT installed axis. The equal-upper-pitch assumption in the existing provisional host merits review; the failed fitted axes must not become CAD transforms. Complete methods, residuals and the rejected extraction are preserved in `docs/components/act-host-registration-20261003-delivery.md`.
