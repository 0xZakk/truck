When rebuilding the 4.9L (300) inline-six (inventory id `engine`), both the main bearing
clearance and the rod bearing clearance are specified at **0.0008–0.0015 in**. The
crankshaft main journals measure **2.3982–2.3990 in** and the connecting-rod journals
**2.1228–2.1236 in**. Crank endplay is controlled by the **thrust bearing clearance of
0.0040–0.0080 in**, and connecting-rod side clearance runs **0.0060–0.0130 in**.

Wear limits matter as much as the nominal fits: maximum journal **taper** is 0.0005 in on
the mains and 0.0006 in on the rods, and crankshaft **runout** must not exceed 0.005 in. A
journal outside taper/runout limits, or a bearing clearance above 0.0015 in, drops oil
pressure and is a candidate for regrinding to an undersize. These clearances are checked
with Plastigage during assembly; the main caps and rod caps are then torqued per the
engine torque table.
