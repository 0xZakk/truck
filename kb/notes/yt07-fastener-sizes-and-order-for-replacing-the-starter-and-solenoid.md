Begin every step by **disconnecting the battery negative terminal**. For the
**fender-mounted solenoid**, the two large cable connections are **9/16" nuts** and the two
mounting fasteners are **8 mm bolts**; remove the electrical nuts first, then the mounting
bolts. The small **trigger "elbow" connector** on top simply pulls off and pushes on — no
fastener. Install in reverse; hand-tightening the small connections is acceptable.

For the **starter motor**, the two mounting bolts are **14 mm** and the two electrical
connections are **one 13 mm nut and one 10 mm nut**. Remove the **electrical connections
first, then the mounting bolts**. On install, reverse it: **bolts in first, then the
electrical connections**. This is part of the `electrical-starting` system on a stock 1994
F-150. The factory manual specifies torque values for the starter mounting bolts and cable
nuts; the video hand-tightens, so prefer the FSM torque figures for the final pass rather
than treating "hand tight" as the spec.
