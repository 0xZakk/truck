Fastener torque values for the F-150's Ford 8.8 rear axle (`rear-axle` system):

- **Differential bearing cap bolt:** 70-85 ft·lb
- **Ring gear attaching bolts:** 70-85 ft·lb
- **Differential pinion shaft lock bolt:** 15-30 ft·lb
- **Oil filler plug:** 15-30 ft·lb
- **Axle vent:** 15-25 ft·lb
- **Brake backing plate nuts:** 20-40 ft·lb
- **RABS (rear anti-lock) sensor hold-down:** 25-30 ft·lb
- **Rear cover screws:** the Drive Axles Mechanical Specifications page lists **25-35 ft·lb**,
  while the Differential Cover page lists **15-20 ft·lb** for the cover bolts — the FSM is
  inconsistent here, so cross-check against the gasket/RTV maker's guidance before final torque.

> "Differential Bearing Cap Bolt 70-85 ft.lb; Ring Gear Attaching Bolts 70-85 ft.lb."
