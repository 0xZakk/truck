The catalog’s style M photograph gives a useful shape comparison but no dimensional drawing. Its application code must stay tied to that edition. It cannot establish OEM head height, shoulder diameter or rod-seat geometry.

For reconstruction, retain the rod/block collision until the bolt and mating seat can be modeled coherently. Do not simply shrink the existing head.
