The factory Alternator No-Load Test confirms the alternator and regulator come up to the
regulated band with nothing loading them. Turn OFF all lamps and electrical components,
connect a tachometer, then start the engine and raise it to approximately **1500 RPM** with
no other electrical load — foot off the brake pedal, doors closed so the dome and courtesy
lamps stay off. The voltmeter reading should increase to approximately **12–14 volts**.

If voltage is to specification, the manual sends you on to the Load Test (the loaded Output
Test). If it is not, the next step is the Over Voltage Test. The "no other load" detail
matters: even a brake-lamp or door-switch draw can pull the reading down and make a healthy
alternator look weak.

> "Start engine and increase engine speed to approximately 1500 RPM. … Voltmeter reading
> should increase to approximately 12-14 volts."

This relates to truck inventory system `electrical-charging`.
