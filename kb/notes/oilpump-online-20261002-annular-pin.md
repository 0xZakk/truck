Figure13 shows part17, listed as378644S pump-body pin, as a short annular piece above the raised mounting face. It is distinct from the long intermediate shaft and retaining ring. This provides a specific shape comparison where earlier evidence left pin form unresolved.

An annular drawing does not establish a measured bore, press fit, insertion depth or pressurized connection. Future geometry should preserve those unknowns until a registered mounting-face source identifies the pin and surrounding passages. Industrial applicability and the actual1994 pump revision remain separate.
