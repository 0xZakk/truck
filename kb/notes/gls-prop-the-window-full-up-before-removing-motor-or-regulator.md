The door glass is carried by the regulator, which in turn is driven by the motor. As soon as
either the motor is disengaged from the regulator gear or the regulator is unfastened, nothing
holds the glass up and it can drop into the door — risking broken glass or a pinched hand. Both
FSM procedures therefore call for supporting the glass in the full-UP position before the final
disengagement step.

For the motor, you push the motor toward the outer sheet metal to disengage it from the
regulator gear, then prop the window UP before removing the motor and drive. For the regulator,
you support the glass full-up first, then drill out the rivets and disengage the regulator arm
from the glass bracket. This is a shared safety step across the `glass` / `body-cab` window
service procedures.
