The actual identified MS93838 photo contains six large openings and nine small ones, including paired openings in two inter-port bridges. The end ears and center opening are offset from the port row. The small-hole count does not establish nine fasteners: the prior service evidence still supports seven retaining fasteners, and the extra two aperture functions require separate identification.

Dimensionless image extraction puts the rightmost port interval at approximately0.894 times the mean of the other four. Pattern comparison with the identified lower casting suggests that image end is the ACT/front end, with the gasket viewed from the opposite face; no printed orientation label confirms that mapping. This independently supports reviewing equal upper-port spacing while leaving exact millimeters, sealing land, thickness and installed registration unknown.
