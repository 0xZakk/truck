To replace the turn-signal/hazard/dimmer multi-function switch on the 1994 F-150: on a tilt
column, first unscrew and remove the tilt lever. Remove the steering column shroud, then
remove the switch-to-column tube casting attaching screws and disengage the switch from the
column. Disconnect the switch's electrical connectors and remove the assembly. Installation
is the reverse, with the attaching screws torqued to 18-27 inch-lbs.

The FSM flags one hazard during removal: do not damage the PRNDL cable, which runs at the
column on column-shift trucks. Because all three lighting functions share this one switch,
this single procedure covers turn-signal, hazard, and high/low-beam switch replacement. This
is part of the truck's `electrical-body` system.

> "torque switch attaching screws to 18-27 inch lbs."
