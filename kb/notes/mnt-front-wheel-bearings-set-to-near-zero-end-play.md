The front tapered wheel bearings are adjustable, not press-fit, so they get a specific seating
procedure. Tighten the adjusting nut to 23-34 Nm (17-25 ft lb) while rotating the hub to seat the
rollers, then back the nut off about one half turn and retighten it to just 2.03-2.26 Nm
(18-20 in lb). The targets are 0.006-0.127 mm (0.00025-0.005 in) end play and 1.13-2.82 Nm
(10-25 in lb) of hub/rotor rotating torque.

This seat-then-back-off-then-snug sequence is what gives a tapered bearing its slight, controlled
end play — too tight and the bearing overheats and fails, too loose and you get wobble and uneven
rotor contact on the `brakes`. Checking rotating torque confirms the preload is right after the
final light torque is applied.
