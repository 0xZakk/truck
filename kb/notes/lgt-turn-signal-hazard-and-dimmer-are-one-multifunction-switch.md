On the 1994 F-150 the turn signal switch, the hazard warning switch, and the headlamp
high/low beam dimmer are not three separate parts — they are functions of a single
steering-column multi-function switch. The FSM lists each under its own component heading,
but gives them the identical removal procedure, the same attaching-screw torque, and a
single shared "Multi-Function Switch" terminal-and-schematic set for testing.

The practical upshot is that a fault in turn signals, hazards, or beam selection points at
the same physical switch (or its connectors), and replacing it restores all three functions
at once. The flashers themselves are separate plug-in units, not part of this switch. This
is part of the truck's `electrical-body` system.
