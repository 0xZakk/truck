MTE5041 photographs support an open protective guard and a visible blue sensing-element envelope. A closed generic metal probe would hide this observed construction. The images do not expose the internal thermistor composition, hidden leads or potting.

A useful local model can represent the visible encapsulation separately without inventing a semiconductor chip. It must label absent wiring and internal support as unresolved, rather than implying a verified electrical circuit. Photo-scaled outer dimensions also do not establish installed thread depth or a matching manifold port.
