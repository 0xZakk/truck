After repairing an external leak on the 1994 F-150 A/C system (inventory id `hvac`), the FSM
restores oil by measuring what drained from the compressor rather than guessing. First run the
A/C at fast idle, max cooling, blower high for 10 minutes to return oil to the compressor; then
discharge, remove the compressor, and drain its oil into a container.

The amount of new PAG oil added depends on what came out: if less than 3 oz drained, add 6 oz;
if 3–6 oz drained, add that same amount back; if more than 6 oz drained, add only 6 oz. This
keeps the total system oil near its 7.0 oz capacity without overfilling, which would itself
hurt cooling.
