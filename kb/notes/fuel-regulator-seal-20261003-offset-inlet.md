DS1125's applicable replacement photographs and the exact-year service underside illustration place one visible O-ring around the larger offset stem, beside a smaller bare tube and a two-port gasket. Ford's separate typical cutaway identifies an offset supply inlet and central return, but its two drawn groove positions do not establish two installed seals. Together these sources challenge the current candidate's tiny seal on its central return stem.

Correct the port and gasket architecture before adjusting a ring merely to close a modeled leak. Free seal size, gland capacity, receiving bore, installed deformation and contact on both surfaces need a coordinated numerical contract. Those dimensions remain unknown; neither a plausible picture nor a valid CAD solid proves a pressure-tight regulator.
