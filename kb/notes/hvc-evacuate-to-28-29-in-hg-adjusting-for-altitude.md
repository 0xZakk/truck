Evacuating the 1994 F-150 A/C system (inventory id `hvac`) pulls out air and moisture before
recharging. The vacuum pump should draw the system down to 28–29.5 in Hg at sea level; that
target drops by 1 in Hg for every 1000 ft of altitude (at 5000 ft only 23–24.5 in Hg is
achievable). The system must be completely discharged first — letting pressurized refrigerant
into the pump damages it.

After pumping down, run the pump 15–30 minutes (longer if the system was open a while), then
verify the system holds vacuum: the low-side gauge must not rise faster than 1 in Hg every 4–5
minutes. If it can't hold vacuum, find and fix the leak before charging.
