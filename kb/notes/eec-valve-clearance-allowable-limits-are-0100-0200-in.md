The 4.9L uses hydraulic lifters, so "valve clearance" here is the collapsed-tappet clearance
between the rocker arm and the valve stem tip, measured with the lifter bled down. The allowable
limits are 0.100-0.200 in, with a desirable range of 0.125-0.175 in. Because the rocker bolts are
torqued fixed (non-adjustable), clearance is corrected by changing pushrod length: install a
shorter pushrod if clearance is too small, a longer one if too large.

The procedure marks the damper into three 120-degree sections and works through the cylinders in
the firing-order sequence 1-5-3-6-2-4, turning the crank one-third turn at a time. Out-of-range
clearance points to lifter or valvetrain wear and can mimic driveability or noise complaints, so
it belongs alongside compression as an `engine` mechanical check.
