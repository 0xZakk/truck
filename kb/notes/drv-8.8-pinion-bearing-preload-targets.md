The drive-pinion bearing preload on the F-150's Ford 8.8 axle is measured as the rotational
torque needed to turn the pinion, and the target depends on whether the bearings are new:

- **New bearings: 16-29 in·lb** rotational torque.
- **Used bearings: 8-14 in·lb** rotational torque.

The lower used-bearing window reflects the slight polishing/wear of already-run bearings — a used
bearing at the new-bearing preload would actually be over-loaded. The pinion nut is torqued (up to
the 217 ft·lb reference) only as far as needed to land inside the correct window. This spec is part
of servicing the `rear-axle` inventory system.

> "New Bearings: 16-29 Inch Pounds. Used Bearings: 8-14 Inch Pounds. [1] Rotational torque."
