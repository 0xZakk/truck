The 4.9L fuel filter is a high-pressure canister with a paper element that traps dirt, water, and
contaminants before they reach the very small orifices in the injector tips. Ford stresses that
clean fuel is extremely important in a fuel-injection `engine`, because those orifices clog far
more easily than a carburetor's passages. The filter bracket nuts torque to 8-10 Nm (6-7 lb in).

This frames the filter as the first suspect in fuel-delivery complaints: a partially clogged filter
restricts flow and shows up as low or sagging fuel pressure under demand. Pairing a filter change
with the fuel-pressure test is the efficient way to separate a tired filter from a failing pump or
regulator.
