The front door window regulator is riveted, not bolted, to the door inner panel from the
factory, so removing it is a drilling job. With the trim panel and access cover off and the
glass supported in the full UP position, you drive the center pin out of each attaching rivet
with a drift punch, then drill the head off each rivet with a 6.35 mm (1/4 in) bit, taking care
not to enlarge or damage the sheet-metal holes. Once the rivets are gone the regulator arm
disengages from the glass bracket channel and the regulator lifts out.

Installation re-creates the riveted joint: position the regulator, insert the arm into the
glass bracket channel, and set new rivets with a Rotunda Hydraulic Rivet Gun (107-00600 or
equivalent). If rivets are not available, the FSM permits substituting a 1/4-20 x 1/2 in
screw-and-washer assembly with a matching 1/4-20 nut and washer (or equivalent metric
hardware). Check window operation before reinstalling the door trim. This is a `body-cab` door
procedure that pairs with the power-window motor replacement.
