Carter specifies W9046M hub height as98.43mm and3.88in (98.552mm), measured from engine mounting face to pulley mating face. Its catalog identifies this replacement for1993–1996 F1504.9L. The small conversion inconsistency and replacement status must remain explicit; neither value is an owner-pump measurement.

The modeled pump's mounting and pulley faces are147mm apart; its earlier143mm reference ended at the hub center. The new primary evidence therefore challenges the complete axial stack, not merely the heater tube. A coordinated hypothesis should hold the engine mounting joint fixed while reconsidering housing length, bearing/seal/shaft placement, hub face, pulley dish and fan attachment. Hub diameter, internal dimensions and heater radial route remain estimates.
