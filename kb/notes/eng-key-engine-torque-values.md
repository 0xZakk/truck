The FSM's torque table gives the load-bearing fastener values for the 4.9L. The rotating
assembly: main bearing caps 60-70 ft-lb, connecting rod caps 40-45 ft-lb, flywheel-to-
crankshaft 75-85 ft-lb, and the crankshaft damper/pulley 130-150 ft-lb. Common service
fasteners: intake manifold 22-32 ft-lb, exhaust manifold 22-32 ft-lb, oil pan 15-18 ft-lb,
oil drain plug 15-25 ft-lb, spark plugs 10-15 ft-lb, rocker cover 6-9 ft-lb, rocker shaft
support 17-23 ft-lb, water pump bolts 12-18 ft-lb, oil pump retaining bolts 10-12 ft-lb,
and engine-to-transmission bolts 40-50 ft-lb.

These values support nearly any teardown or reassembly on the `engine` inventory system.
Note the units: a few items (fan shroud bolts, valve pushrod cover) are specified in
inch-pounds, not foot-pounds.

> "Main Bearing Cap 60-70 ft lb ... Connecting Rod Cap 40-45 ft lb ... Flywheel To Crankshaft
> 75-85 ft lb ... Damper Or Pulley 130-150 ft lb."
