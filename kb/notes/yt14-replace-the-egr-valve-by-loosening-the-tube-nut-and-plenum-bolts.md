On the OBS F-150 (1987–1996), the EGR valve is held by two distinct connections: a large
EGR tube nut at the bottom that takes a **1-1/16 inch** wrench, and two **1/2-inch**
fasteners that secure the valve and its bracket to the intake plenum. The host of this
video soaks the tube nut and all the bolts in penetrating fluid before touching them, then
works the tube nut loose until it spins by hand. An open-end wrench may be needed on the
back nut, though sufficient penetrant let him skip it. This is an `engine`/intake-side
emissions job.

The order matters: remove the electrical connector and the vacuum line at the back before
freeing the second plenum nut, then ease the valve off — it tends to stick to its old
gasket and needs a little persuasion. The valve sits at the front of the engine on this
truck, which makes it an easy reach compared to vehicles where the EGR lives at the back.
