The finish panel (the trim bezel around the cluster and switches) is the gateway to the gauges,
headlamp switch, and radio on the 1994 F-150, and it comes off without removing the whole dash.
First set the tilt column full down (and, with column shift, set the parking brake and drop the
shift lever to its lowest position) for clearance. Unsnap the right and left mouldings by
prying gently at the notches along their bottom edge, then pull the headlamp switch knob and
shaft — insert a hooked tool into the knob slot to depress the release spring, then pull the
shaft off.

With the mouldings off, two screws are exposed (one under each). Removing those frees the panel
to be pulled rearward, which releases five retaining clips: do the two at the steering column
(one each side) first, then the three across the top. The panel still has wiring connected to
it, so disconnect those connectors before lifting it away. Reassembly is the reverse. This is a
`body-cab` interior-trim procedure and a prerequisite for cluster/switch service.
