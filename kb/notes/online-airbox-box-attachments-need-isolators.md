The complete salvage assembly shows two opposing attachment stacks between the lower box and its formed support. Visible rubber-like pieces and metal cups should not be collapsed into a single rigid mounting shelf.

Their dimensions, hidden sleeves and exact body attachment pattern remain unknown. The listing identifies a comparison specimen; it does not establish that every component matches the owner’s truck.
