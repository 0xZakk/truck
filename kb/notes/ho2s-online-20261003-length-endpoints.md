Bosch's US 15718 data specifies18.9 in from sealing surface to connector end. The catalog separately prints18.9 in/481 mm; the decimal-inch conversion is480.06 mm, so neither rounding nor tolerance should be invented. The indexed regional 0258005718 page instead describes511 mm as overall length.

Different endpoint names can explain differing values, but a common drawing/version was not obtained. Subtracting these numbers does not establish30 mm probe reach or free wire length. The lead is flexible, and its pictured bundle is not its installed route. Preserve the catalog values with their endpoints and resolve the route with pipe, harness and retention evidence.
