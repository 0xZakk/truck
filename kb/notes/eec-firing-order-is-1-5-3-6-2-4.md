The firing order for the 1994 F-150's 4.9L (300 cu in) inline-six is **1-5-3-6-2-4**. This same
sequence governs spark-plug-wire routing on the distributor cap and the order in which valves are
adjusted during the collapsed-tappet valve-clearance procedure.

Two related plug specs from the same tune-up data: spark plugs are type BSF-44C, gapped
0.042-0.046 in, and torqued to 10-15 ft lb (14-20 Nm). Getting the firing order right is
essential `ignition`-system knowledge — a single swapped pair of plug wires produces a violent
misfire that no sensor or PCM adjustment will fix.
