The bench seat tracks bolt straight through the cab floor pan, so each attaching screw and stud
penetrates a sealed surface that separates the cab from underneath the truck. To keep water out
of those penetrations, the FSM's installation step is to apply Caulking Cord D6AZ-19560-A
(ESB-M4G32-A) or equivalent around the seat-track attaching screw and stud locations on the
front floor pan before securing the seat.

This is easy to skip when simply swapping a seat, but leaving it out invites floor-pan rust and
a wet cab — a real concern on a 30-year-old truck. The same caulking step appears in both the
standard bench and split bench procedures. It is a `body-cab` / `frame-chassis` interface
detail (the seat anchors into structural floor-pan sheet metal).
