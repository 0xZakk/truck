The 4.9L's normal engine oil pressure is 40-60 PSI measured with the engine warm and running
at 2000 rpm. The FSM lists this both in the General Engine Specifications and in the
Engine Lubrication specifications, making it the reference value for diagnosing a low- or
high-oil-pressure complaint or a flaky oil-pressure gauge on the `engine` inventory system.

Because the figure is tied to 2000 rpm and a hot engine, a much lower reading at warm idle is
not necessarily a fault; the spec only describes the 2000-rpm operating point. A genuinely low
reading there points toward the pump, bearing clearances, or a worn relief valve.

> "Engine Oil Pressure (Hot) 40-60 at 2000 Pound Per Square Inch At RPM (psi @ rpm)."
