Two debris/fastener details make or break this EGR replacement. First, once the valve is
off, plug the open EGR tube and the intake ports with a glove and a paper towel before
wire-brushing the old gasket. Anything that falls into that opening goes straight into the
`engine`, so it has to be sealed while you scrape and then clean the mating surface with
brake cleaner. The new gasket must go on in its original orientation because the two faces
differ — note which way the old one came off.

Second, the solenoid/plastic portion of the valve is fastened with three **1/4-inch** bolts
that are brittle (all three sheared off on the host's used valve; new valves typically ship
with fresh bolts). Because that body is plastic, the new bolts get tightened only until
barely hand-tight — just enough to seal without cracking the housing. The same restraint
applies to the plenum fasteners: snug them, don't strip or over-torque them. The video gives
no factory torque figure, so treat "barely hand-tight" as the host's feel-based guidance
rather than a spec.
