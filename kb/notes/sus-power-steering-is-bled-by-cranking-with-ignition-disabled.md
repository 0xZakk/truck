Air trapped in the power steering system causes foaming, moaning, and erratic assist. The FSM
first has you fill the pump, run the engine to operating temperature, and turn the wheel left
and right several times (without hitting the stops); if bubbles remain, it bleeds with the
engine cranking but NOT running.

The bleed sequence: disable the ignition (disconnect the coil wire or ICM) and disconnect the
fuel shutoff solenoid so the engine cannot start; crank for 30 seconds and top up fluid; crank
another 30 seconds while cycling the wheel lock to lock — but never hold the wheel against its
stops; then recheck and add fluid. Cranking without firing lets the pump circulate and purge
air at low speed without whipping it back into foam. Holding the wheel on the stops would dead-
head the pump at relief pressure. This restores clean assist in the `steering` system.
