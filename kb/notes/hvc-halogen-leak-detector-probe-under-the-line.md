To find an R-134a leak on the 1994 F-150 (inventory id `hvac`) with an electronic halogen leak
detector, move the probe slowly — about one inch per second — along areas of suspected leakage;
the detector signals refrigerant with an audible tone or flashing light. Because R-134a is
heavier than air, position the probe under the area being tested so escaping refrigerant
settles onto it.

Check the obvious leak paths too: the gauge manifold, its hoses, and the service ports
themselves. This is the same procedure used to leak-test compressor seals and manifold O-rings
after a repair.
