Melling lists VS-2215 for intake and VS-2216 for exhaust on the 1992–1996 Ford Truck 300. In millimeters, intake/exhaust free heights are 49.784/45.212, outside diameters 35.2806/35.1282, wire diameters 4.699/4.6228, and solid heights 28.956/25.654. The table gives 6.20/5.87 turns, both left-hand. Reusing one spring geometry for both positions loses these source-supported differences.

The load-reference closed heights are 41.656/37.338 mm and open heights 31.496/27.178 mm. These are catalog test heights, not proof of the actual engine's installed stack or cam lift. Compare the separate [[notes/spc-valvetrain-overhaul-specs-cover-springs-guides-and-seats|factory service specifications]] before interpreting them as repair limits.

Both rows list inside diameter 25.654 mm, but outside diameter minus twice wire diameter gives 25.8826 mm. Preserve this 0.2286 mm discrepancy. The candidate chooses outside diameter plus wire diameter; it does not pretend all three dimensions agree. End grinding, transition pitch and installed spring identity remain unverified, so a successful clearance or mesh check cannot establish factory-exact geometry.
