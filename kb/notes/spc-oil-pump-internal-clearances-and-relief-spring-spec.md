When the 4.9L oil pump (inventory id `engine`) is inspected during an overhaul, the
factory wear limits are: **rotor backlash 0.012 in**, **rotor-to-body clearance
0.0010–0.0130 in**, **rotor end clearance 0.0040 in**, driveshaft-to-pump-body clearance
0.0015–0.0030 in, and relief-valve-to-body clearance 0.0015–0.0030 in. The **relief spring
pressure is 20.6–22.6 lb at 2.4900 in**.

These clearances determine whether the pump can build pressure. A pump with excessive rotor
end or body clearance loses volume internally and cannot reach the **40–60 psi hot at 2000
rpm** normal running pressure; a weak or stuck relief valve/spring causes either low
pressure (stuck open) or over-pressure (stuck closed). Worn pump clearances are a prime
suspect when oil pressure is low but the bearings measure within limits.
