When reinstalling the dashboard/instrument panel on the 1994 F-150, the FSM gives these
fastener torques:

- Instrument Panel-to-Dash screws: 2.0-2.5 Nm (17-22 in-lbs)
- Selector Indicator-to-Instrument Cluster: 1.4 Nm (12 in-lbs)
- Lower Instrument Panel screw: 4-6 Nm (36-53 in-lbs)
- Instrument Panel-to-Right Side Cowl: 2.0-2.4 Nm (18-21 in-lbs)

These are all small inch-pound values — overtightening cracks plastic and strips screw
bosses, so a low-range inch-pound torque tool is appropriate. The values come into play any
time the cluster or panel is pulled to service the gauges, warning lamps, or chime module in
the `interior` / `electrical-body` systems.
