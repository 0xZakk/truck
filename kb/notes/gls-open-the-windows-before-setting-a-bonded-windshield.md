A cab is nearly sealed once the doors are shut, so closing a door rapidly raises interior air
pressure. With a freshly set windshield whose urethane has not yet cured, that pressure spike
can push the glass back out of the opening. The FSM's safeguard is simple: open the windows
before installing the glass, so the cab can vent and any door that gets closed during the work
does not pop the windshield loose.

This pairs with the broader rule that the truck must not be driven until the urethane cures.
Both steps protect the uncured bond that gives the `glass` / `body-cab` windshield its structural
retention.
