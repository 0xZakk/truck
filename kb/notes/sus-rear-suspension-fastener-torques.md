Rear suspension torque specifications for the 1994 F-150:

- Leaf spring to axle U-bolt nut: 75-115 ft lb
- Leaf spring to front bracket nut & bolt: 75-115 ft lb
- Leaf spring to rear shackle: 75-115 ft lb
- Rear shackle to frame: 75-115 ft lb
- Rear shock absorber, upper mount: 40-60 ft lb; lower mount: 52-74 ft lb
- Jounce bumper to frame nut: 19-30 ft lb
- Stabilizer bar to axle nut: 30-42 ft lb
- Stabilizer link to bar: 15-25 ft lb; stabilizer link to frame: 40-60 ft lb

The big 75-115 ft lb figure recurs across all the spring-mounting joints (U-bolts, front
bracket, and both shackle ends), reflecting how much these fasteners carry on a load-hauling
truck. Use these whenever reassembling the rear of the `suspension` system.
