The driver air bag lives in the steering wheel hub, so any column, wheel, or gear-lash work
starts by disarming it. The FSM disarm sequence: record radio presets, disconnect the battery
NEGATIVE cable then the positive cable, and wait at least one minute for the air-bag back-up
power supply to bleed off its stored energy before touching the module. Then remove the module
retaining nuts, unplug the driver-side module connector, and connect the Rotunda air-bag
simulator (105-00010 or equivalent) to the harness at the top of the wheel.

Arming reverses it: disconnect battery negative then positive, wait one minute, remove the
simulator, reconnect the module connector, install the module, then reconnect the battery.
Because the battery was disconnected, the PCM loses its adaptive strategy and the truck may
drive abnormally until it relearns — often 10 or more miles. Skipping the one-minute wait risks
firing the air bag while your hands are on it, so it is the non-negotiable step in the
`steering` system's safety work.
