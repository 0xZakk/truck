The FSM Diagnosis Guide separates bearing noise from gear noise and lists the specific
bearings to suspect: the pinion bearing, the wheel bearing, and the differential thrust
washer/bearing. It also groups two limited-slip symptoms here — limited-slip operation
noise and a click on engagement — because those originate at the differential rather than
the ring-and-pinion mesh.

The diagnostic value is that bearing noise typically holds a steadier pitch tied to a
single rotating member, unlike gear howl which varies with the gear mesh. On the 1994
F-150 (`engine`), a pinion bearing tends to whine and change with vehicle speed, a wheel
bearing growls and may change with cornering load, and a clicking on engagement or
chatter on turns points to the limited-slip clutch pack and its friction modifier rather
than a failed bearing.

> "Bearing Noise — Pinion Bearing, Wheel Bearing, Differential Thrust Washer/Bearing,
> Limited-Slip Operation, Click on Engagement."
