The truck uses two different kinds of glass. The windshield is **annealed** (laminated safety
glass that holds together on impact), while the side and rear windows are **tempered** — heat-
treated so internal compression and tension make them far stronger and cause them to shatter
into small blunt pieces rather than shards.

This distinction explains a diagnostic point in the TSBs: tempered glass can show iridescence or
"mottling" because the tempering forces polarize light, whereas annealed windshields do not
exhibit that effect. Knowing which `glass` / `body-cab` panel is tempered versus annealed tells
you whether an optical "defect" is expected behavior or worth investigating.
