The FSM compression test follows a fixed sequence on the `engine`: warm the engine to normal
operating temperature, remove all spark plugs, and set the throttle plates wide open so the
cylinders can draw a full charge. Install a compression gauge in the No. 1 spark plug hole and
an auxiliary starter switch in the starting circuit. With the key off, crank the engine at least
five compression strokes and record the highest reading, then repeat on each remaining cylinder.

Each detail matters: warming the engine seats parts to operating clearance, wide-open throttle
removes the intake restriction so a low reading reflects the cylinder rather than a closed
throttle, and five strokes ensures the gauge stabilizes at peak. Recording the highest reading
per cylinder is what feeds the within-25% pass/fail comparison.
