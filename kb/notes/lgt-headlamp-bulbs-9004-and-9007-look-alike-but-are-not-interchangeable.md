When buying a replacement halogen bulb for the 1994 F-150 headlamp, the FSM carries an
explicit caution that bulbs No. 9004 and No. 9007 look similar but are not interchangeable.
They share a similar plastic base appearance but differ in filament/pin arrangement, so
fitting the wrong one will not seat or align correctly in the socket.

During installation the flat side of the plastic base faces upward and the grooves in the
forward part of the base must align with locating tabs inside the socket before the bulb is
pushed home — another reason the correct part number matters. This is part of the truck's
`electrical-body` system.
