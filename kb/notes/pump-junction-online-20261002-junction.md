Carter's multiple views show a cast arm joining the flange-side chamber and transitioning to a round beaded inlet. The machined sealing ring and raised fastener pads remain identifiable beside that junction. GMB's identified views corroborate this external arrangement, while replacement contour details differ.

The photographs do not reveal the internal passage cross-section or prove a particular axial offset. They support an integrated curved junction, not the earlier union of a full round tube through a protected casting slab. Future dimensions should remain declared estimates constrained by functional sealing, bolt and impeller interfaces, with the existing carrier treated as another estimated neighbor rather than an authority for pump shape.
