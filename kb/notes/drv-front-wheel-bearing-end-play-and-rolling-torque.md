After the F-150 front wheel bearing is adjusted, two measurable values confirm it is right:

- **Bearing end play: 0.006-0.127 mm (0.00025-0.005 in)** — a small amount of axial free play, not
  preload.
- **Torque required to rotate the hub: 1.13-2.82 N·m (10-25 in·lb).**

These give an objective check beyond "feel": a dial indicator on the hub face confirms the end
play, and a torque wrench/spring scale on a wheel stud confirms rolling torque. Zero end play
(preload) means the bearing is too tight and will overheat; excessive end play means it is too
loose and will wobble and wear the rotor. This spec supports the `rear-axle` inventory system.

> "End play should be 0.006-0.127mm (.00025-.005 in). Torque required to rotate the hub should be
> 1.13-2.82 Nm (10-25 in lb)."
