The firing order of the Ford 300 inline-six is **1-5-3-6-2-4**, the standard order for this
inline-six layout, chosen so combustion events are spaced to keep the engine smooth and
balanced. Cylinders are numbered 1–6 from the front of the engine.

This is reference data you need whenever ignition work touches cylinder order — setting plug
wires on the distributor cap, or checking for a cross-fire after a tune-up. Getting it wrong
produces a no-start or a violently rough run, so it's worth confirming against the
distributor rotation direction when wiring the cap.

This relates to truck inventory system `ignition`.
