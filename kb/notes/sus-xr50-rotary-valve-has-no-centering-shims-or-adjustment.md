A point of confusion with older power steering gears is "valve centering." On the XR-50 there
is nothing to center: it uses a one-piece rotary valve and sleeve assembly, so no centering
shims are fitted and no valve-centering adjustment is possible. The one-piece design also
lets the fluid passages run internally between valve and cylinder, eliminating all external
lines except the pump pressure and return hoses.

The practical takeaway for the `steering` system: if the truck pulls or has uneven assist,
do not go looking for a centering adjustment that does not exist. Wandering assist points
instead to internal leakage in the valve/cylinder (found with the pressure-analyzer test) or
to the gear needing replacement — not to a shim stack. The only thing a tech can actually
adjust in this gear is the sector-to-rack overcenter lash.
