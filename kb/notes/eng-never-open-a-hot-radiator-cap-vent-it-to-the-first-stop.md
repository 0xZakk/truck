The FSM warns never to remove the radiator cap while the engine is running, because the
pressurized cooling system can blow out scalding coolant or steam and cause injury or system
damage. On a hot system, the safe sequence is: wait for the engine to cool if possible, wrap a
thick cloth around the cap, turn it slowly to the first stop to release system pressure, then —
still using the cloth — press the cap down and turn it the rest of the way to remove it.

This is a core safety procedure for any `cooling` inventory system work. The first-stop detent
exists precisely so trapped pressure (held at 13 psi by the cap) can vent before the seal fully
breaks.

> "Use extreme care when removing the radiator cap from a hot system to avoid a blowout of
> scalding hot coolant or steam. ... turn it slowly to the first stop to release system
> pressure."
