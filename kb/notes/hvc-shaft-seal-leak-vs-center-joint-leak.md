When the 1994 F-150 compressor (inventory id `hvac`) is leak-tested off the vehicle, the FSM
distinguishes two leak locations with very different repairs. A leak at the compressor shaft
seal is fixed by installing a new shaft seal — a serviceable part. A leak at the center joint
(center seal) of the compressor body, however, cannot be resealed: the FSM calls for replacing
the entire compressor assembly.

The off-vehicle test connects an R-134a manifold gauge set and refrigerant container to a
pressure test adapter on the rear head; the shaft is hand-rotated ten revolutions first to
spread oil, then a halogen leak detector is run over the shaft seal and center seal. Knowing
which seal leaks tells you whether to order a seal kit or a whole compressor.
