A hazy fog or film that builds up on the inside of the windshield and interior glass is, per
TSB 96-22-1, caused by chemicals outgassing from the interior trim during manufacture — not a
leak, not a coolant problem, and not a health hazard (Ford tested the material). There is no
known field fix to prevent the fogging from recurring.

The practical action is to clean it off: Ford Glass Cleaner (E4AZ-19C507-AA) was found best for
removing the film. So for a `glass` / `body-cab` interior-fog complaint, the answer is periodic
cleaning rather than parts replacement, and the technician can reassure the customer it is
cosmetic and safe.
