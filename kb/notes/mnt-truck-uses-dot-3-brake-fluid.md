The `brakes` system uses DOT 3 hydraulic brake fluid — Ford High Performance DOT 3 (C6AZ-19542-AA)
or a DOT 3 equivalent meeting Ford specification ESA-M6C25-A. The defining quality metric is boiling
point: if fluid boils in the system it forms vapor that behaves like air in the lines, reducing the
ability to build hydraulic pressure (a spongy or fading pedal), and it recovers once the fluid cools.

Brake fluid is hygroscopic — it absorbs moisture from the air, which lowers its boiling point over
time — so it must be stored sealed in its original container and the master-cylinder reservoir kept
properly capped. Color changes alone (from heat, aging, or mixed brands) are normal and not a
reliable contamination indicator.
