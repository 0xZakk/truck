The 1994 F-150 uses a replaceable halogen headlamp bulb that installs from the rear of the
composite headlamp body. The bulb's glass envelope contains gas under pressure, so the FSM
warns it can shatter if the glass is scratched or the bulb is dropped. Always grasp the
bulb by its plastic base and avoid touching the glass; skin oils left on the envelope also
shorten bulb life. Energize the bulb only while it is installed in the headlamp.

To service it, confirm the headlamp switch is OFF, unplug the connector by snapping it
rearward, rotate the bulb retainer about one-eighth turn counterclockwise (viewed from the
rear), and pull the bulb straight back out of the socket without rotating it. Do not pull a
burned-out bulb until the replacement is ready, because an open socket lets dust, moisture,
and smoke into the headlamp body and degrades its output. A properly aimed headlamp normally
does not need re-aiming after a bulb change. This is part of the truck's `electrical-body`
system.
