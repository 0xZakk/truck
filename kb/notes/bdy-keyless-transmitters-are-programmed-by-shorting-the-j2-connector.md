To teach the 1994 F-150's keyless entry module a transmitter, you put it into program mode with
a jumper. Turn the ignition to ON (or ON/ACC) and short the two terminals of program connector
J2 — a two-pin connector below the driver's side of the instrument panel, at the base of the
steering column. The doors lock then unlock to confirm program mode is active.

With the short in place, press any button on each transmitter, one at a time; the doors lock
and unlock to acknowledge each one. If they do not respond, press again (up to three tries per
transmitter) before sending it to PINPOINT TEST D. The module holds a maximum of four
transmitters. Turn the ignition off (doors cycle again) and remove the short to exit. The key
gotcha: all of the customer's transmitters must be present and re-programmed together at the
same session — adding one is really a re-teach of the whole set — so a lost or added fob means
gathering every fob the truck should answer to. This is a `body-cab` locks procedure.
