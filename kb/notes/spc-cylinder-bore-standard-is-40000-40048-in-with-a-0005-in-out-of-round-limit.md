The standard cylinder bore diameter for the 4.9L engine (inventory id `engine`) is
**4.0000–4.0048 in**, matching the 4.00 in bore of the general engine spec. During an
overhaul the block's bores are measured against three service limits: **out-of-round
0.005 in**, **taper 0.01 in**, and **deck warpage 0.006 in**. A bore exceeding the
out-of-round or taper limit must be honed or bored oversize; a deck out of flat beyond
0.006 in must be resurfaced.

The companion main-bearing bore (in the block) is 2.5902–2.5910 in standard. Holding the
bore round and straight is what lets the piston rings seal — a worn or tapered bore is a
classic cause of high oil consumption and the cylinder-to-cylinder spread that a
compression test reveals.
