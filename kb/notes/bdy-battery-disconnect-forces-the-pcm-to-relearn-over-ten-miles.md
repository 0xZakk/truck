Almost every body and interior procedure in this section starts with "disconnect the battery
ground cable," and the FSM repeatedly attaches the same warning: once the battery has been
disconnected and reconnected, the Powertrain Control Module (PCM / EEC processor) has lost its
learned adaptive strategy and the truck may show abnormal drive symptoms until it relearns,
often needing 10 or more miles of driving.

This is a useful troubleshooting anchor: if a truck idles oddly, hesitates, or shifts strangely
right after door-panel, dash, ash-tray, or seat work, the cause is frequently the battery
disconnect those jobs required — not a new fault you introduced. The fix is simply to drive it
and let the PCM re-adapt before chasing a phantom problem. Recording the radio presets before
disconnecting saves a second annoyance. This precaution spans the `body-cab` work but its effect
lands in the powertrain.
