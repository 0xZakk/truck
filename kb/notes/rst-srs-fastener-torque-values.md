Torque values for the supplemental air bag restraint system fasteners, gathered from the air bag, clockspring,
and sensor specification pages:

- Air bag module-to-steering wheel nuts: 4-6 Nm (36-53 in lbs)
- Air bag sliding contact (clockspring) screws: 2-3 Nm (18-28 in lbs)
- Center sensor-to-radiator support screws: 10.2-13.8 Nm (89-124 in lbs)
- Front sensor-to-right frame screws: 10.2-13.8 Nm (89-124 in lbs)
- Safing (front) sensor-to-right cowl side panel screws: 7.6-10.4 Nm (62-89 in lbs)

These are low torques — the module nuts and sliding contact screws in particular are in the inch-pound range,
so a calibrated in-lb wrench is appropriate. The sensor fasteners matter for more than clamp load: correct,
undamaged mounting preserves the sensor orientation the system depends on across the `body-cab` structure and
`interior` steering wheel.
