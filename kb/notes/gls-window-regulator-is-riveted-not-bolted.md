The front door window regulator is attached to the door inner panel with rivets rather than
threaded fasteners, so it cannot simply be unbolted. To remove it, drive the center pin out of
each attaching rivet with a drift punch, then drill the head off each rivet with a 6.35 mm
(1/4-inch) bit, being careful not to enlarge or damage the sheet-metal holes. The regulator arm
then disengages from the glass bracket channel and the regulator lifts out.

On reinstall the factory method is a Rotunda Hydraulic Rivet Gun (107-00600) to re-rivet the
regulator, but the FSM permits substituting threaded hardware when rivets are unavailable: a
1/4-20 x 1/2-inch screw and washer with a matching 1/4-20 nut and washer (or equivalent metric
retainers). This is the `glass` / `body-cab` regulator R&R, and the glass must be propped full-up
throughout because the regulator is what holds it.
