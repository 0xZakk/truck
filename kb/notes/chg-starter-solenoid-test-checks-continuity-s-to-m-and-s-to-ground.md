To test the starter solenoid, use a digital volt/ohmmeter to check for continuity between
the **"S" terminal and the "M" terminal**, and between the **"S" terminal and ground
(frame)**. These two checks confirm the pull-in/hold-in windings are intact and grounded as
designed.

If there is no continuity, the manual's first suspect is not a dead coil but contamination:
check for **ice, dirt, or foreign material** preventing contact, then repair or replace the
solenoid as necessary. This is a quick bench check that can explain a starter that clicks
but does not engage.

> "Check for continuity between 'S' terminal and 'M' terminal, and between 'S' terminal and
> ground (frame). If there is no continuity, check for ice, dirt or foreign material
> preventing contact."

This relates to truck inventory system `electrical-starting`.
