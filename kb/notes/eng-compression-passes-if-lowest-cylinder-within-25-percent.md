Ford does not publish an absolute cranking-pressure number for the 4.9L; instead, the engine is
judged on cylinder-to-cylinder consistency. To test, warm the engine, remove all spark plugs,
and hold the throttle wide open. Install a compression gauge in No. 1, then with the key off use
an auxiliary starter switch to crank at least five compression strokes and record the highest
reading. Repeat for every cylinder.

The pass criterion is that the lowest-reading cylinder is within 25% of the highest. A spread
greater than 25% points to an improperly seated valve or worn/broken piston rings on the `engine`
inventory system. Wide-open throttle and warm-engine conditions matter — a closed throttle or
cold engine will depress and skew the readings.

> "Indicated compression pressures are within specification if the lowest reading cylinder is
> within 25% of the highest reading. ... Variations exceeding 25 percent indicate an improperly
> seated valve or worn/broken piston rings."
