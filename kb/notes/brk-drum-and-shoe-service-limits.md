Rear drum brake specifications for the 1994 F-150 (type DSSA): nominal drum size 11-1/32 x 2.25 in, with a maximum refinish (inside) diameter of 11.090 in. The maximum diameter is also stamped on the outside of each drum, and a drum whose inside diameter exceeds the limit must be replaced rather than turned. The wheel cylinder bore diameter is 0.9375 in, with a maximum allowable hone of 0.003 in.

Lining figures: primary lining thickness 0.224 in / secondary 0.229 in; primary lining length 10.46 in / secondary 10.62 in; total lining area per brake 47.22 sq in, per axle 94.44 sq in; swept area per axle 155.27 sq in. On inspection, shoe lining must be at least 1 mm above the rivets or 1.5 mm above the shoe surface, judged at the thinnest measured spot. These are the `brakes`-system numbers that decide whether drums get refinished or replaced and whether shoes pass.
