When rebuilding the F-150's M5OD 5-speed, each synchronizer (blocker) ring is checked by
assembling it onto its gear cone and measuring the gap between the side faces of the ring and
the gear with a feeler gauge. If that clearance is **less than 0.031 inch**, the ring (or the
gear) is worn beyond service and must be replaced. The FSM notes the limit is identical for the
1st/2nd, 3rd/4th, and 5th/reverse synchronizers.

This measurement is the practical way to diagnose the classic "grinds going into a gear" symptom
on a worn manual box: the ring has worn down enough that it no longer matches shaft speed before
the sleeve engages. Beyond the clearance check, contact between the ring's inner surface and the
gear cone is verified with Prussian blue and corrected by lapping if the pattern is poor. This
applies to the `driveline` inventory system.

> "Measure clearance between side faces of synchronizer ring and gear... If clearance is less than
> 0.031 inch, replace synchronizer ring or gear."
