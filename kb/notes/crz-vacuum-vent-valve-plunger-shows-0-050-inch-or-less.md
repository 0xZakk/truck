The vacuum vent (dump) valve mounts at the brake pedal and is adjusted by position. With the
plunger contacting the brake pedal adapter and the brake pedal in the released position, move
the valve forward in its retaining clip until 0.050 inch (about 1/8 inch) or less of the
plunger shows. After adjusting, confirm the brake pedal is against its stop in the release
position.

This 0.050 inch setting ensures the valve fully closes (holds vacuum) only when the pedal is
released and vents promptly as the pedal moves. Getting it wrong undermines a deactivation
path on the `electrical-body` system: too much plunger showing and the valve may not seat with
the pedal released; too little and it may vent at rest. Always verify the pedal returns
against its stop after the adjustment.

> "Move valve forward in retaining clip with valve plunger contacting brake pedal adapter and
> pedal in released position until 0.050 (1/8 inch) or less of the plunger shows."
