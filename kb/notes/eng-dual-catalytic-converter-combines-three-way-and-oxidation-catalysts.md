The 4.9L's exhaust uses a dual catalytic converter that combines a Three-Way Catalytic Converter
(TWC) with an Oxidation Catalyst (OC). The oxidation function burns off hydrocarbons (HC) and
carbon monoxide (CO), while the three-way function additionally reduces oxides of nitrogen (NOx).
The NOx reduction works in concert with the Secondary Air Injection (AIR) system. Internally the
catalyst is a ceramic substrate coated with catalytically active precious metals.

Understanding this on the `intake-exhaust` inventory system explains why exhaust and emissions
faults are interrelated: a converter problem, an AIR-system fault, or a fueling problem upstream can
all show up as failed emissions or driveability symptoms, and a restricted (melted/plugged)
converter raises backpressure and saps power.

> "The dual catalytic converter is a Three-Way Catalytic Converter (TWC), combined with the
> Oxidation Catalytic Converter (OC). ... it also catalyzes the reduction of NOx."
