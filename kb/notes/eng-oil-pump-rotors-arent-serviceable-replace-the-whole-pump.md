The 4.9L oil pump is a gerotor (inner/outer rotor) pump, and the FSM states its internal
components are not individually serviceable: if any measurement falls out of spec, you replace
the entire pump assembly rather than rebuild it. Inspection covers the housing and rotors for
wear or damage, the cover surface (minor scuff marks are normal), inner/outer rotor tip
clearance with a feeler gauge, and rotor endplay measured with a straightedge across the rotor
and housing — endplay must not exceed 0.005 in. The relief valve is checked for free operation
and its spring for collapse or wear.

On the `engine` inventory system this is the right mental model when chasing low oil pressure:
measure the pump, and if it's worn beyond limits, swap it whole. Reference rebuild clearances
include rotor backlash 0.012 in, rotor-to-body 0.0010-0.0130 in, and relief-spring pressure
20.6-22.6 lb at 2.49 in.

> "Internal components are not serviceable, if any component is not within specifications,
> replace pump assembly. ... measure rotor endplay ... clearance should not exceed 0.005 in."
