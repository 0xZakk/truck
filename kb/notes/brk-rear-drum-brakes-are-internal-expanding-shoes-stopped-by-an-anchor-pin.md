The rear brakes on the 1994 F-150 are internal-expanding drum brakes (type DSSA). Hydraulic pressure pushes the wheel-cylinder pistons (2196) outward, forcing the brake shoes (2018) against the inside of the rotating drum (1126). An anchor pin on the non-rotating brake shoe anchor pin guide plate (2028) acts as a fixed stop, preventing the shoes from being dragged around with the drum and instead reacting the braking force into the backing plate.

This is a single-anchor, dual-servo style drum (primary and secondary shoes have slightly different lining lengths and thicknesses). In the `brakes` system the rear drums also carry the parking-brake mechanism and the automatic adjuster, so the same assembly handles service braking, parking braking, and clearance take-up.

> "The brake drums (1126) utilize internal, hydraulically activated, expanding brake shoes that are applied against a rotating drum by movable wheel cylinder pistons (2196)."
