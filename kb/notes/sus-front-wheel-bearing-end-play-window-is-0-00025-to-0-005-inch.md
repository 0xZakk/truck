After the front wheel bearing is adjusted, the FSM gives two numbers to verify the result.
End play (axial slop measured at the hub) must be 0.006-0.127 mm (.00025-.005 in), and the
torque required to keep the hub rotating must be 1.13-2.82 Nm (10-25 in lb).

These bracket the acceptable range from "too loose" to "too tight": below the end-play window
the bearing is preloaded and will overheat; above it the wheel knocks and wears the races.
The companion adjusting-nut figures are 23-34 Nm (17-25 ft lb) to seat, then a final snug of
2.03-2.26 Nm (18-20 in lb). Keep these handy whenever servicing front hubs in the
`suspension` system.
