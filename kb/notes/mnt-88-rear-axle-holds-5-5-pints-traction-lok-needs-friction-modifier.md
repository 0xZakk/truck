The integral-carrier 8.8-inch ring-gear rear axle on the F-150 `driveline` holds approximately
5.50 US pints (4.58 imperial pints / 2.60 L). Fill until the lubricant is 1/4 to 9/16 inch below
the bottom of the filler hole with the vehicle level. Ford notes the rear axle lube need only be
replaced every 160,000 km (100,000 miles) or if the axle has been submerged in water.

If the axle is a Traction-Lok limited-slip unit, the capacity is essentially the same (~5.5 US
pints) but it requires an added friction modifier: rear axle lubricant (ESP-M2C154-A) plus 4 ounces
of friction modifier C8AZ-19B546-A (EST-M2C118-A). Skipping the friction modifier in a Traction-Lok
axle causes the clutch packs to chatter and shudder in tight turns.
