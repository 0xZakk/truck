The identified specimen separates two unequal tubes, their joining web, end clamps and an external retainer. Corrugations occupy only the region near the large cuffs; the narrow ends have substantial smooth sections.

A local specimen candidate can preserve this topology. Its bends, cuff sections and wall thickness remain estimates. The photographed tape supplies a broad projected envelope, not the installed distance between the airbox and throttle.
