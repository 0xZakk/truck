Because the 300 (`engine`) self-adjusts via hydraulic lifters, "setting" the rockers is just
an assembly torque step. The presenter torques the rocker fasteners to roughly **20 ft-lb**.
There is no rotating-the-engine-and-adjusting routine.

The critical caution during tightening: **confirm the top of each pushrod is seated in the
pocket/cup of the rocker arm** before and as you torque down. If a pushrod is riding on the
edge of the pocket instead of inside it, tightening the rocker can bend the pushrod or knock
it out of position. He inspects every pushrod, rechecking them, before reassembling the valve
cover.

Note: this video is a hobbyist quote of ~20 ft-lb. For a stock 1994 F-150, verify the exact
rocker-arm fastener torque against the factory service manual before final assembly rather
than treating the video's figure as definitive.
