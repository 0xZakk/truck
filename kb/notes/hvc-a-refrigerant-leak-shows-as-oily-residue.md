The FSM notes that on the 1994 F-150 A/C system (inventory id `hvac`) a refrigerant leak will
usually appear as an oily residue at the leakage point. Because the PAG oil circulates with the
refrigerant, escaping refrigerant carries a film of oil that collects and traps dirt at the
exact spot it is leaking.

This makes a visual inspection a fast first step before reaching for an electronic detector:
greasy, dust-caked fittings, line joints, the compressor shaft area, or the condenser are
telling you where to point the halogen probe. The same visual pass also catches obstructed air
passages, broken wires, and loose mounting brackets.
