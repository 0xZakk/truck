The radiator cap's relief pressure is 13 psi. By holding the sealed cooling system at that
pressure, the cap raises the coolant's boiling point above atmospheric boiling, which is what
lets the engine run safely near or above 212 deg F without boiling over.

On the `cooling` inventory system, this is the number a cooling-system pressure test is checked
against: a cap that relieves well below 13 psi can't suppress boiling and will cause overheating
or coolant loss, while a stuck cap that won't relieve risks bursting a hose or the radiator.

> "Relief Pressure 13 psi."
