For the 4.9L engine (inventory id `engine`), piston-to-bore **clearance is 0.0010–0.0018
in**. When fitting rings, the **compression ring end gap is 0.0100–0.0200 in**, and ring
**side clearance** in the groove is 0.0019–0.0036 in for the top ring, 0.0020–0.0040 in for
the second ring, and a "snug fit" for the oil ring. The piston pin bore is 0.9754–0.9757 in
and the standard pin diameter 0.9749–0.9754 in.

These tolerances are checked at assembly: end gap is measured by squaring a ring in the
bore with a piston and reading the gap with a feeler gauge, while side clearance is checked
with the ring installed in its land. Too little end gap risks the ring ends butting and
scuffing the bore when hot; too much gap or side clearance lets combustion gases blow by
and oil up. They depend directly on the bore being within its out-of-round and taper
limits.
