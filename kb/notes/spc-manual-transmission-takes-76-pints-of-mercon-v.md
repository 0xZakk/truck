The 1994 F-150's 5-speed manual transmission (the Mazda-built M5OD-R2 behind the 4.9L,
inventory id `engine`) has a **refill capacity of 7.6 pints** per the FSM capacity table
(the "5-speed (Mazda) ... Bronco, Econoline, F-series" line). The fluid type for this
"all others" 5-speed is **Mercon V automatic transmission fluid**, a supersession noted in
TSB 06-14-4 — not the 80W-EP gear oil the same table lists for the older 4-speed boxes.

This is a common point of confusion: although it is a manual gearbox, it is filled with ATF,
not gear oil. Using a heavy gear lube here causes hard cold shifting and notchy
synchronizer engagement. The fill is checked at the side fill plug with the truck level.
