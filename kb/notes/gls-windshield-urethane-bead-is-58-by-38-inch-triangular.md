When bonding a replacement windshield, the FSM specifies cutting the Essex urethane adhesive
nozzle to lay a **triangular bead 5/8 inch (16 mm) tall by 3/8 inch (9 mm) wide** around the
perimeter of the pinch weld. The residual urethane left on the flange from the old glass is
first trimmed to a uniform layer of about **1/16 inch (1.6 mm)** so the new bead seats evenly.

The bead must be set before the urethane skins over, and exposed bare metal on the pinch weld
gets Essex Pinchweld Primer U-413 (6-10 minute dry) before the bead is applied. These dimensions
are the load-bearing detail of the `glass` / `body-cab` windshield replacement — too little
adhesive cross-section undermines structural retention.
