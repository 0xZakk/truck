Fel-Pro's manufacturer catalog identifies MS93838 as the upper plenum gasket in its4.9MFI gasoline VINY application covering1994. Its separate model index includes the corresponding F1502WD engine. This establishes an applicable replacement identity for research.

The application does not supply gasket thickness, compression, bolt dimensions or a measured joint outline. Photo-derived proportions remain comparison estimates, and manufacturer application does not prove the owner's installed component brand.
