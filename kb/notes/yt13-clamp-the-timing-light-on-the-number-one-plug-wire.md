To strobe the timing marks on the `ignition` system, connect the timing light's two power leads
to the battery (the tool may emit a faint whine when powered). Then clamp the inductive pickup
around the **number-one** spark plug wire — the clamp has a "plug" arrow that must point toward
the spark plug so the pickup senses the firing pulse in the correct direction. Each time the
number-one cylinder fires, the light flashes, freezing the balancer mark so you can read the
timing.

A basic timing light without an advance dial works fine for this job. A dialed (advance) light
lets you set a target advance on the dial and then read zero on the balancer when you reach it,
but with a plain light you simply read the actual degree mark on the balancer directly. Mind the
spinning fan and pulley while positioning the light; a flexible-stem pickup helps get a good
viewing angle without your hands near the belt.
