The 4.9L 300 inline-six in this truck (`engine`) is a **hydraulic-lifter pushrod OHV**
engine. The hydraulic lifters use oil pressure to take up clearance in the valvetrain
automatically, so there is no manual valve-lash to set. Unlike a solid-lifter or adjustable
valvetrain — where you rotate the engine and adjust each valve in turn on its intake/exhaust
event — the 300's rockers have nothing to "dial in."

This is why searching for a lash-setting procedure for the 300 comes up empty: the procedure
does not exist for this design. The presenter notes the same is true of other Ford
hydraulic-lifter engines such as the 302. Setting the rockers on the 300 means torquing the
rocker fasteners to spec, not measuring or adjusting a gap.
