The owner's guide sets engine coolant service at **every 4 years or 50,000 miles
(80,000 km)**. The system uses **Ford Premium Cooling System Fluid** (or an equivalent), and
the guide provides a drain, flush, and refill procedure.

A safety point the manual stresses: **never remove the coolant recovery cap while the engine
is hot** — the system is pressurized and hot coolant can cause serious burns. Let the engine
cool first. When topping up or changing coolant, follow the guide's refill procedure to avoid
trapping air in the system.

This relates to truck inventory system `cooling`.
