On the 4.9L (300 cu in) a truck that is hard to start, stalls almost immediately after
starting, and hesitates on acceleration commonly has corrosion on the **throttle position
sensor (TPS)** and **idle air control (IAC, "idle speed control")** electrical connectors.
Because the TPS feeds the PCM the throttle angle and rate of change, a dirty or high-resistance
connection there produces erratic readings that show up as poor throttle response.

The cheap first fix is to unplug each connector, spray the corrosion away with brake cleaner or
electronic/contact cleaner, let it dry about a minute, and reconnect. The presenter says
cleaning the TPS connector "usually solves almost all" hesitation-on-acceleration complaints.
This is a connection-quality fix, not a sensor replacement — do it before condemning the sensor
itself.

This touches the `fuel` and `engine` systems (TPS and IAC are part of the EEC-IV fuel/idle
control). It is a hobbyist driveway tip; if symptoms persist, pull codes rather than assuming
the connectors were the whole problem.
