On the XR-50 gear the thrust-bearing preload and the worm-to-rack preload are set at the
factory and cannot be changed in service. The single field adjustment is the total overcenter
(meshload) position load, which takes up excess lash between the sector and rack teeth so the
steering does not feel loose on center.

The FSM sequence: disconnect the pitman arm and discharge fluid from the gear; turn the wheel
45 degrees from the right stop and, with an inch-pound torque wrench on the steering-wheel
nut, measure the torque to rotate the shaft about 1/8 turn; return to center and measure the
torque to rock the shaft across center. The center "meshload" should be at least 7 in lb
greater than the 45-degree reading; if it is not, loosen the locknut and turn the adjusting
screw until the center reading is 10-14 in lb above the 45-degree reading, then hold the screw
and tighten the locknut. Note this gear carries an air bag, so the module must be removed
first. This keeps on-center feel tight in the `steering` system.
