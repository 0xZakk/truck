The elongated catalog gasket has two interior openings as well as its end holes, but the drawing does not label their functions. Preserve both openings in a topology study without declaring either a pressurized outlet or locating dowel.

A future installed model needs a separate correspondence proof from pump passage to gasket aperture to block gallery. A shaft-clearance hole alone cannot pass that test. Useful negative controls include swapping the two interior openings, mirroring the pattern, blocking the proposed discharge, and replacing the shape with a single-hole ring. These tests can improve the next candidate without pretending an unmeasured passage is verified.
