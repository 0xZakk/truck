Ford Racing's Basic Engine Dimensions table gives the 1965–96 300 inline-six a nominal deck height of 10.000 inches, or 254.000 mm. This supports the current reconstruction's deck datum at 254 mm above its crank centerline. It establishes a family reference dimension, without measuring this particular engine or supplying a permissible machining tolerance.

The coordinated option B pump/cover candidate places its fourth screw above the existing deck and adds block material through the head joint. Independently observed head/block seam pixels, transformed using the candidate's existing camera and scale, imply approximately 290 mm. That disagreement is evidence against the registration hypothesis; changing the block deck to match the candidate would contradict the primary nominal reference. The photograph's forward gear centers cannot serve as coplanar crank datums because their depth and parallax are unknown.

A conditional smaller footprint fitted to the deck and the inherited pan datum is useful sensitivity analysis only. The pan height and seal-origin registration remain unverified. New coupled CAD requires another independent front-plane datum, then fresh checks of the fixed shafts, gears, cover, pump, head, and outlet. The Carter pump's axial mounting height is a separate constraint and cannot calibrate a transverse photograph.
