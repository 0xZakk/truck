Actual photographs of the RF-E7TE-9K461-A5E casting show a boss and installed electrical-looking sensor projecting from the terminal runner's side near the head flange. A close-up independently displays the casting identifier. The keyed connector, wrench hex and location agree with the earlier ACT service illustration; no visible part number or exposed probe establishes this sensor's exact identity.

Different views retain six circular upper ports, staggered flange holes, head ports and an asymmetric rear opening. These are useful camera-registration landmarks rather than dimensions. A future fit can anchor the upper flange as a plane and use the head flange as an independent check, while declaring the provisional pitch used for scale. The gallery provides neither calibrated stereo nor the installed thread/gauge depth, and the broad seller fitment table cannot establish exact1994 application.
