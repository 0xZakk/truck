Both the upper and lower ball joints holding the spindle to the I-beam are checked with the
wheel raised and rocked by hand, and both share one go/no-go limit: more than 1/32 inch of
movement at the joint means replace it.

To check the LOWER joint, grasp the lower edge of the tire and move the wheel in and out
while watching the lower spindle arm relative to the lower portion of the axle jaw. To check
the UPPER joint, grasp the upper edge of the tire and move it in and out. In each case the
inspection is reading relative motion across the joint, not total wheel wobble — so the
bearings must be tight first.

This is the standard front-end looseness test for the `suspension` system; a knock over
bumps or vague steering that traces to ball-joint play beyond 1/32 inch condemns the joint.
