The specified `cooling` fluid is Ford Premium Cooling System Fluid (Ford specification ESE-M97B44-A),
mixed 50% with water. Ford explicitly warns not to mix coolant types.

The 50/50 mix sets the freeze/boil protection and corrosion-inhibitor concentration the system was
designed for; going too rich or too lean degrades heat transfer or protection. The do-not-mix
warning matters because combining incompatible coolant chemistries can drop additives out of
solution and form gel or sludge that clogs the radiator and heater core. When topping off or
refilling, stick to one compatible coolant type at the 50/50 ratio.
