Ford does not publish an absolute cranking-pressure number for the 4.9L — only a relative
acceptance rule. To run the test: warm the engine to operating temperature, remove all spark
plugs, and hold the throttle plates wide open. Install a compression gauge in the No. 1 plug
hole, fit an auxiliary starter switch, and with the key off crank the engine at least five
compression strokes, recording the highest reading. Repeat for every cylinder.

The readings are within specification if the lowest-reading cylinder is within 25% of the
highest. Variations exceeding 25% indicate an improperly seated valve or worn/broken piston
rings. This relative test is the core `engine` mechanical-health check and a sanity step before
chasing driveability faults through the sensors and `fuel`/`ignition` electronics.
