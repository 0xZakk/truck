On this OBS truck the PCM (`electrical-starting` / engine control) is mounted in the fender area
and is straightforward to swap. The owner's steps: remove the single **10 mm retaining bolt**,
pull off the **rubber surround/boot** that weatherproofs the connector, disconnect the harness,
and **loosen the fender liner** to give enough clearance to draw the module out. He used a
battery jumper to repower it and the truck fired immediately.

Critically, a replacement module **must match the original by serial number and part number**
for the specific truck — not just the model year. He cites roughly the 1993–1995/96 range for
this 4.9L application but is explicit that year alone is not enough to guarantee compatibility.
He sourced a used unit on eBay for about $150. (He does not mention clearing Keep-Alive Memory
or relearning after the swap; the FSM-correct practice after replacing an EEC part is to clear
KAM and drive a relearn cycle, which is worth doing even though this video skips it.)
