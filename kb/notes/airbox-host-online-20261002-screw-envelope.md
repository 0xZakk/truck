Auveco 13019 cross-references the exact body screw in the 1994 service illustration. Its 6.3 mm tapping diameter, 1.81 mm pitch, 19 mm length, 11.5 mm head envelope and 8 mm hex provide a bounded replacement specification. It is not an M6 × 1 machine screw.

These dimensions do not specify the receiving clip, pilot hole or bracket thickness. The two box screws have a different identity and remain dimensionally unresolved. An isolated screw envelope could be modeled after review; installed engagement cannot yet be accepted.
