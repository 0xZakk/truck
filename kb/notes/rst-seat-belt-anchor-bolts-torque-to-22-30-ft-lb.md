The seat belt specification page gives a single, consistent torque for every belt fastener on the truck:
30-40 Nm (22-30 ft lbs). This covers front seat belt bolts, front outboard anchor bolts, front center anchor
bolts, rear outboard anchor bolts, and rear inboard/center anchor bolts.

Having one number across all positions simplifies belt reinstallation in the `interior` and `body-cab` —
there is no position-specific value to look up. It also underlines that these are structural fasteners
carrying crash loads, so they should be torqued (and the mounting sheet metal restored to its original shape)
rather than just snugged, consistent with the FSM's broader caution against bolting belts into distorted
mounting areas.

> "Front Seat Belt Bolts 30-40 Nm (22-30 ft lbs)... Rear Inboard Center Anchor Bolts 30-40 Nm (22-30 ft lbs)"
