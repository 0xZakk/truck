Fuel tank capacity on the 1994 F-150 is set by cab style and wheelbase, not by the engine
(inventory id `engine`). The **midship tank** ranges from 62 L (16.3 gal) on the shortest
Regular Cab up to 72 L (19.0 gal) on the longer-wheelbase trucks; a Super Cab on the
138.8 in wheelbase carries a 64 L (16.5 gal) midship tank. The **aft-of-axle tank is 69 L
(18.2 gal)** across configurations and is the standard second tank on dual-tank trucks.

The FSM warns against "topping off" past the automatic pump shutoff: fuel expansion from
heat can overflow at the filler cap. On dual-tank trucks it recommends drawing from both
tanks after a fill to keep levels down. Knowing which tank is fitted and its capacity is
the baseline for diagnosing fuel-gauge and sender complaints.
