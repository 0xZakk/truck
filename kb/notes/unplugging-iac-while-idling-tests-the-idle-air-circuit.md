A quick field test for an idle problem on the EFI 4.9L: with the engine idling, **unplug the
idle air control (IAC) valve** and watch what the idle does. The IAC meters air around the
throttle plate to set idle speed, so disconnecting it should change the idle noticeably.

In the documented case, unplugging the IAC made the idle worse and started a rhythmic
**surge up / surge down** hunting pattern. That confirms the idle-air path is active and
worth investigating — pointing toward the IAC itself, the throttle body, or a vacuum leak
near it, rather than toward ignition. Pair this with measuring the IAC's resistance per the
manual, and follow up by inspecting the throttle-body gasket and vacuum lines.

This is a diagnostic step, not a fix: it tells you the idle-air system is implicated and
where to look next. It relates to truck inventory systems `fuel` and `intake-exhaust`.
