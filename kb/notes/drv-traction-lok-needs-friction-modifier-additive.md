The F-150's Ford 8.8 rear axle holds **5.5 pints (2.6 L)** of hypoid gear lubricant. If the axle
is the **Traction-Lok** limited-slip type, the FSM requires adding **4 ounces of friction modifier
additive** (part C8AZ-19B546-A or equivalent) on top of the gear oil.

The friction modifier is not optional on a limited-slip: the Traction-Lok's clutch packs depend on
it for smooth engagement. Without it (or with plain gear oil after a fluid change), the clutches
grab and release abruptly, producing the classic limited-slip **chatter or shudder** felt and heard
when turning tightly, such as pulling into a parking spot. This is part of maintaining the
`rear-axle` inventory system.

> "Rear Axle Oil (Hypoid Gear Lubricant) 5.5 pt / 2.6L. Add 4 ounces of friction modifier additive
> part No. C8AZ-19B546-A or equivalent."
