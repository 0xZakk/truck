The Windows and Glass service precautions open with the supplemental restraint disarm/arm
procedure because windshield removal and related work can involve the steering column, mirror,
and battery. To disarm, disconnect the battery negative then positive cable, wait at least one
minute for the diagnostic monitor's backup power supply to deplete, remove the driver air bag
module, disconnect its connector, and connect Rotunda Air Bag Simulator 105-00010 (or
equivalent) to the harness at the top of the steering wheel. Arming reverses the steps.

Two practical reminders accompany it: record the radio presets before pulling the battery, and
expect the PCM to relearn its adaptive strategy afterward, which may require 10+ miles of
driving before driveability is normal. This ties the `glass` / `body-cab` work into the same
one-minute SRS depletion rule used throughout the truck.
