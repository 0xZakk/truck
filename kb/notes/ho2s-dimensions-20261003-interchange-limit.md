NTK's own catalog bridges Bosch 15718 to 22503, but also lists other alternatives and warns that interchange data is a guide. The22503 image and 558 mm lead entry differ from the Bosch specimen. The bridge establishes a research comparison, not identical body shape, harness routing or dimensions.

A primary NTK 22503 thread specification would now have a qualified route into the comparison ledger. The reviewed catalog lacks that specification, so common retail M18×1.5 listings cannot yet be relabeled manufacturer-confirmed. Keep the isolated estimated specimen separate from an accepted receiving-bung interface.
