For the 4.9L, the owner's guide specifies **SAE 5W-30 as the preferred viscosity**, calling
it best for engine performance, fuel economy, and protection in all climates down to
**-15 °F (-25 °C)**. The oil must be Motorcraft or an equivalent meeting **Ford specification
ESE-M2C153-E** and carrying the API certification mark.

The guide is explicit about what **not** to use: non-detergent oils, oils labeled API SA
through SG, and any aftermarket oil additives or engine treatments. Certified synthetic of
the preferred viscosity is permitted, but using synthetic does **not** extend the change
interval — oil and filter still change on schedule.

> "Engine oils with an SAE 5W-30 viscosity are PREFERRED for your vehicle… for all climates
> down to -15°F."

This relates to truck inventory system `engine`.
