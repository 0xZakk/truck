Because the wiper/washer multi-function switch is mounted on the air-bag-equipped
steering column, the FSM's service precautions require disarming the air bag before
the switch is serviced. To disarm: disconnect the battery negative cable then the
positive cable, wait at least one minute for the back-up power supply to deplete its
stored energy, remove the air bag module retaining nuts, disconnect the driver-side
air bag connector, and connect a Rotunda Air Bag Simulator (105-00010 or equivalent)
to the harness before reconnecting the battery.

Two follow-on cautions matter. Record the radio preset frequencies before
disconnecting the battery, and expect the Powertrain Control Module to relearn its
adaptive strategy afterward — the truck may need 10 or more miles of driving before
drive feel returns to normal. This precaution applies to any wiper/washer column work
(`electrical-body`).
