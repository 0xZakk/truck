When rebuilding the F-150's alternator, the brush and slip-ring limits differ by the
alternator's current rating. For the **40, 40HE, 60, 65, and 75 A** units: slip-ring
minimum turning diameter **1.22 in**, maximum runout **0.0005 in**; brush length new
**0.480 in** with a wear limit of **0.25 in**. For the **95 A** unit: slip-ring minimum
diameter **1.04 in**, maximum runout **0.0005 in**; brush length new **0.709 in** with a
wear limit of **0.12 in**.

Matching the spec block to the actual unit matters because the 95 A alternator uses
noticeably longer brushes and a smaller slip-ring diameter than the smaller units. A brush
worn below its limit, or a slip ring turned under the minimum diameter, means the part is
done regardless of how the alternator bench-tests.

> "Slip Ring Turning — Minimum Diameter 1.22 Inch — Maximum Runout 0.0005 Inch; Brush
> Length — New 0.480 Inch — Wear Limit 0.25 Inch"

This relates to truck inventory system `electrical-charging`.
