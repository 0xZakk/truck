The Mazda-built M5OD 5-speed in the F-150 is lubricated with **Mercon V automatic transmission
fluid** ("5-speed, All others," per TSB 06-14-4), not the 80W EP gear oil used in the 4-speed or
the Mitsubishi 5-speed. The **refill capacity for the F-series Mazda 5-speed is 7.6 pints**.

This is an easy fact to get wrong, because a manual transmission "should" take gear oil by
intuition. Putting heavy gear oil in an M5OD that calls for ATF produces stiff, notchy shifting —
especially when cold — and can damage the synchronizers over time. This belongs to the
`driveline` inventory system.

> "5-speed, All others — See TSB 06-14-4 — Mercon(R) V Automatic Transmission Fluid"
