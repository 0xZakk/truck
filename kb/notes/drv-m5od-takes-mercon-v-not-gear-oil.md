The Mazda-built M5OD-R2 5-speed in the 1994 F-150 (behind the 4.9L, inventory id `engine`) is
lubricated with **Mercon V automatic transmission fluid** ("5-speed (Mazda R1/R2), All others,"
a supersession noted in TSB 06-14-4), not the 80W-EP gear oil the FSM table lists for the older
4-speed boxes or the Mitsubishi 5-speed. The **refill capacity for the Bronco/Econoline/F-series
Mazda 5-speed is 7.6 pints**. This unit belongs to the `driveline` inventory system.

This is an easy fact to get wrong, because a manual gearbox intuitively "should" take heavy gear
oil. Putting gear oil in an M5OD-R2 that calls for ATF produces stiff, notchy cold shifting and
poor synchronizer engagement, and can damage the synchronizers over time. The fluid level is
checked at the side fill plug with the truck level.

> "5-speed, All others — See TSB 06-14-4 — Mercon(R) V Automatic Transmission Fluid"
