The 4.9L inline-six fires its cylinders in the order 1-5-3-6-2-4. Its base (initial) ignition
timing is 10 degrees BTDC, and the spark-plug electrode gap is 0.042-0.046 in. These three
numbers are the backbone of a basic ignition tune-up and are also the values referenced when
setting valve lash, which is performed in firing-order sequence.

Keeping the firing order handy on the `engine` inventory system prevents the classic
crossed-plug-wire no-start after a tune-up, and the relatively wide 0.042-0.046 in gap is
characteristic of this EEC-IV distributor ignition with its high secondary voltage.

> "Electrode Gap 0.042-0.046 Inch (in). ... Base Timing 10deg BTDC. ... firing order sequence of
> 1-5-3-6-2-4."
