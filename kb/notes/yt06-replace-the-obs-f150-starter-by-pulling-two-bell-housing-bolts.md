Replacing the starter on an OBS F-150 is mechanically simple and can be done with basic hand
tools, even at the roadside. Start by disconnecting the **negative battery cable** so the heavy
starter feed cannot short out while you work. The starter is bolted to the **bell housing with
two bolts**; once both are removed, the unit hangs free, which gives the clearance needed to
reach the electrical connections behind it. Pulling the remaining cable lets the starter drop out.

Reinstallation is the literal reverse: hang the new starter, reconnect the electrical terminal,
run the two bell-housing bolts back in, reconnect the battery, then crank the engine to confirm
it spins and starts. This relates to the `electrical-starting` system.

The video gives no torque values for the bell-housing bolts or the cable nut; consult the
factory service manual for the correct figures rather than guessing, since the heavy battery
cable connection in particular needs to be tight to carry full cranking current.
