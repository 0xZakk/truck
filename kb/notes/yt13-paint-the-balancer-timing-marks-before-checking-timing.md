Before checking base timing on the `engine`, make the harmonic-balancer degree marks legible
under the timing light by painting them. Turn the engine over by hand with a ratchet or breaker
bar (clockwise) to bring each mark up where you can reach it, brush paint into the groove, then
quickly wipe off the excess so the paint stays down in the engraved line. A bright paint fill
makes the marks flash clearly when the strobe hits them.

On the demonstrated balancer the graduation marks are spaced every 2 degrees, and the small
numbered marks (such as 10 and 20) are actually narrower and harder to spot than the larger
unnumbered graduations between them — so know which mark is which before you start. The presenter
also recommends painting all the marks you care about *before* the balancer is installed (for
example during an engine rebuild), because it is far easier to do off the engine than reaching in
around the fan and pulley.
