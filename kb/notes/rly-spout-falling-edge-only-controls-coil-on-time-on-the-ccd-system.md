SPOUT (Spark Output) is a digital signal generated by the PCM that carries spark angle (timing)
information to the ICM. On the push-start (gray) system, only the rising edge of SPOUT matters — it
fires the coil — and the falling edge is ignored. On the computer-controlled-dwell (CCD, black)
system the SPOUT signal is the same except the falling edge is now generated to control when the
coil is turned ON, so the CCD system uses both edges of SPOUT.

This is why dwell on a CCD truck is "entirely controlled by the SPOUT signal": the PCM shapes both
edges and the ICM simply obeys. Understanding which edges are active explains why disturbing the
SPOUT line affects spark timing and coil charging, and it ties the `electrical-starting` spark path
directly to PCM control rather than to a self-contained module.
