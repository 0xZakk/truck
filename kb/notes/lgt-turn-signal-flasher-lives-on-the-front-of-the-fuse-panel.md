The turn signal flasher on the 1994 F-150 is a discrete plug-in unit, separate from the
column multi-function switch. The FSM locates it behind the left-hand side of the instrument
panel, on the front of the fuse panel. Knowing it is a standalone plug-in matters for
diagnosis: turn signals that come on steady but do not flash usually point at the flasher
itself rather than the switch.

The hazard warning circuit uses its own separate flasher. This is part of the truck's
`electrical-body` system.

> "The Turn Signal Flasher is located behind the LH side of the instrument panel, on front
> of the fuse panel."
