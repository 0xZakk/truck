The HVAC vacuum harness on the 1994 F-150 (inventory id `hvac`) is a pre-assembled, color-
coded bundle of nylon tubing measuring 0.15 in outside diameter and 0.09 in inside diameter,
bonded to nylon or rubber connectors. A rubber insert in each connector end seals against the
component's nipple. The colors are a visual aid for routing during both production and service.

For field repair, a damaged section of nylon tube can be replaced with 5/32 in or 7/32 in
rubber hose. Knowing these sizes lets you splice a cracked vacuum line without replacing the
whole harness.
