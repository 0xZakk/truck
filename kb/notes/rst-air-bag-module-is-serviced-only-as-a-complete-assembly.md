The driver-side air bag module sits in the center of the steering wheel and is made up of an inflator, the
air bag, a mounting plate and retaining ring, and the steering-wheel trim cover. The FSM repeats for each of
these that they are NOT serviceable items — the module is replaced only as a complete assembly. You do not
rebuild an inflator, re-skin a torn trim cover, or swap an individual bag.

Inside the module, when the sensors close, an igniter converts the electrical signal to heat and ignites the
sodium azide / copper oxide gas generant, producing the nitrogen gas that inflates the bag; the mounting
plate and retaining ring seal the bag to the inflator and bolt the whole module to the wheel; and the molded
trim-cover tear seams split to let the bag out. Because everything is integrated and pyrotechnic, service is
remove-and-replace of the entire `interior` steering-wheel module.

> "The inflator is a component of the driver side air bag module and is not a serviceable item. The driver
> side air bag module is serviced as a complete assembly."
