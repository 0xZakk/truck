When the M5OD is apart, the FSM gives go/no-go inspection rules for the main rotating and
housing parts of the `driveline`:

- **Input shaft** — replace if the splines are damaged, the needle-bearing surface in the bore
  is worn or rough, or the cone surface (where the synchronizer ring seats) is damaged.
- **Countershaft** — replace if the gear teeth or splines are bent, worn, or scored.
- **Extension housing** — check for cracks and make sure the machined mating surfaces are free of
  burrs and nicks. The **rear bushing is not serviceable**: if it is worn, the whole housing is
  replaced as a unit. The output-shaft oil seal is installed after the housing is on the
  transmission.
- **Speedometer drive/driven gears** — check the gear shafts for wear or damage and inspect the
  O-ring and oil seals for weakness or damage.

These are qualitative "inspect and replace if damaged" checks that complement the dimensional
limits (synchronizer clearance, shaft runout) measured elsewhere in the rebuild.

> "Extension housing rear bushing cannot be serviced. If service is required, replace as a unit."
