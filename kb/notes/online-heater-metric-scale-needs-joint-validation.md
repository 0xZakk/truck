A project analysis of the GMB replacement photographs found an inconsistency between the inherited model proportions and a scaled-orthographic interpretation of the side view. The estimated image ratio of mounting-to-pulley separation to hub diameter is approximately 1.30, with declared pixel perturbations giving 1.19–1.43. The inherited 143 mm reference span and 70 mm hub diameter imply 2.04. These are project inferences from photographs, not manufacturer dimensions.

This rejects that combination of estimated geometry and camera model; it does not establish the correct physical dimensions. Perspective, landmark choice and replacement differences remain possible causes. Rear-view hole correspondence provides conditional radial constraints, but the occluded front views do not complete a calibrated four-view fit. A collision-free tube must therefore remain a conditional candidate until its scale and installed interfaces are supported. The reproducible analysis is `reference/engine/online-heater-radial-registration.json` outside the knowledge base.
