When inspecting an M5OD shift fork at rebuild, the contact surface where the fork rides in the
clutch hub sleeve groove is checked for wear and damage, and the fork-to-sleeve clearance should
be **0.031 inch**. A fork worn thinner than this lets the sleeve drift and contributes to the
truck popping out of gear or being slow to engage.

The FSM pairs this with a function check of the hub and sleeve: with the sleeve held horizontal,
the hub is lifted about three-quarters of the way out and released, and a good assembly will let
the hub slide back down into the sleeve under its own weight. If the hub has to be pushed, the
synchronizer assembly needs service. This is part of the `driveline` inventory system.

> "Check contact surface of shift fork and clutch hub sleeve for evidence of wear or damage.
> Measure from shift fork to clutch hub sleeve. Clearance should be 0.031 inch."
