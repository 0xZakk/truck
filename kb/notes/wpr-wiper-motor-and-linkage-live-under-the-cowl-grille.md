The wiper motor, pivot shafts, and linkage on the 1994 F-150 all sit under the cowl
grille, so the FSM's arm/linkage and motor removal procedures share the same access
steps. After disconnecting the battery ground cable, remove both wiper arms, the blade
assemblies, and the upper antenna assembly; then remove the cowl grille attaching
screws, raise the grille slightly, disconnect the washer nozzle hose, and lift off the
cowl grille assembly.

With the grille off, the linkage clips and the retaining clip on the wiper motor arm
release the linkage, and the pivot body-to-cowl panel screws free the arm and
pivot-shaft assemblies; the motor's own attaching screws then free the motor. Two
details are easy to forget: the upper antenna comes off as part of this teardown, and
the washer nozzle hose must be disconnected before the grille will come off
(`glass`, `electrical-body`). Handle the freed motor gently to protect its ceramic
magnets.
