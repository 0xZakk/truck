The ICM is serviced as a module/heatsink assembly mounted on the fender. To remove it: take out
the two ignition module/heatsink retaining screws, disconnect the module connector (push where it
says PUSH, then slide the connector off), remove the assembly, then remove the two screws that
separate the module from the heatsink.

On installation the heatsink interface must be managed for cooling: clean the mating surfaces and
apply dielectric silicone (Ford D7AZ-19A331-A or equivalent) to the ignition module base before
seating it. Two different torque values apply — the module-to-heatsink retaining screws go to
15-35 lb-in (1.7-4.0 N-m), and the heatsink-to-fender retaining screws go to 80-124 lb-in
(9-14 N-m). Getting the dielectric grease and the lower torque right matters because the ICM relies
on the heatsink to shed heat; this is part of `electrical-starting` ignition serviceability.
