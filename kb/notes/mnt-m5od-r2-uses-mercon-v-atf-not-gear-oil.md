A common mistake is assuming a manual gearbox always takes heavy gear oil. For the 5-speed
(Mazda R1/R2) — the M5OD-R2 in F-series — the FSM, via TSB 06-14-4, specifies Mercon V Automatic
Transmission Fluid for the "all others" group. The older 4-speed boxes used 80W EP gear oil, but
this 5-speed `driveline` unit runs ATF.

Using the wrong, thicker lubricant in the M5OD-R2 leads to hard shifting (especially when cold) and
poor synchronizer action. The FSM table lists a refill of 7.6 for the Bronco/Econoline/F-series
application, so plan the fill quantity accordingly when servicing the transmission.
