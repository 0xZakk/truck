The 2WD F-150's 5-lug wheels use a 1/2-20 stud, and each lug nut torques to 135 Nm (100 ft lb).
Heavier 8-lug applications instead use a 9/16-18 stud at 190 Nm (140 ft lb).

The FSM is emphatic that the torque value assumes clean, dry, dirt- and paint-free threads and
that oil or grease must never be applied to the studs or nuts. Lubricated threads reach the
same torque reading at a much higher actual clamp load, which can yield or break studs. So when
servicing wheels on the `suspension` system: clean threads, no lube, 100 ft lb on the half-ton.
