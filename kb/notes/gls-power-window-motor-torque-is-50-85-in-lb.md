When installing a new power window motor and drive assembly in the front door, the FSM
specifies tightening the motor retaining screws to **5.6-9.6 N·m (50-85 in-lb)**. This is an
inch-pound spec, not foot-pounds — the fasteners thread into the regulator/door structure and
are easy to strip or shear if overtightened to a foot-pound figure.

This value applies to the `glass` / `body-cab` power window service. The drilled motor-access
holes used to reach the screws are separately sealed with 1-inch-square waterproof body tape.
