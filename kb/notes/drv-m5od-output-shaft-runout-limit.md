The M5OD output shaft is checked for straightness by setting it in V-blocks, mounting a dial
indicator against it, and measuring runout at several points along the shaft. **Total output
shaft runout should not exceed 0.002 inch.** A bent output shaft beyond this limit causes
vibration and accelerates synchronizer and bearing wear, so it is a replace item.

This is a rebuild-bench check rather than an in-vehicle test, and it belongs to the `driveline`
inventory system.

> "Total output shaft runout should not exceed 0.002 inch."
