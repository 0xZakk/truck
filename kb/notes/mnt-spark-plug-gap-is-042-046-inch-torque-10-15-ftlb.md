For the 4.9L `engine`, spark plugs (type BSF-44C) are gapped to 0.042-0.046 in and tightened to
10-15 ft lb (14-20 Nm). The relatively wide gap suits the high-energy distributorless-style
secondary that can reach ~40,000 volts, giving a strong spark kernel for clean combustion.

The torque window matters because these plugs thread into a cast iron head: under-torquing risks
blow-by and a poor heat path, while over-torquing can damage threads. Setting gap and torque to
spec is the core of a routine tune-up alongside the cables and base timing.
