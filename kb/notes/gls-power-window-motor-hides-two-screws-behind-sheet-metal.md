The power window motor and drive is a one-piece replacement assembly mounted inside the door,
held by three retaining screws. The catch is that two of those three screws sit opposite solid
door inner-panel sheet metal with no factory access hole. The FSM procedure is therefore to
drill them open: locate the two drill dimples stamped opposite the unexposed screws and drill
through with a 12.7 mm (1/2-inch) bit. Before drilling, check inside the door that no electrical
wires lie in line with the holes.

With the two drilled holes plus the existing larger access hole, all three screws can be
reached. The motor is then pushed toward the outer sheet metal to disengage it from the
regulator gear and removed from inside the door. On reassembly each drilled hole is sealed with
a 25.4 mm (1-inch) square of waterproof body tape (D6AZ-19627-A or equivalent) to keep the door
cavity watertight. This procedure is part of the truck inventory `glass` / `body-cab` systems.
