The Army C5AZ-6600-A exploded view and table distinguish two mounting bolts, four cover screws and two inlet screws. This reinforces the raised mount and separate side inlet, instead of treating the cover ears as engine mounting feet.

Together with the identified replacement gasket row, this supports a three-aperture inlet and four-aperture mounting-pattern hypothesis. It does not establish the pattern coordinates or prove which interior mounting aperture is the shaft or pressure-side opening. The industrial pickup/gasket revision is not automatically applicable to the1994 truck.
