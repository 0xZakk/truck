The driver air bag itself has concrete physical specs worth recording. It is constructed of neoprene-coated
nylon, is 711 mm (28 inches) in diameter, and fills to a volume of 0.065 cu m (2.3 cu ft) in approximately
40 milliseconds.

That 40-millisecond fill time puts a number on why deployment is described as happening in a "fraction of a
second" and why the system must run straight off the battery rather than wait for any slow logic — there is
no time to spare between sensor closure and full inflation in front of the driver in the `interior`. These
figures also distinguish this single driver-side bag from later dual-bag systems.

> "The air bag: Is constructed of neoprene coated nylon. Is 711 mm (28 inches) in diameter. Fills to a
> volume of 0.065 cu m (2.3 cu ft) in approximately 40 milliseconds."
