On the 1994 F-150 there is no separate wiper switch and washer switch — both are the
same column-mounted multi-function switch. The FSM lists identical component testing
and service entries under both the "Wiper Switch" and "Windshield Washer Switch"
headings, each pointing to the same multi-function switch component testing procedure,
terminal identification, and schematic.

This matters for parts and diagnosis: a fault in either the wipe controls or the wash
button is traced and replaced as one unit. The switch feeds its signals to the wiper
control module rather than directly powering the motor, so confirming the switch with
the component test (terminal continuity per the schematic) separates a bad switch from
a bad module (`electrical-body`).
