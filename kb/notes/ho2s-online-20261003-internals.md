Bosch illustrates a ceramic thimble, separate heater, protection tube, electrode/filter layers and electrical connections in its thimble-family brochure. These establish distinct functions to explain; they do not fix the exact 15718 internal stack. The pictured family nose has holes unlike the target's visible slots.

Heated narrow-band operation is target-supported, and Bosch explains its switching family using a zirconia Nernst cell. Exact15718 ceramic shape, seals, supports, wire joints and dimensions need a target-specific section or identified teardown. Until then, any internal teaching geometry must be explicitly illustrative and must not be presented as an exact physical BOM.
