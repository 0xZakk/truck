When the 4.9L cylinder head (inventory id `engine`) is rebuilt, the factory geometry is:
**valve seat angle 45°**, seat runout 0.002 in, and seat width 0.060–0.080 in intake /
0.070–0.090 in exhaust. The **valve stem-to-guide clearance is 0.0010–0.0027 in**, with a
guide inside diameter of 0.3433–0.3443 in. The cylinder head warpage limit is 0.007 in.

Valve springs are checked against **free length** (intake 1.96 in, exhaust 1.78 in), an
**out-of-square limit of 0.078 in**, installed height (intake 1.61–1.67 in, exhaust
1.44–1.50 in), and pressure: **closed 66–74 lb**, **open 166–184 lb**. Valve tips may be
refinished up to 0.01 in and the minimum valve margin is 1/32 in.

These are the limits a head shop works to; a stem clearance over 0.0027 in or a collapsed,
out-of-square spring leads to valve guttering, oil down the guides, and poor sealing —
distinct from the running **valve clearance** (lash) set on the rocker, which is a separate
spec.
