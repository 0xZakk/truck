A common misconception is that every air bag sensor must be scrapped after any collision. The FSM is clear
that undamaged sensors reset automatically after an accident and can be reused. They only need replacement
when proper alignment is impossible — bent mounting brackets, cut wires, or smashed connectors — or when the
crash damaged the structure they bolt to.

The decision rule for crash repair: if the radiator support, frame rails, or cowls were damaged, inspect the
sensor mounting brackets for deformation, and replace a sensor whose bracket is deformed whether or not the
bag deployed, then restore the body structure to its original condition. Sensor orientation is critical to
proper operation, so a sensor that looks intact but sits on bent sheet metal can leave the system unable to
deploy correctly. This is the diagnostic crossover point between the `body-cab` structure and the `interior`
SRS.

> "Undamaged sensors will reset automatically after an accident and can be reused."
