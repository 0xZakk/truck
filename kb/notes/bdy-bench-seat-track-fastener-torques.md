When reinstalling the 1994 F-150's bench seats, the FSM gives distinct torque values for the
different joints in the seat-mounting stack:

- Seat track-to-floor-pan retaining screws (standard and split bench): 34-46 N-m (25-34 ft-lb)
- Seat track-to-seat-cushion-frame screws: 17-23 N-m (13-17 ft-lb)
- Split bench front seat-to-seat-back-frame nuts: 18-27 N-m (13-27 ft-lb)

These are safety-critical fasteners — the seat is an anchor point for occupants and, on the
split bench, for a center safety belt — so they get proper ft-lb torque rather than "snug."
The FSM also cautions to handle the seat and tracks carefully, since dropping the assembly or
sitting on a seat that is not yet secured in the vehicle can damage the track components. These
values apply to the `body-cab` seat system.
