Key clutch fastener torques for the F-150 (`driveline` system):

- **Pressure plate & cover to flywheel:** **15-20 ft·lb** for bolt #382087, or **20-29 ft·lb**
  for bolt #387299 — the correct value depends on which bolt the assembly uses, so confirm the
  bolt before torquing.
- **Clutch housing (bellhousing) to rear engine cover plate:** **40-50 ft·lb**.
- **Clutch release lever seat to housing screws:** **12-18 ft·lb**.
- **Concentric slave cylinder bolt:** **14-19 ft·lb**.
- **Slave cylinder bleed screw:** **2.2-3.7 ft·lb**.

Pressure-plate bolts should be tightened gradually in a cross/star pattern to avoid distorting
the cover, even though the FSM lists only the final torque value.

> "Pressure Plate & Cover Assembly to Flywheel — Bolt # 382087: 15-20 ft.lb; Bolt # 387299: 20-29 ft.lb."
