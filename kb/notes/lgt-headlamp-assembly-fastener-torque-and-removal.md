Removing a complete headlamp assembly on the 1994 F-150 starts at the headlamp door: take
out the two screws and two nuts that retain the door, pull the door, and remove the three
bulbs and sockets. After disconnecting the engine-control sensor wiring from the headlamp
bulb, remove the two retainers holding the upper attachments of the headlamp, then turn the
horizontal headlamp adjusting screw completely out of its adjusting nut so the lamp can be
pulled from the vehicle.

On reinstallation the FSM specifies tightening the screws to 1.4-2.3 N·m (13-20 lb-in) and
the nuts to 4-7 N·m (36-61 lb-in), then checking headlamp aim. These are deliberately light
torques into plastic, so a regular torque wrench in foot-pounds is the wrong tool here. This
is part of the truck's `electrical-body` system.
