For the F-150 heater-core job, the narrator calls out the fastener sizes: the blend/control
**module** unbolts with a **7 mm** socket, and the **perimeter screws** around the heater-core
cover are **5/16"**, reachable with a ratchet on an extension. One perimeter screw is **hidden
behind a cable** — pull the cable out and lift it to free its little hook, then reach the screw
with a stubby or ratcheting driver. A wire to the module also has to be disconnected to get the
cover fully loose.

These are the practical sizes to stage before starting `hvac` heater-core work; they are the
video author's observations rather than factory-manual torque specs, so treat them as
loosen/remove guidance, not a torque reference.
