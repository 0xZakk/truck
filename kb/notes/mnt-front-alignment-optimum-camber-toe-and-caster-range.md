The FSM front alignment specs are: camber optimum 0.25° with a ±0.5° range; total toe optimum
0.06° with a ±0.25° range; caster minimum 2.0° and maximum 6.0°. Camber split is optimum 0.0°
(±0.4°) and caster split optimum -0.5° (±0.4°), describing the allowable left-to-right difference.

These numbers ground tire-wear and steering-pull diagnosis. Positive camber outside range or a
toe error scrubs tires and pulls the truck; caster affects straight-line stability and steering
return. Because the front bearings and steering geometry share the same wheel end, alignment is
typically checked alongside bearing preload and tire condition when the front end is serviced.
