For the manual-transmission 4.9L, cooling system capacity is 13 qts (US) without A/C, 14 qts with
A/C, and 15 qts with A/C plus Super Cooling. The variation reflects the larger radiator and heater
circuit volume that comes with air conditioning and the heavy-duty cooling option.

When servicing the `cooling` system, this capacity sets how much 50/50 premix to mix for a complete
fill after a flush. Knowing which options the truck has (A/C, Super Cooling) tells you whether to
target 13, 14, or 15 quarts so the system ends up properly full and at the correct concentration.
