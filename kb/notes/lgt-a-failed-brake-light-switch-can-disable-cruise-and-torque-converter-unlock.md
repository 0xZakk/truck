Because the 1994 F-150's brake on/off switch feeds the PCM, speed control, shift lock, and
ABS in addition to the stop lamps, a misadjusted or failed switch can produce symptoms with
no obvious connection to lighting. Cruise control that will not engage or that won't hold,
or a torque converter clutch that never unlocks when braking, can all trace back to a
brake switch that isn't signaling correctly.

The FSM's install note points at the most common adjustment fault: the stop light switch
wires must be long enough to allow full travel of the brake pedal, and the switch/pushrod
must be reassembled with its nylon washers and bushings and the hairpin retainer. A switch
positioned so it never fully releases (or never closes) at the pedal's travel limits will
mislead every module downstream. When chasing an intermittent cruise or shift-interlock
fault on this truck's `electrical-body` system, verify the brake switch and its pedal
geometry first.
