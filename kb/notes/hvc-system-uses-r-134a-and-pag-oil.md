The 1994 F-150 (inventory id `hvac`) is one of Ford's early R-134a trucks. The system charge
is 2.0 lb (32 oz) of R-134a refrigerant, and the total system refrigerant-oil capacity is
7.0 oz of PAG oil — specifically Motorcraft YN-12b or equivalent.

These two numbers anchor every A/C service on this truck: PAG is the correct oil (R-12 mineral
oil is not compatible with R-134a), and 2.0 lb is the charge target when recharging. Getting
either the oil type or the charge weight wrong is a common cause of poor cooling.
