When servicing brake hydraulics on the 1994 F-150, the FSM specifies cleaning all brake parts in clean isopropyl alcohol (or a Rotunda Brake Parts Washer 065-00016 or equivalent) — not petroleum solvents, which would swell the rubber seals. Each part is then inspected for wear or damage, cylinder bores are checked for rust, scores, or other damage, and bleeder-screw passages are verified clean and open. Worn or damaged parts are replaced.

Critically, if dirt is found anywhere in the hydraulic system, the entire system must be flushed with clean isopropyl alcohol — contamination is treated as system-wide, not local. This protects the `brakes` system's seals and bores from abrasive or solvent damage that would otherwise cause leaks or sticking pistons.

> "If dirt is found in any part of the hydraulic system, flush the entire system with clean isopropyl alcohol."
