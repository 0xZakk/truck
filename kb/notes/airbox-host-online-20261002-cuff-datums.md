The service illustration requires both tube end faces to meet their throttle stop flanges around the full circumference. Arrow alignment fixes orientation at the throttle and allows ±2° relative to the air-cleaner cover arrows. Four clamps retain the four ends.

The current modeled mouth diameters and spacing are estimates. They cannot establish actual insertion depth or the airbox world pose. A useful future interface must name the stop face, cuff axis and clock reference for each end, while keeping body-to-engine motion and unmeasured diameters explicit.
