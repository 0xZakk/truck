The speed control system maintains any driver-selected speed above approximately 30 mph and
below 125 mph. To set it, the engine must be running and the vehicle must already be moving
faster than 30 mph; with the On switch engaged and speed stable, pressing Set-Acc captures
that speed, which is then held until the brake is pressed, the system is turned off, or a new
speed is set.

This is a useful diagnostic baseline: a complaint that "cruise won't set at low speed" may be
normal behavior, not a fault. Genuine inoperative complaints should be evaluated at a stable
speed comfortably above 30 mph. The lower limit exists because below it the system has no
reliable speed reference to regulate against and engagement could be erratic.

> "The speed control system is designed to automatically maintain vehicle speed at any
> driver-selected speed above approximately 30 mph and below 125 mph."
