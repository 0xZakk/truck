If a power window or door-lock rocker switch stays depressed instead of returning to neutral —
which can make the door locks cycle continuously — TSB 94-11-18 points to excess molded plastic
("flash") in the gray portion of the switch bezel binding the rocker, not an electrical fault.

The repair is mechanical: operate the switch slowly to find where it binds, remove the bezel
(one retainer screw, then pull up on the front to release the clip), unscrew and remove the
switch from the bezel, and carefully scrape off the excess plastic from the gray housing without
marring its appearance. Reassemble and confirm the switch no longer binds before reinstalling.
This is the one genuinely mechanical/repairable switch complaint in the `glass` / `body-cab`
window-switch TSBs.
