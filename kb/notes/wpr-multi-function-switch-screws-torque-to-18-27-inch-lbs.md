When reinstalling the wiper/washer multi-function switch onto the steering column, the
FSM specifies torquing the switch-to-column attaching screws to 18–27 inch-lbs (note:
inch-pounds, not foot-pounds). Two cautions accompany the procedure: on tilt-column
trucks the tilt lever must be unscrewed and removed first, and the PRNDL (gear
indicator) cable must not be damaged during removal.

The low torque value reflects that these screws thread into the column tube casting
and the switch body; overtightening risks stripping or cracking the plastic switch
housing (`electrical-body`).
