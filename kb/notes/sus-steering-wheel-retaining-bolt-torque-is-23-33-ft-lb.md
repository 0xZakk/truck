When reinstalling the steering wheel on the F-150, the retaining bolt torques to 31-45 Nm
(23-33 ft lb), per the Steering Wheel specifications. Because the air bag and clockspring sit
behind the wheel, this is done only after the air bag is disarmed and the wheel is correctly
indexed to the shaft. Keep this value with the air-bag arming/disarming procedure when
servicing the `steering` system.
