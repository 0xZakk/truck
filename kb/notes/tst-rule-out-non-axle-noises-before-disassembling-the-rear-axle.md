The first step in the FSM Diagnosis Guide for the rear axle is to confirm the noise is
actually coming from the axle and not from somewhere else. The guide dedicates a whole
"Non-Axle Noises" section to sources that are easy to mistake for axle noise: grille
whistle, trim moulding, roof-top luggage rack, belt chirp, A/C system pulses, belt squeal,
exhaust, tires, and aftermarket add-ons such as running boards, body-to-frame grounding,
antennas, visors, and bug deflectors.

This matters because rear-axle teardown is costly and many "axle" complaints turn out to
be tires or wind/exhaust noise that vary with speed in a similar way. On the 1994 F-150
(`engine`), eliminate these external sources first — swap or re-balance tires, check
exhaust clearance, remove or secure aftermarket add-ons — before draining the axle and
opening it up to inspect gears and bearings.

> "Non-Axle Noises: Grille Whistle, Trim Moulding, Roof Top Luggage Rack, Belt Chirp, A/C
> System Pulses, Belt Squeal, Exhaust, Tires, After Market Add On's."
