Under **normal service**, the owner's guide calls for changing the engine oil and oil filter
**every 6 months or 5,000 miles (8,000 km), whichever comes first**. Under **severe service**
— heavy towing or hauling, extended idling, dusty or off-road conditions — the interval
tightens substantially (the guide ties severe use to a much shorter, roughly 200-hour-
equivalent schedule).

Use the correct oil for the job ([[notes/49l-uses-5w30-oil-meeting-ford-spec-ese-m2c153-e|5W-30
meeting ESE-M2C153-E]]) and always change the **filter with the oil**. Switching to synthetic
does not buy a longer interval. Let the oil drain back into the pan before draining for a
fuller change.

This is the single most important recurring maintenance item for keeping the 300-six
"bulletproof." It relates to truck inventory system `engine`.
