The public intake illustration’s numbered runner references and engine-front arrow place the sensor at the number-one end of the lower casting. The exact1994 service detail shows its dedicated boss. This identifies the host and topological station; it does not justify moving the sensor into the upper plenum or air-cleaner duct to simplify installation.

An installed comparison must expose the protected sensing tip to that runner’s air space and preserve adjacent flange, injector and rail interfaces. Neither source provides an installed gauge plane, port-axis angles or connector clocking. The16–24Nm service torque is a tightening requirement, not a depth measurement. Current provisional lower-intake geometry has no ACT receiver, so numerical placement requires a separate declared host revision.
