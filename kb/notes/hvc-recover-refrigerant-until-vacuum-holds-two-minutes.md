Before any A/C repair on the 1994 F-150 (inventory id `hvac`), refrigerant is recovered with a
recovery/recycling station rather than vented. Connect the station hoses to the service ports
(hoses should have shutoff/check valves within 12 in of the ends to keep air out), then run the
station until the gauge shows a vacuum.

The completeness check is the key step: close the inlet, turn the station off, and let the
system sit closed for about two minutes. If pressure rises (refrigerant boiling out of the
oil), repeat the recovery cycle until the vacuum stays at 0 psi or less for two full minutes.
Only then is the system truly empty and ready to service, evacuate, and recharge.
