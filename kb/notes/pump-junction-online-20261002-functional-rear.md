The identified Carter rear view separates a machined sealing ring and bolt apertures from the inlet casting outside it. Those features support protecting named sealing and mounting interfaces; they do not establish the arbitrary14mm-deep solid slab used by an earlier candidate contract.

A revised research contract should retain the actual gasket mating face, explicit positive backing, dry bolt seats and bores, fifth-aperture identity, and rotating impeller clearance. Backing thickness remains an estimate until sourced. Additions or removals farther forward can then be assessed against wet-wall and flow requirements rather than silently exempted. The old full-slab failure must remain recorded.
