Walker identifies two inlet connections with loose two-bolt flanges and spherical flares. A future model must distinguish each pipe flare/seat from its movable retaining flange. A flat plate touching a modeled manifold is not evidence that this spherical joint seals or is centered correctly.

The source supplies no flare radius, inlet bore, bolt spacing, flange thickness or clocking. Existing manifold outlet coordinates are model state, not primary pipe measurements. Preserve those interfaces until the integration owner reviews the joint contract; do not bend the new pipe or flatten its seats merely to obtain a collision pass.
