## Key Notes

- [[notes/clutch-pedal-position-switch-is-the-start-interlock-on-manual-f150|The clutch pedal position switch is the start interlock on a manual F-150]] — this truck cranks only with the clutch down

## Common Issues

- A no-crank on this manual truck often traces to the **clutch pedal position switch** rather than the starter itself.
