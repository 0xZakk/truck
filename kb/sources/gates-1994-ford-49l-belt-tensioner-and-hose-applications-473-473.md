## Summary

The 2008 Gates application catalog, PDF473 (printed389), separates the 1994 4.9L F-Series pickup from the Econoline van. It lists K060980 serpentine belt with air conditioning and K060970 without it, tensioner pulley 38022, and tensioner assembly 38131. This gives specific replacement identities to investigate for the front accessory drive.

The upper radiator hose is 22417. The lower hose is 20609 for factory A/C, versus 21236 or flexible alternative 26505 for dealer-installed A/C. For the five-speed manual, both heater-to-thermostat and heater-to-water-pump entries list 18767. The automatic-transmission tee branches should not be copied into the manual-truck reconstruction.

The routing index on PDF119 (printed35) specifies S7004 with A/C and S6008 without A/C. S7004 on PDF163 (printed79) shows seven pulley contact points; it was visually reviewed. The schematic establishes routing topology, not pulley identities or measured center coordinates.

## Key Points

- These are application identities, not measured installed geometry.
- A/C installation type remains an unresolved lower-hose qualifier.
- Belt routing, pulley centers, tensioner angle and hose paths require separate evidence.
- The manufacturer damper photograph shows integrated serpentine grooves; avoid inventing a separate bolt-on pulley from a generic engine diagram.
