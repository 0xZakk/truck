## Summary

The FSM lists cooling-system capacity by transmission and equipment. For a manual-transmission
4.9L the capacity is 13 qts (US) without A/C, 14 qts with A/C, and 15 qts with A/C and Super
Cooling. The specified fluid is Ford Premium Cooling System Fluid (Ford spec ESE-M97B44-A) mixed
50% with water; Ford warns not to mix coolant types.

These figures anchor `cooling` service: capacity drives the amount of premix needed for a fill,
and the 50/50 mix plus single-type rule prevents the chemistry problems that come from mixing
incompatible coolants.

## Key Points

- Manual transmission without A/C: 13 qts (US).
- Manual transmission with A/C: 14 qts (US).
- Manual transmission with A/C and Super Cooling: 15 qts (US).
- Fluid: Ford Premium Cooling System Fluid, Ford spec ESE-M97B44-A.
- Mix 50% coolant with water; do not mix coolant types.
