## Summary

The Ignition Control Module (ICM) switches the ignition coil on and off under the direction of
the PCM's Spark Output (SPOUT) signal. The internal circuitry comes in one of two arrangements,
identified by module color: a push-start ICM (gray) or a computer-controlled-dwell (CCD) ICM
(black).

In the push-start system the ICM itself determines when to turn the coil ON based on engine rpm,
allowing increased dwell (coil ON time) during cranking; the coil fires (turns OFF) on the rising
edge of the SPOUT signal, and the falling edge is ignored. In the CCD system, dwell is controlled
entirely by the SPOUT signal — the ICM does not internally decide coil ON time but responds
directly to SPOUT, which now uses both edges: the rising edge fires the coil and the falling edge
sets when the coil is turned ON. SPOUT itself is a digital signal generated by the PCM that
carries spark angle (timing) information to the ICM.

Service involves a module/heatsink assembly mounted on the fender, retained by screws, with
dielectric silicone applied to the module base on installation. Specific torque values are given
for the module-to-heatsink screws and the heatsink-to-fender screws.

## Key Points

- ICM color identifies strategy: gray = push-start, black = computer-controlled dwell (CCD).
- Push-start: ICM decides coil ON time from rpm; coil fires on SPOUT rising edge (falling ignored).
- CCD: SPOUT controls dwell entirely; both SPOUT edges are used (rising fires, falling sets ON).
- SPOUT is a digital PCM-generated signal carrying spark angle information to the ICM.
- Install with dielectric silicone (Ford D7AZ-19A331-A or equivalent) on the module base.
- Module-to-heatsink screws: 15-35 lb-in (1.7-4.0 N-m); heatsink-to-fender screws: 80-124 lb-in (9-14 N-m).

## Notable Excerpts

> "The internal circuitry of the Ignition Control Module (ICM) will have one of two possible
> arrangements, push start (Gray ICM) or computer controlled dwell (CCD) (Black ICM)."

> "The coil ON time, or dwell, for this system is thus entirely controlled by the SPOUT signal.
> The ICM does not internally determine when to turn the coil ON as it does on the push start
> system."

> "Place the module in the heatsink assembly, then install and tighten the retaining screws to
> 15-35 lb. in. (1.7-4.0 N-m). ... Tighten the screws to 80-124 lb. in. (9-14 N-m)."
