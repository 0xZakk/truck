## Summary

The factory service manual's description of the 1994 F-150 starting system. The truck uses
a **Motorcraft permanent-magnet gear-reduction starter motor** with a solenoid-actuated
drive. The page enumerates the full starting circuit and the components that must all be
intact for the engine to crank.

Because this truck is a **manual-transmission (M5OD-R2)** F-150, its starting safety
interlock is the **clutch pedal position switch** rather than the park/neutral position
switch used on automatics — a distinction that matters when diagnosing a no-crank.

## Key Points

- Starter type: permanent-magnet, gear-reduction, with a solenoid-actuated drive.
- The starting system includes: the starter motor, the battery, a remote-control starter
  switch (part of the ignition switch), the park/neutral position switch (automatic) **or**
  the clutch pedal position switch (manual), the starter relay, and heavy-gauge circuit wiring.
- Any one of these in the circuit can be the cause of a no-crank condition.
