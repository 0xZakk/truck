## Summary

The wiper/washer system is protected by an 8.25-amp circuit breaker located in the
fuse junction panel. The FSM specifies removing the breaker and running two separate
bench tests to confirm correct operation: a sustained-rating test and an
overcurrent trip-time test.

## Key Points

- Circuit breaker is rated 8.25 amps and lives in the fuse junction panel.
- Test 1 (hold rating): adjust tester current to the breaker rating, then hold it
  for 10 minutes. If the breaker opens during those 10 minutes, replace it.
- Test 2 (overcurrent trip): adjust to twice rated current; the ammeter reading
  should drop to zero within 20 seconds. If it takes longer than 20 seconds to open,
  replace the breaker.
- Before connecting, short the tester leads together to set the current draw.
