## Summary

The voltmeter displays current battery/charging-system voltage to the driver, measuring the
electrical flow from the battery to indicate whether output is within tolerance. Under normal
operating conditions the reading ranges from 13.5 to 14.0 volts. A sustained undercharge or
overcharge indication means the battery and charging system should be checked.

This gives the driver a direct, continuous read on charging health that complements the
charge warning lamp (which only signals an out-of-range condition).

## Key Points

- Displays battery/charging-system voltage continuously.
- Normal operating range: 13.5-14.0 volts.
- Sustained undercharge or overcharge -> inspect battery and charging system.
- Complements the charge warning lamp, which only trips on an out-of-range condition.

## Notable Excerpts

> "The voltmeter reading can range from 13.5-14.0 volts under normal operating conditions. If
> an undercharge or overcharge condition is indicated for an extended period, the battery and
> charging system should be checked."
