## Summary

This consolidates the 4.9L I6 tune-up and engine-performance specifications and procedures.
Base ignition timing is 10 deg BTDC. To set it, place the transmission in NEUTRAL (manual) with
A/C and heater off, connect an inductive timing light, and disconnect the in-line Spark Output
(SPOUT) connector (or pull the shorting bar) so the PCM can't add computed advance. Start the
engine, bring it to operating temperature, and check/adjust initial timing at timing rpm, then
reconnect SPOUT and confirm the distributor advances beyond the initial setting. The FSM warns
to start with the ignition key only — a remote starter or disconnected start wire leaves the ICM
in start-mode timing.

The firing order is 1-5-3-6-2-4. Spark plugs are type BSF-44C, gapped 0.042-0.046 in, torqued
10-15 ft lb (14-20 Nm). A compression check is done warm with throttle held wide open, cranking
each cylinder at least five strokes; readings are acceptable if the lowest cylinder is within
25% of the highest. The hydraulic-lifter valve clearance (collapsed-tappet) procedure checks
rocker-to-valve-tip clearance with allowable limits 0.100-0.200 in (desirable 0.125-0.175 in),
correcting out-of-range clearance with a shorter or longer pushrod.

## Key Points

- Base timing: 10 deg BTDC; set with SPOUT disconnected, transmission in NEUTRAL, A/C/heat off.
- After setting, reconnect SPOUT and verify advance beyond the initial setting.
- Use the ignition key to start, not a remote starter, or the ICM stays in start-mode timing.
- Firing order: 1-5-3-6-2-4.
- Spark plug: BSF-44C, gap 0.042-0.046 in, torque 10-15 ft lb (14-20 Nm).
- Compression: warm engine, throttle wide open, crank >=5 strokes; lowest cylinder within 25% of highest.
- Valve clearance (collapsed tappet): allowable 0.100-0.200 in, desirable 0.125-0.175 in; adjust via pushrod length, in firing-order sequence.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Disconnect the single wire in-line Spark Output (SPOUT) connector or remove the shorting bar from the double wire SPOUT connector."

> "Indicated compression pressures are within specification if the lowest reading cylinder is within 25% of the highest reading."
