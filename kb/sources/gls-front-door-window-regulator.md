## Summary

The front door window regulator is riveted to the door inner panel rather than bolted. Removal
requires drilling out the rivets: punch the center pin from each attaching rivet, then drill
the head off with a 1/4-inch bit, taking care not to enlarge or damage the sheet-metal holes.
The regulator arm is then disengaged from the glass bracket and the regulator pulled out.

On installation the FSM specifies a hydraulic rivet gun to re-rivet the regulator, but also
allows substituting screw-and-nut hardware (1/4-20 x 1/2-inch screw and washer plus a 1/4-20
nut and washer, or equivalent metric retainers) when rivets are not available. The glass must
be supported full-up throughout, since removing the regulator frees the glass.

## Key Points

- Remove the door trim panel and access cover (if equipped) first.
- Support the glass in the full-UP position — the regulator carries the glass, so it must be propped before disconnecting.
- Remove the center pin from each regulator attaching rivet with a drift punch, then drill the head off each rivet with a 6.35 mm (1/4-inch) drill; avoid damaging the sheet-metal holes.
- Disengage the regulator arm from the glass bracket channel, then remove the regulator.
- Reinstall with Rotunda Hydraulic Rivet Gun 107-00600 (or equivalent).
- Alternative fastener: a 1/4-20 x 1/2-inch screw + washer with a 1/4-20 nut + washer (or equivalent metric retainers) may replace the rivets.
- Check window operation before reinstalling the trim panel.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Remove center pin from regulator attaching rivets with drift punch. Then, drill head from each rivet using a 6.35mm (1/4-inch) drill and remove rivet. Be careful not to damage sheet metal holes during drilling."

> "A 1/4 inch -20 x 1/2 inch screw and washer assembly and a 1/4 inch -20 nut and washer assembly may be used in place of the rivets if rivets are not available."
