## Summary

PDF page 184 (printed 166) lists FC65662 needle pilot bearing for the six-cylinder Ford half-ton truck family, 1990–2002, including 300/4.9L. This establishes a catalog replacement application, not proof of the installed bearing. The page also lists clutch-release bearing 614169 for 1993–1999 five-speed hydraulic-piston applications. Application qualifiers should remain attached to each identity.
