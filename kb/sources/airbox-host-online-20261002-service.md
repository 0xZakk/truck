## Summary

The inspected 1994 2WD 4.9L illustration identifies two N611062-S2 box screw/washer assemblies, two base-17C431 grommets and four N610959-S2 body screws. Base 9647 is the formed bracket. These counts must stay separate. Full bracket and grommet service numbers remain unknown.

Two clean-air tubes use four clamps. Their throttle ends seat against the full stop-flange circumference; arrows establish clocking at the throttle and within ±2° at the lid. Base 9A675 fresh-air inlet is a separate interface. The figure supplies no metric installed frame. The later California automatic single-duct illustration is excluded.
