## Summary

The Ford PE&I Standard and Utility Parts Catalog, FPS3733, February1996, lists 378644-S on PDF page216 / printed205, H-143 PIN—DOWEL. The actual row gives diameter 1/2 inch, length 13/32 inch, locator NN-61-B and Type1. Converted nominal values are 12.7 mm diameter and 10.31875 mm length; conversion adds no manufacturing precision. The scanned page footer also prints copyright1998.

The generic Type1 drawing depicts an unstepped cylinder. It does not establish the part-specific bore or split. The Army industrial exploded view identifies the same part number and illustrates an annular piece; that separate evidence must not be replaced by a claim that the catalog icon proves a solid pin. Neither source establishes exact1994 truck applicability.

## Key Points

- The accessible primary Ford row verifies nominal hardware dimensions independently of the blocked earlier engine-catalog snippet.
- The word “split” in that snippet remains unverified. Do not use it to construct a slot.
- Unknowns include bore, wall, gap, tolerance, material, insertion and passage function.
