## Summary

This gallery-specific source supplements [[sources/pump-metric-20261002-carter|Carter's height specification]]. The manufacturer's product HTML supplies six full-resolution static views and a24-frame rotation pattern. All six static views and rotation frames1/7/13/19 were inspected; this is not a24-frame survey.

The rear view shows a machined ring around the impeller, four bolt apertures and a distinct larger fifth opening. The inlet casting extends laterally beyond the ring and transitions to a round beaded neck. Front/side views show a formed heater tube with a root collar, raised bolt pads, a bearing-support tower and an externally threaded hub nose. The initial impression that frame1 lacked a tube was an occlusion error, corrected by the other views.
