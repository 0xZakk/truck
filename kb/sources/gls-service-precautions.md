## Summary

Before any glass or window work that involves the steering column, instrument panel, or
disconnecting the battery, the FSM directs the technician through the supplemental restraint
(air bag) disarm and arm procedures. The page is shared boilerplate that protects against
accidental air bag deployment and against losing PCM adaptive strategy when the battery is
disconnected.

The disarm sequence removes the driver air bag module and substitutes a Rotunda Air Bag
Simulator on the harness; the arm sequence reverses it. A mandatory one-minute wait after
disconnecting the positive cable lets the diagnostic monitor's backup power supply bleed off
before the deployment circuit can be touched.

## Key Points

- Record pre-set radio frequencies before disconnecting the battery.
- Disconnect battery NEGATIVE first, then POSITIVE.
- Wait at least one minute for the back-up power supply to deplete before working near the air bag.
- Disarm: remove air bag module retaining nuts, disconnect the driver air bag connector, connect Rotunda Air Bag Simulator 105-00010 (or equivalent) to the harness at the top of the steering wheel, then reconnect battery.
- Arm: reverse — disconnect battery, remove simulator, reconnect air bag connector, install module, reconnect battery.
- After reconnecting the battery, the PCM relearns its adaptive strategy; the vehicle may need 10+ miles of driving before driveability normalizes.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Wait at least one minute for back-up power supply to deplete its stored energy."

> "Connect Rotunda Air Bag Simulator 105-00010, or equivalent, to vehicle harness at top of steering wheel."

> "After the battery has been disconnected and reconnected, abnormal drive conditions may be present while the Powertrain Control Module (PCM) relearns its adaptive strategy. The vehicle may need to be driven 10 or more miles to relearn the strategy."
