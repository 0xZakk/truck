## Summary

The Mirrors Description and Operation page covers the optional automatic day/night (electro-
chromic auto-dimming) inside rearview mirror. The mirror's reflective coating darkens
automatically in response to a built-in light sensor, reducing glare from headlights behind
the truck without a manual flip lever.

This is an `interior-trim` / `body-cab` component. The 1994 F-150 also offered power exterior
mirrors (with a power mirror switch and dedicated wiring diagrams), but the only descriptive
operation text in this section is for the auto-dimming inside mirror.

## Key Points

- The mirror face changes from highly reflective to slightly reflective when its built-in
  light sensor detects light (glare).
- A DISTANCE control on the bottom of the unit sets the mirror's sensitivity to light.
- Power exterior mirrors and their switch are documented separately, mostly via wiring
  diagrams rather than operation text.

## Notable Excerpts

> "The mirror face changes from highly reflective to slightly reflective when the built-in
> light sensor detects light. DISTANCE control, located on the bottom of the unit, determines
> the mirror's sensitivity to light."
