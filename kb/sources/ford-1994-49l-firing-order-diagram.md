## Summary

The saved factory firing-order page states 1–5–3–6–2–4. Its diagram, visually reviewed from the local image, numbers cylinder 1 at the vehicle front and cylinder 6 at the rear. A curved arrow beside the distributor cap shows clockwise rotation as viewed from above the cap.

The diagram supports correcting the explorer's distributor animation to clockwise, at half crankshaft speed. It does not provide distributor body dimensions, the installed engine's body clocking, the wire paths, base ignition timing, or the detailed shutter indexing. The black cap terminal marks cylinder 1 in the illustrated orientation; the current model does not yet reproduce that absolute installed orientation.

## Key Points

- Firing sequence: 1–5–3–6–2–4.
- Cylinder numbering runs from front to rear.
- The rotor turns clockwise viewed from the cap.
- Use the applicable installation/timing procedure rather than treating this provisional CAD assembly as an installation jig.
