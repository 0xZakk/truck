## Summary

Dorman's vehicle selector confirms 598-105 for a 1994 Ford F-150 with the 4.9L inline-six. The direct OE cross is F4TZ9D477C. Published dimensions are 17.9 inches in length and 0.74 inches outside diameter; construction is stainless steel with two threaded connectors.

The manufacturer photograph shows a protective woven sleeve covering most of the bent tube and exposed end fittings. The page does not establish thread sizes, wall thickness, bend coordinates, sleeve composition or the definition of its length field. Those remain modeling uncertainties, rather than dimensions to infer from the photograph.

## Key Points

- Vehicle-specific fit was verified using the live manufacturer selector.
- OD converts to 18.796 mm; the 454.66 mm length conversion does not determine whether length is developed or overall.
- Replacement applicability does not prove the owner's installed original part number.
