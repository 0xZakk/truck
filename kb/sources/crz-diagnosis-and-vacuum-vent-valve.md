## Summary

Cruise control diagnosis starts with a visual inspection to find obvious faults before any
pinpoint testing: check for bare, broken, or disconnected wires, and inspect the servo
throttle actuator function and free operation of the throttle linkage. There is an important
prerequisite — if the amber anti-lock brake (ABS) indicator stays lit with the ignition in
Run, service the rear anti-lock brakes first, because the speed control system shares the
brake/ABS inputs and ABS faults can corrupt the diagnosis.

From there the FSM routes to a symptom chart and a set of lettered pinpoint tests (A through
K), each targeting a distinct complaint: A Speed Control Inoperative, B No Power to Servo,
C No Power on Deactivator Switch, D Steering Wheel "On" Switch Inoperative, E Speed Control
Surges, F Coast/Tap-Down Inoperative, G Accel/Tap-Up Inoperative, H Resume Inoperative,
J Speed Control Does Not Disengage When Brakes Applied, and K Will Not Turn Off With the
"Off" Switch. The detailed step tables are delivered as scanned images.

The system also includes a vacuum vent (dump) valve mounted at the brake pedal. Its
adjustment: move the valve forward in its retaining clip with the plunger contacting the
brake pedal adapter and the pedal released, until 0.050 inch (about 1/8 inch) or less of the
plunger shows, then confirm the brake pedal is against its release stop.

## Key Points

- Visual inspection first: bare/broken/disconnected wires, servo actuator function, free
  throttle linkage operation.
- Prerequisite: service rear ABS first if the amber ABS indicator stays lit in Run before
  diagnosing speed control.
- Diagnosis is organized into a symptom chart plus lettered pinpoint tests A-K (each a
  specific symptom).
- Vacuum vent/dump valve adjustment: 0.050 inch (~1/8 inch) or less of plunger showing with
  pedal released, plunger contacting the brake pedal adapter.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Inspect for bare, broken or disconnected wires. Inspect the servo throttle actuator
> function and free operation of throttle linkage."

> "If the amber check anti-lock brake indicator remains lit when the ignition switch is in the
> Run position, service the rear anti-lock brakes before continuing the speed control
> diagnosis."

> "Move valve forward in retaining clip with valve plunger contacting brake pedal adapter and
> pedal in released position until 0.050 (1/8 inch) or less of the plunger shows. ... Ensure
> brake pedal is against stop in release position after adjustment."
