## Summary

The archived factory pan installation drawing depicts two side gaskets, front and rear pan seals, and screw-and-washer assemblies. Its fastener callout is 5/16-18 x .87 at 25 places. The separate rear-pan-seal drawing distinguishes that interface from the crankshaft rear oil seal. Bolt stations and flange dimensions are not supplied. This illustration alone does not establish the gasket piece count for the specific 1994 service part: see [[sources/fel-pro-vin-y-engine-gasket-applications|the VIN-Y gasket catalog review]] for the molded-rubber replacement and one-piece variant leads.

Dorman 264-011 directly cross-references the factory catalog's F6TZ6675LA. Its published envelope is 29.5 inches long, 12 inches maximum width and 9.25 inches maximum depth. It is a steel rear-sump replacement without a baffle. The photograph shows a long shallow forward section transitioning into a deeper rear section. Maximum dimensions do not establish flange width or mounting-hole coordinates.

## Key Points

- The existing constant-depth pan is insufficient; pan, seals, flange and pickup need coordinated reconstruction.
- The Dorman replacement specifies M14x1.5 drainage. The separately researched 090-005.1 plug has a 1/2-20 UNF thread and must not be silently combined with it.
- Replacement geometry does not prove which pan or plug is installed on the owner's truck.
