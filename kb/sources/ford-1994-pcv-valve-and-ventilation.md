## Summary

Ford describes crankcase gases being returned to the intake for combustion. A metering valve regulates ventilation flow and prevents an intake backfire from propagating into the crankcase. The illustrated typical valve has a body, plunger, orifice washer and spring. This generic section establishes the teaching mechanism; it is not a dimensional drawing of the installed valve.

The parts listing distinguishes trucks below and above 8,500 lb GVW. It prints EOTZ6A666A for the lower category and D9DZ6A666A for the higher category. The owner's 6,050-lb door rating selects the lower category, but the printed letter O versus digit zero in the first identifier still needs reconciliation against a manufacturer catalog. The actual installed valve remains unidentified. Historical prices are not current buying guidance.

## Key Points

- The valve is removable from a separate crankcase-ventilation grommet.
- The procedure directs inspection of that grommet for deterioration.
- The ventilation diagram also depicts a clean-air closure hose, distinct from the PCV metering path.
- The evaporative connection is explicitly application-dependent; do not add it without confirming the installed configuration.
