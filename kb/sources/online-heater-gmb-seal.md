## Summary

The mechanical coolant seal separates the wet chamber from the shaft bearing. Its vapor escapes through a housing weep path; bearing damage can follow coolant intrusion.

This is general pump architecture; do not choose an applicable bearing row type or seal size from the generic drawing.
