## Summary

The spring-lock fitting combines a male spigot, two fuel-resistant O-rings, a circular cage and a garter spring. The female fitting sleeves over the seals; its flare passes behind the spring. A separate horseshoe clip provides redundant retention. Factory illustrations distinguish the cage on the male side from the flare on the female side.

The service procedure specifies a large black clip on the supply connection and a small gray clip on the return connection. It illustrates a tether and warns against incorrect clip placement. These documents establish construction and service identities; they do not provide a production dimensional drawing. The model's dimensions, clearances, spring winding count, seal compression and connection coordinates remain provisional.

## Key Points

- Two O-rings per fitting: 390846-S96 for the listed 3/8-inch size; 390847-S96 for the listed 1/2-inch size.
- Replacement garter springs: E1ZZ-19E576-A and E1ZZ-19E576-B respectively.
- Disconnect tools: D87L-9280-A and D87L-9280-B respectively. Tool size is not a measured bore or O-ring cross-section.
- Clips fit over the metal coupling. The depicted service tool is not a permanently installed coupling component.
