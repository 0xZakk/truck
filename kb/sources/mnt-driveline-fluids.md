## Summary

This source consolidates the manual-transmission and rear-axle lubricant specs for the 2WD 4.9L.
The 5-speed (Mazda) R1/R2 — the M5OD-R2 in Bronco, Econoline, and F-series applications — has a
refill capacity of 7.6 (quarts/pints per the FSM table units), and per TSB 06-14-4 the
recommended fluid is Mercon V Automatic Transmission Fluid for "all others." Older 4-speed boxes
specified 80W EP gear oil; the 5-speed uses ATF.

The integral-carrier 8.8-inch ring-gear rear axle holds approximately 5.50 US pints (4.58 imperial
pints / 2.60 L) for the conventional axle. Traction-Lok 8.8-inch axles also take ~5.5 US pints and
require an added friction modifier: use rear axle lubricant (ESP-M2C154-A) plus 4 ounces of
friction modifier C8AZ-19B546-A (EST-M2C118-A). Ford notes rear axle lube need only be replaced
every 160,000 km (100,000 miles) or if the axle is submerged. These figures ground `driveline`
service for both the gearbox and the differential.

## Key Points

- M5OD-R2 5-speed (Mazda R1/R2) refill: 7.6 (FSM table) for Bronco/Econoline/F-series.
- 5-speed "all others" fluid: Mercon V ATF (per TSB 06-14-4).
- 8.8-inch conventional rear axle capacity: ~5.50 US pints (4.58 imp pt / 2.60 L).
- 8.8-inch Traction-Lok: ~5.5 US pints plus 4 oz friction modifier (C8AZ-19B546-A).
- Axle lubricant: ESP-M2C154-A rear axle lubricant for non-Lightning, non-4.10 axles.
- Rear axle lube interval: replace every 100,000 miles or if submerged in water.
