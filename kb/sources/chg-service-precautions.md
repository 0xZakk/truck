## Summary

Before working on the 1994 F-150's starting or charging system, the factory service manual
requires disarming the air bag (supplemental restraint) system, because that work involves
disconnecting the battery. The procedure uses a Rotunda Air Bag Simulator in place of the
driver-side module so the harness sees a valid resistance while disconnected.

The manual also warns that disconnecting and reconnecting the battery wipes the Powertrain
Control Module's learned adaptive strategy, so the truck may drive abnormally until the PCM
relearns — typically after 10 or more miles of driving. It also reminds you to record radio
presets before pulling the battery.

## Key Points

- Record pre-set radio frequencies before disconnecting the battery.
- **Disarming:** disconnect battery negative then positive; **wait at least one minute** for
  the back-up power supply to deplete; remove the air bag module nuts, disconnect the driver
  air bag connector, connect Rotunda Air Bag Simulator 105-00010 to the harness, then
  reconnect the battery cables.
- **Arming:** reverse the process — disconnect battery (negative then positive), wait one
  minute, remove the simulator, reconnect the air bag module connector, install the module,
  reconnect the battery.
- After any battery disconnect/reconnect, the PCM relearns its adaptive strategy; abnormal
  drive conditions may persist until the truck is driven **10 or more miles**.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Wait at least one minute for back-up power supply to deplete its stored energy."

> "After the battery has been disconnected and reconnected, abnormal drive conditions may be
> present while the Powertrain Control Module (PCM) relearns its adaptive strategy. The
> vehicle may need to be driven 10 or more miles to relearn the strategy."
