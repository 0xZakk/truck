## Summary

The F-150 rear axle is suspended on semi-elliptic leaf springs. Each spring bolts to the axle
with U-bolts, anchors to a front bracket, and pivots at a rear shackle attached to the frame;
shock absorbers control rebound and a stabilizer bar limits body roll. The FSM gives the rear
suspension fastener torques and the stabilizer-bar removal sequence (link nut, washers and
insulators, link-to-frame bolts, then the bar's U-bolt/bracket/retainer nuts).

This source consolidates the rear suspension torque specifications and the stabilizer bar
service procedure for the `suspension` system.

## Key Points

- Leaf spring to axle U-bolt nut: 75-115 ft lb.
- Leaf spring to front bracket nut & bolt: 75-115 ft lb.
- Leaf spring to rear shackle: 75-115 ft lb; rear shackle to frame: 75-115 ft lb.
- Rear shock absorber: upper mount 40-60 ft lb, lower mount 52-74 ft lb.
- Jounce bumper to frame nut: 19-30 ft lb.
- Stabilizer bar to axle nut: 30-42 ft lb; stabilizer link to bar: 15-25 ft lb; link to frame: 40-60 ft lb.
- Stabilizer bar R&R: remove link lower nut, washers/insulators, link-to-frame bolts, then U-bolt/bracket/retainer nuts.
