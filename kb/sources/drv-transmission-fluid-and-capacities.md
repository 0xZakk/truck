## Summary

The M5OD 5-speed in the F-150 does not take a heavy gear oil — it is filled with automatic
transmission fluid. The FSM capacity table specifies fluid type by transmission variant and
gives refill volumes by vehicle line. For the F-series Mazda-built 5-speed, the refill volume
is notably larger than the Ranger/Aerostar figure because it shares the casting family but a
larger fill.

This is one of the more commonly needed maintenance facts for the `driveline` system, since
using the wrong lubricant (e.g., an 80W gear oil) in an M5OD that calls for ATF causes notchy,
hard shifting, especially when cold.

## Key Points

- **Fluid type, M5OD ("5-speed, All others"): Mercon V Automatic Transmission Fluid** (per TSB
  06-14-4), not a gear oil.
- The 4-speed and the Mitsubishi 5-speed (used in some Ranger/Bronco II) use **80W EP** gear oil
  — those do not apply to this F-150.
- **Refill capacity, 5-speed (Mazda), Bronco/Econoline/F-series: 7.6 pt.** (The Aerostar/Ranger/
  Bronco II/Explorer figure for the same family is 5.6 pt; the ZF S5-42 5-speed is 6.8 pt.)
- The separate "Fluid Type Specifications" page is provided only as a scanned image and adds no
  machine-readable detail beyond the capacity table.
