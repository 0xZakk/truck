## Summary

The Compressor Clutch Relay is described by the FSM as the Wide Open Throttle A/C (WOT A/C) relay.
Its purpose is to momentarily suspend air conditioning operation during hard acceleration. When
the driver goes to Wide Open Throttle (WOT), the WOT A/C relay opens, suspending A/C operation
until normal vehicle operation resumes.

The stated benefit is to free up engine power: cutting the compressor load under heavy
acceleration or load allows maximum power to reach the drive wheels. The relay is a powertrain-
management controlled device that ties HVAC operation to throttle state.

## Key Points

- The relay opens at Wide Open Throttle, momentarily disabling A/C compressor operation.
- The purpose is to deliver maximum power to the drive wheels under heavy acceleration or load.
- A/C resumes automatically once normal (non-WOT) operation resumes.
- This relay sits within the `electrical-body` accessory-control distribution.

## Notable Excerpts

> "When the vehicle operator goes to Wide Open Throttle (WOT), the Wide Open Throttle A/C (WOT A/C)
> relay opens, suspending A/C operation until normal vehicle operation resumes. This is done to
> allow maximum power to the drive wheels under heavy acceleration or load."
