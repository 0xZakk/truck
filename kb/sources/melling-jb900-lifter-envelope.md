## Summary

The public Melling specification search returns JB-900 as a steel hydraulic flat-tappet lifter, standard replacement, with2.000in/50.80mm length and0.874in maximum outside diameter. Its22.0mm diameter display is coarsely rounded;0.874in converts to22.1996mm. The response does not give pushrod-socket height or its measurement convention.
