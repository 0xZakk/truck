## Summary

PDF pages 6–7 (printed 1-02–1-03) were visually reviewed. The description places the distributor on the engine's left side and identifies the drive sequence from camshaft gear through distributor and intermediate shaft to the rotor oil pump. Figure 2 illustrates their relationship in a sectional engine drawing.

This is an industrial comparison reference, not dimensional identification of the 1994 EFI truck. The small drawing does not establish shaft length, mounting angle, tooth profile or mounting-boss dimensions. Its value here is architectural: the separate model studies must be reconciled as a continuous drive system, not joined arbitrarily at their existing display positions.

## Key Points

- Camshaft gearing drives the distributor.
- An intermediate shaft connects the distributor to the oil pump.
- Block, mounting neck and pump positions must be resolved together.
