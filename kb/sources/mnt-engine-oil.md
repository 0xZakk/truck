## Summary

The FSM gives the 4.9L engine oil refill as 5.0 quarts (4.7 L), with the note to add 1 quart
when changing the filter. The truck also has a low oil-level warning system: a float-type sensor
in the oil pan, an electronic control module, and an instrument-panel "Check Oil" lamp. The lamp
proves out in START, then determines from the sensor whether the level is low.

If oil is approximately 1.5 quarts or more low, the relay turns the warning lamp on and keeps it
on until the ignition is turned off. After shutoff, the module waits about five minutes before it
will read again — a deliberate delay allowing oil to drain back so the float reads accurately. The
bench test: with oil at FULL and warm, the lamp lights briefly in START then goes out; drain two
quarts, wait ~five minutes, restart, and the lamp should come on and stay on. This grounds `engine`
oil service and indicator diagnosis.

## Key Points

- Engine oil refill: 5.0 qts (4.7 L); add 1 qt with a filter change.
- Low oil-level system: float sensor in pan + control module + dash lamp.
- Lamp proves out in START; goes out in RUN if level is adequate.
- Warning triggers when oil is approximately 1.5 quarts or more low.
- Module waits ~5 minutes after shutoff before re-reading (allows drain-back).
- Test by draining 2 quarts, waiting 5 minutes, and restarting; lamp should stay on.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Engine Oil Refill 5.0 Qts.(4.7L) Note: Add 1 Qt. with filter change."

> "If oil level is approximately 1.5 quarts or more low, the relay turns the warning lamp ON... After ignition is turned OFF, module will not reset for approximately five minutes. This delay allows time for oil drain back before another reading is allowed to occur."
