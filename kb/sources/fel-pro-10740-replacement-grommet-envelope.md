## Summary

The applicable Fel-Pro catalog lists six 10740 pushrod-cover grommets. The retailer's replacement listing supplies a 0.48-inch thickness and diameter ranges around one inch outside and one-third inch inside. Those published replacement dimensions support an envelope study; they do not establish the installed grommet's condition, compression, hidden profile or material section.

The product photograph was inspected in the browser. It shows a black annular component with a raised central land and a recessed surrounding face. Only one face is visible. A lower locating neck in the CAD candidate is explicitly assumed, and a separate metal washer has not been added without evidence.

## Key Points

- Model the grommet separately from the bolt and cover so the sealing function is visible.
- Use the manufacturer catalog for application and quantity; label the retailer as the source of the dimensional envelope.
- Maintain an intact seating land around the cover hole. Its shape must remain coherent with the grommet rather than breaking through at the X-shaped pressed ribs.
- Free-state replacement dimensions do not determine tightened joint geometry.
