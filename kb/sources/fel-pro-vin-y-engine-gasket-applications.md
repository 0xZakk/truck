## Summary

Application 42 in the manufacturer catalog covers the gasoline MFI 4.9L VIN-Y engine for 1991–1997. It starts near the bottom of PDF page 353 and continues on page 354 (printed pages 327–328). The neighboring VIN-9 and bi-fuel VIN-Z applications must not be substituted for this truck. Both pages were rendered and visually reviewed after text extraction.

The catalog supplies replacement identities, material descriptions and selected quantities, not CAD dimensions. These identities provide specific search targets for the remaining seals and hardware. The detailed transcription is saved in `reference/engine/fel-pro-vin-y-gaskets-reviewed.json`.

## Key Points

- Pan set OS 34601 R contains a molded-rubber gasket. The pan row does not literally state one-piece; the nearby one-piece descriptions belong to rear-main seals. Dealer descriptions of Ford E7TZ6710B and the replacement provide a one-piece lead that still needs shape review. The older factory illustration's separate side/end pieces are not sufficient to establish the installed gasket construction.
- Pushrod-cover options include PS 13809 C and the older PS 13809 R. Six 10740 grommets are explicitly listed. Alternative service designs must not become duplicate installed parts.
- Intake and exhaust valve-seal applications differ for 1994: SS 72623 and SS 72839-1, respectively.
- Both listed timing-cover sets require the OS 34601 R pan gasket. This is an interface constraint, not an instruction to mix arbitrary end seals.
- The oil-pump gasket's description says “to block,” despite the catalog heading “Oil Pump Cover.” It must not be assigned to the internal pump-cover joint on the basis of that heading alone.
