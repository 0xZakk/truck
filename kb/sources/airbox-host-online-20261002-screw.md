## Summary

Printed page 102 (PDF page 2) identifies replacement 13019 with Ford N610959-S2. Nominal tapping diameter is 6.3 mm, pitch 1.81 mm and length 19 mm; the washer head is 11.5 mm across its circular envelope and the hex is 8 mm. This is a replacement comparison, not a complete OEM manufacturing drawing.

The source does not determine head height, point length, exact thread profile or receiving pilot/clip geometry. These dimensions may support an isolated hardware envelope; they cannot scale the entire photographed bracket or establish body-hole locations.
