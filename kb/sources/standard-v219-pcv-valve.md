## Summary

The manufacturer's live V219 product page and photograph were reviewed. It describes a push-on assembly made of plastic and metal, with two hose connectors and an angled nipple orientation. The photograph shows a metal cylindrical body and a black head with two unequal outlets perpendicular to the body axis. This exterior differs from the straight generic valve section in the factory explanation.

The specifications list an inlet diameter of 0.32 inch / 8.128 mm. That field does not establish the outside diameter of the metal body or either hose barb. The model-level buyers guide includes 1980–96 F-150s; engine-specific and installed-part applicability remain unverified. Do not treat an aftermarket product photograph as a production Ford dimensional drawing.
