## Summary

The 1994 F-150 uses a composite (aero) headlamp assembly with a replaceable halogen
bulb installed from the rear of the headlamp body. The headlamp assembly itself is
retained by two screws and two nuts at the headlamp door plus two upper retainers, and
its horizontal position is set with an adjusting screw that threads into an adjusting nut.
The halogen bulb is a serviceable item separate from the reflector/lens, handled only by
its plastic base because the pressurized glass envelope can shatter if scratched or
dropped, and because skin oils shorten its life.

The dash-mounted main light switch (headlamp switch) controls park and headlamps and is
removed by unsnapping the instrument-panel finish panel after extracting the knob and
shaft with a hooked tool. The headlamp high/low beam dimmer is part of the steering-column
multi-function switch and shares that switch's removal procedure. This component is part of
the truck inventory system `electrical-body`.

## Key Points

- Headlamp assembly: 2 screws + 2 nuts at the door, 2 upper retainers; horizontal aim screw threads into an adjusting nut and must be turned fully out to free the lamp.
- Headlamp assembly fastener torque: screws 1.4-2.3 N·m (13-20 lb-in); nuts 4-7 N·m (36-61 lb-in). Check aim after installation.
- Halogen bulb removed by rotating its retainer ~1/8 turn counterclockwise (viewed from rear), then pulling the bulb straight back without rotating it.
- Bulbs No. 9004 and No. 9007 look similar but are NOT interchangeable.
- A properly aimed headlamp normally does not need re-aiming after a bulb change.
- Headlamp switch knob/shaft is released by inserting a hooked tool into the knob slot to depress a spring; the finish panel has 5 retaining clips.

## Notable Excerpts

> "WARNING: THE HALOGEN HEADLAMP BULB CONTAINS GAS UNDER PRESSURE. THE HEADLAMP BULB MAY
> SHATTER IF THE GLASS ENVELOPE IS SCRATCHED OR THE HEADLAMP BULB IS DROPPED. ... GRASP
> HEADLAMP BULB ONLY BY ITS PLASTIC BASE. AVOID TOUCHING THE GLASS ENVELOPE."

> "CAUTION: Bulbs No.9004 and No.9007 look similar but are not interchangeable."

> "Tighten screws to 1.4-2.3 N.m (13-20 Lb-in). Tighten nuts to 4-7 N.m (36-61 Lb-In). Check headlamp aim."

> "A burned-out headlamp bulb should not be removed from the headlamp reflector until just
> before a replacement headlamp bulb is to be installed. Removal of a bulb for an extended
> period of time may allow contaminants (dust, moisture, smoke) to enter the headlamp body."
