## Summary

The FSM specifies bleeding the primary and secondary hydraulic systems separately, bleeding the longest line first on each. The reservoir must not run dry, and fluid drained from the system must never be reused. The master cylinder is bled first by loosening its line nuts and slowly depressing the pedal to force trapped air out at the fittings, holding the pedal down while the fittings are tightened (releasing the pedal before tightening lets air back in), repeating until the pedal is firm.

The wheel-by-wheel bleed sequence is RR, LR, ABS valve, RF, LF: pump the pedal, hold it down firmly, open a bleeder until the pedal fades, close it, and repeat until a continuous flow of fluid (no air) is released. The RABS valve is bled as part of the rear sequence. Afterward, fill the reservoir to 1/4 inch from the top (or the fill line), centralize the pressure differential valve via the ignition (ACC/ON, press pedal so the piston centers and the warning light goes out, ignition OFF), and road test for a firm pedal.

A special ABS bleed using the Antilock Brake Adapter (bleeder box) T90P-50-ALA and jumper cable T93T-50-ALA is required only if the Hydraulic Control Unit (HCU) has been replaced; otherwise conventional bleeding is used.

## Key Points

- Bleed primary and secondary systems separately; longest line first on each.
- Never let the reservoir run dry; never reuse drained fluid.
- Bleed the master cylinder first (loosen line nuts, slow pedal strokes, tighten with pedal held down).
- Wheel sequence: RR, LR, ABS valve, RF, LF.
- Final fill to 1/4 in from the top of the reservoir.
- Centralize the pressure differential valve (ignition ACC/ON, press pedal, light goes out, ignition OFF).
- Special HCU bleed (bleeder box T90P-50-ALA + jumper T93T-50-ALA) only if the HCU was replaced.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Bleed primary and secondary hydraulic brake systems separately, bleeding longest line first on each system. Do not allow reservoir to run dry during bleeding operation. Never reuse fluid that has been drained from hydraulic system."

> "Bleed brakes as follows: RR, LR, ABS valve, RF, LF"

> "Perform this procedure only if the Hydraulic Control Unit (HCU) has been replaced. Follow conventional bleed procedures if the HCU is not being replaced."
