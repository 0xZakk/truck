## Summary

The maintenance chapter of the same-generation Ford F-Series Owner Guide, covering engine
oil and engine coolant for the 4.9L. It specifies oil grade and certification, normal and
severe-service change intervals, the oil-and-filter change procedure, and the coolant
specification and service interval.

For the 4.9L specifically, **SAE 5W-30 is the preferred viscosity**, providing the best
performance, fuel economy, and protection down to -15 °F (-25 °C). Under normal service,
change the oil and filter **every 6 months or 5,000 miles**, whichever comes first; severe
service (heavy towing, extended idling, dusty conditions) tightens that interval. Coolant
service is **every 4 years or 50,000 miles**.

## Key Points

- Use Motorcraft oil (or equivalent) meeting Ford spec **ESE-M2C153-E** with the API
  certification mark. Never use non-detergent oils, oils labeled API SA–SG, or aftermarket
  oil additives/treatments.
- **5W-30 preferred** for the 4.9L for all climates down to -15 °F. Synthetic of the
  certified preferred viscosity is allowed but does not extend the change interval.
- Normal-service oil & filter change: **every 6 months or 5,000 miles (8,000 km)**, whichever first.
- Engine coolant service: **every 4 years or 50,000 miles (80,000 km)**.
- Coolant uses Ford Premium Cooling System Fluid (or equivalent); the guide gives a drain,
  flush, and refill procedure and warns never to open the recovery cap while hot.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Engine oils with an SAE 5W-30 viscosity are PREFERRED for your vehicle. They provide the
