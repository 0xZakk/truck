## Summary

The FSM gives the 4.9L spark plug gap as 0.042-0.046 in, plug type BSF-44C, tightened to
10-15 ft lb (14-20 Nm). The secondary ignition cables deliver coil high voltage to each plug
in firing order. They are built with a carbon-impregnated, multi-filament synthetic fiber core
surrounded by heat-resistant rubber insulation; built-in internal resistance suppresses radio
static and interference.

Together these define a basic tune-up on the `engine` system: gap and torque the plugs to spec,
and treat the cables as resistive suppression wires whose internal resistance is part of normal
construction (not a fault) when measuring them.

## Key Points

- Spark plug gap: 0.042-0.046 in.
- Plug type: BSF-44C.
- Spark plug tightening torque: 10-15 ft lb (14-20 Nm).
- Ignition cables: carbon-impregnated synthetic fiber core with heat-resistant rubber insulation.
- Cables have built-in internal resistance to suppress radio static and interference.
