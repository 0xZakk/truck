## Summary

The 4.9L lubrication system runs 40-60 PSI of oil pressure hot at 2000 rpm. Engine oil
refill is 5.0 qts (4.7 L), which already includes the extra quart added at filter change.
The oil pump is a gerotor (inner/outer rotor) pump whose internal parts are not serviceable
individually — if any measurement is out of spec, the whole pump is replaced.

Oil pump inspection covers the housing and rotors for wear or damage, the cover surface
(minor scuff marks are normal), rotor tip clearance with a feeler gauge, and rotor endplay
measured with a straightedge across the rotor and housing (must not exceed 0.005 in). The
relief valve and its spring are checked for free operation, collapse, and wear. Rebuild
clearances give rotor backlash 0.012 in, rotor-to-body 0.0010-0.0130 in, and relief-spring
pressure 20.6-22.6 lb at 2.49 in.

This source backs the `engine` inventory system.

## Key Points

- Oil pressure: 40-60 PSI hot at 2000 rpm.
- Oil refill: 5.0 qts (4.7 L), already counting the +1 qt for a filter change.
- Oil pump rotors are not individually serviceable — replace the pump if out of spec.
- Inspection: housing/rotors for wear, cover surface (minor scuffs OK), rotor tip clearance
  (feeler gauge), rotor endplay must not exceed 0.005 in.
- Relief valve checked for free operation; spring for collapse/wear.
- Rebuild clearances: rotor backlash 0.012 in; rotor-to-body 0.0010-0.0130 in;
  rotor endplay 0.0040 in; relief-spring pressure 20.6-22.6 lb @ 2.49 in.
- Oil pump retaining bolts: 10-12 ft-lb.
