## Summary

Actual PDF page529, printed 530, lists Bosch 15718→NTK 22503. The same Bosch number maps to several NTK alternatives, and the page says these are guide-only references. PDF 258, printed 259, illustrates 22503 and lists a 558 mm lead; it does not give mounting diameter or pitch. The NTK photo has different shell/protection-tube details from the identified Bosch specimen, so its shape and harness length are not transferred.

The manufacturer bridge is now established, but the reviewed primary catalog does not supply the required mounting-thread dimension. Bosch's linked 15729 universal product and installation sheet also omit it. Retail M18×1.5 remains a lead pending primary confirmation. Capture hashes and source locators are in `reference/engine/ho2s-dimensions-20261003-evidence.json`; originals are excluded.
