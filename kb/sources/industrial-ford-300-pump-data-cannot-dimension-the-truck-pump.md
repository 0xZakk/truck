## Summary

Foley's Tech Tip 221 distinguishes two industrial CSG649 water pumps and warns that an automotive pump is not interchangeable with the industrial installation. This is useful applicability evidence, not a dimensional source for this truck's 1994 automotive pump.

The page reports industrial casting identities F6JE-8510-BA and E9PZ-8501-B / E3TE-8505-CA. None has been established as the pump fitted to the owner's truck. Do not transfer the page's industrial flange, shaft or radiator-connection dimensions into the automotive model.

## Key Points

- The industrial manual may support shared engine internals without establishing automotive accessory geometry.
- The modeled pump hub height, pulley diameter and crank-to-pump center spacing remain provisional.
- A dimensioned automotive pump drawing or compatible part measurement is still needed.
