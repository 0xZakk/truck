## Summary

The manufacturer lists DS1125 for gasoline Ford F-150 4.9L six-cylinder applications from 1987 through 1994. Its side and underside photographs show one visible green O-ring on the larger offset stem, a separate smaller bare tube, a two-port gasket and a triangular three-hole mounting plate. This is applicable replacement comparison evidence, not proof of the owner's installed regulator identity.

The photographs corroborate the exact-year service underside illustration's O-ring location. Neither the photographs nor the reviewed product page supply seal ID, cross-section, groove dimensions, material or installed compression. Do not calibrate dimensions from the unscaled image or use the listed replacement pressure as a newly verified vehicle diagnostic specification.

## Key Points

One visible service seal does not establish all hidden internal seals. Two grooves in Ford's separate *typical* cutaway do not override the exact-year service illustration or establish two installed O-rings. Port identity, dimensions and mating rail geometry must be reconciled together before a seal candidate is accepted.
