## Summary

The factory service manual lists the electrical and mechanical wear specifications for the
1994 F-150's alternators. All units use an **internal (integral) voltage regulator**. The
electrical spec is the regulated set-point voltage; the mechanical specs are the rebuild
wear limits for slip rings and brushes, which vary with the alternator's amperage rating.

These numbers are what you measure a rebuild against: a regulator that holds the rated
volts, slip rings turned within diameter and runout limits, and brushes longer than the
wear limit. They span the family of alternators Ford fitted (40 through 130 A), so match
the spec block to the unit's current rating.

## Key Points

- **Regulated voltage:** alternators rated 40, 40HE, 60, 65, 75, and 95 amperes are spec'd
  at **15.0 volts** (the 130 A unit's volt value is not listed).
- **Regulator:** internal on all ratings.
- **40/40HE/60/65/75 A units** — slip-ring minimum turning diameter **1.22 in**, maximum
  runout **0.0005 in**; brush length new **0.480 in**, wear limit **0.25 in**.
- **95 A unit** — slip-ring minimum diameter **1.04 in**, maximum runout **0.0005 in**;
  brush length new **0.709 in**, wear limit **0.12 in**.
- **130 A unit** — internal regulator (no further mechanical figures listed).
