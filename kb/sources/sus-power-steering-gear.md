## Summary

The F-150 uses the Ford XR-50 integral power steering gear — a recirculating-ball,
torsion-bar, hydraulically assisted unit. It reduces steering effort and absorbs road shock.
Internally it pairs a worm shaft with a one-piece rack-piston whose teeth mesh with the
sector shaft; turning the wheel twists a torsion bar that operates a rotary hydraulic valve.

Because it uses a one-piece rotary valve and sleeve, there are no centering shims and no
valve centering adjustment. All hydraulic passages run internally between valve and cylinder,
so the only external plumbing is the pressure and return hoses to the pump. The power
cylinder is integral with the gear housing and the piston is double-acting (pressure can be
applied to either side). This source covers the gear's operation plus the only in-service
adjustment — sector/rack overcenter lash — for the `steering` system.

## Key Points

- Ford XR-50, integral, recirculating-ball + torsion-bar, hydraulic assist.
- Worm and one-piece rack-piston mesh with sector shaft; torsion bar drives a rotary valve.
- One-piece rotary valve/sleeve: NO centering shims, NO valve centering adjustment possible.
- Internal fluid passages; only external lines are pressure and return hoses to the pump.
- Power cylinder is integral with the housing; piston is double-acting.
- Only serviceable adjustment is sector-to-rack overcenter (meshload) lash.

## Notable Excerpts

> "The Ford XR-50 power steering unit is a torsion bar type of hydraulic-assisted system...
> The unit includes a worm and one piece rack-piston which is meshed to the gear teeth on the
> steering sector shaft."

> "This rotary valve type gear, uses a one piece rotary valve and sleeve assembly. Therefore no
> centering shims are used and no adjustment is possible."

Sector/rack overcenter lash adjustment (abbreviated): disconnect pitman arm; discharge gear
fluid; turn wheel 45 deg from right stop and measure torque to rotate the shaft ~1/8 turn;
return to center and measure torque across center; if meshload across center is not at least
7 in lb above the 45-deg reading, loosen the locknut and turn the adjusting screw until the
center reading is 10-14 in lb greater than the 45-deg reading; hold and tighten locknut.
