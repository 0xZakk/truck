## Summary

This source gathers the wheel-end and alignment specs. Lug-nut torque is 135 Nm (100 lb ft) for the
5-lug (1/2-20) wheel and 190 Nm (140 lb ft) for the 8-lug (9/16-18) wheel; Ford requires clean, dry,
oil-free threads and explicitly says never to use oil or grease on studs and nuts.

The front wheel bearings are an adjustable tapered set: snug the adjusting nut to 23-34 Nm
(17-25 ft lb) while rotating, then back it off about a half turn and retighten to 2.03-2.26 Nm
(18-20 in lb), targeting 0.006-0.127 mm (0.00025-0.005 in) end play and 1.13-2.82 Nm (10-25 in lb)
hub/rotor rotating torque. Front alignment optimum values are camber 0.25° ±0.5°, total toe 0.06°
±0.25°, with caster between 2.0° and 6.0°. These specs support `brakes`/front-end service: correct
bearing preload and lug torque are directly tied to rotor runout, pad wear, and safe wheel retention.

## Key Points

- 5-lug wheel (1/2-20) lug torque: 135 Nm (100 lb ft).
- 8-lug wheel (9/16-18) lug torque: 190 Nm (140 lb ft).
- Lug threads must be clean and dry — never use oil or grease.
- Wheel bearing: tighten adjusting nut to 23-34 Nm while rotating, back off ~1/2 turn, retighten to 2.03-2.26 Nm (18-20 in lb).
- Bearing end play target: 0.006-0.127 mm (0.00025-0.005 in).
- Hub/rotor rotating torque: 1.13-2.82 Nm (10-25 in lb).
- Alignment: camber 0.25° ±0.5°, total toe 0.06° ±0.25°, caster 2.0°-6.0°.
