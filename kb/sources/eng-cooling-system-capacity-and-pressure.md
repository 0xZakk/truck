## Summary

The cooling-system capacity for the manual-transmission 4.9L F-150 depends on accessory
content: 13 qts (US) without A/C, 14 qts with A/C, and 15 qts with A/C plus super cooling.
Coolant is a 50% mix of premium Ford coolant (spec ESE-M97B44-A) with water; the FSM warns
not to mix coolant types. The radiator cap holds a relief pressure of 13 psi.

Service work is simple. The thermostat is removed by draining the radiator, unbolting the
coolant outlet (elbow), and pulling the elbow rearward to free the thermostat and gasket;
installation reverses the steps. The FSM gives strong safety warnings around the cooling
system: never remove the radiator cap with the engine running, and on a hot system release
pressure by turning the cap slowly to its first stop using a thick cloth before removing it.

This source backs the `cooling` inventory system.

## Key Points

- Capacity (manual trans): 13 qts no A/C, 14 qts with A/C, 15 qts with A/C and super cooling.
- Coolant: 50% premium Ford coolant (ESE-M97B44-A) and water; do not mix coolant types.
- Radiator cap relief pressure: 13 psi.
- Thermostat service: drain radiator, remove coolant-outlet (elbow) bolts, pull elbow
  rearward to remove thermostat and gasket; reverse to install.
- Cap safety: never remove with engine running; on a hot system, wrap a cloth, turn to the
  first stop to vent pressure, then remove.
