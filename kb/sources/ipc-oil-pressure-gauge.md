## Summary

The oil pressure gauge uses the truck's shared three-coil magnetic gauge movement. The
sender's variable resistance steers the direction of the coils' magnetic field, and a
pointer-mounted magnet aligns to that field to indicate oil pressure. Like the other
magnetic gauges, it needs no adjustment, calibration, or maintenance and uses no IVR.

Diagnosis uses gauge tester tool No. 021-00055 (or equivalent) and the pinpoint tests. The
FSM splits the symptom paths into "Oil Pressure Gauge Inaccurate" and "Oil Pressure Gauge
Inoperative."

## Key Points

- Uses the shared three-coil magnetic movement (see Magnetic Gauge Movement source).
- Pointer position driven by oil pressure sender resistance.
- No IVR; no adjustment/calibration/maintenance.
- Diagnose with tester tool No. 021-00055 or equivalent, then the relevant pinpoint test.
- Two symptom branches: "Inaccurate" and "Inoperative."

## Notable Excerpts

> "A primary magnet which is attached to a shaft and pointer rotates to align to this primary
> field resulting in pointer position. ... These gauges require no adjustments, calibrations
> or maintenance. This gauge system does not use an IVR."

The Temperature Gauge component is documented identically (same magnetic movement, same
tester No. 021-00055, same Inaccurate / Inoperative pinpoint branches), so its diagnosis
follows this same procedure.
