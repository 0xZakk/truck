## Summary

The Interior Moulding / Trim section covers the cab's removable interior pieces: the full
instrument panel and its finish panel, the ash receptacle, the glove compartment and its lock,
and the floor/seat-mounted console. Most are simple snap-clip-and-screw assemblies that come
off after disconnecting the battery, while a full instrument panel replacement is a long
multi-step teardown reaching into the engine compartment, cowl side panels, and steering
column. These are `body-cab` / interior-trim components.

A recurring theme is that pulling the finish panel, ash tray, or full instrument panel means
disconnecting wiring connectors (headlamp switch, cigar lighter, gauge harness), and that any
battery disconnect leaves the PCM relearning its adaptive strategy for ~10 miles.

## Key Points

- Instrument panel finish panel: with tilt column full down, unsnap the right/left mouldings,
  pull the headlamp switch knob/shaft (depress the spring through the knob slot), remove two
  exposed screws, then unsnap five retaining clips (two at the steering column first, then
  three across the top) and disconnect its wiring.
- Full instrument panel replacement is a major job: disconnect engine-compartment connectors,
  remove the radio (Tool T87P-19061-A), windshield side garnish mouldings, four cowl-top
  screws, hood latch handle, both cowl side panels, panel brace bolts, and the parking-brake
  control before the panel comes free.
- Ash receptacle: one screw under the panel at the rear of the drawer retainer plus two at the
  front retainer; pull the receptacle rearward and disconnect the cigar lighter wire.
- Glove compartment lock: unsnap the four tabs on the inside latch cover, remove two latch
  screws.
- Floor console: remove the bolts at the base of the console panel and lift it out.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Unsnap five retaining clips by pulling the instrument panel finish panel rearward starting
> with the two at the steering column (one each side) and then the three at top..."

> "Remove radio using Tool T87P-19061-A." (instrument panel replacement)
