## Summary

The speed control switch (the steering-wheel driver control switch) signals the speed
control amplifier to activate or deactivate the system, to set and hold vehicle speed, to
accelerate, or to coast. These driver inputs (On/Off, Set-Acc, Coast, Resume) are the
operator's interface to the amplifier.

On manual-transmission trucks, the clutch switch deactivates the speed control system when
the clutch pedal is depressed. This is one of several inputs that drop the servo so the
engine isn't held at a commanded speed while the clutch is disengaged. (The same physical
clutch switch family also serves as the starter interlock on manual trucks; see related
notes.)

## Key Points

- Speed control switch purpose: signals the amplifier to activate/deactivate, set and hold,
  accelerate, or coast.
- The driver control switches are mounted in the steering wheel (hence the air-bag disarm
  service precaution before steering-wheel work).
- Clutch switch purpose: deactivates speed control when the clutch pedal is depressed
  (manual transmission only).
