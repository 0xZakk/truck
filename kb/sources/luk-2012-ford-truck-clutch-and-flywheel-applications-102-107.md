## Summary

The manufacturer catalog lists LFW132 for the 1975–1996 4.9L six-cylinder Bronco/F100–450 family (printed page 103, PDF page 105). This establishes an applicable replacement family; it does not establish the owner's installed part or its dimensions.

Printed page 100 (PDF page 102) lists 07-128 for the 1993–1994 five-speed 11-inch clutch, and 07-098 for a 1994 10-inch clutch. Both show a 1 1/16-inch spline diameter and ten spline teeth. The truck's clutch diameter therefore remains unresolved. The catalog notes include release-bearing compatibility and upgrade-specific hardware; those must not be applied indiscriminately to the installed truck.

## Key Points

- PDF pages 102 and 105 were rendered and visually reviewed; extracted fractional characters are corrupted, so dimensions were read from the page image.
- LFW112 was an initial search lead, not the confirmed 4.9L application. Do not use it as the truck flywheel reference.
- No verified flywheel bolt circle, ring-gear diameter, tooth count or thickness is established by these application pages.
