## Summary

The 1994 F-150 uses a dual master cylinder (2140) with a plastic see-through fluid reservoir, primary and secondary hydraulic pistons, a Fluid Level Indicator (FLI), and an integral proportioning valve. The rear wheel brakes connect to the secondary outlet port and are actuated by the secondary piston; the front wheel brakes connect to the primary outlet port (nearest the dash panel) and are actuated by the primary piston. Both pistons function together in normal braking, but the split design means a failure in one circuit still leaves the other operative.

The master cylinder reservoir carries a Low Fluid Level Warning Switch consisting of a float-and-magnet assembly and a reed switch, serviced only as part of the reservoir. When a low fluid level occurs, the switch provides a path to ground through the dual brake warning switch, which both disables the anti-lock brake system and illuminates the ABS indicator. Low fluid also illuminates the red BRAKE indicator.

Certain conditions are normal and not faults: the reservoir level rises on application and falls on release (net level unchanged); a trace of brake fluid on the booster shell below the mounting flange (normal bore/seal lubrication); and a gradual fall in level as disc pads wear.

## Key Points

- Dual master cylinder: primary piston → front circuit (port nearest dash); secondary piston → rear circuit.
- Integral proportioning valve regulates rear-circuit pressure.
- Reservoir is plastic see-through with Fluid Level Indicator (FLI).
- Low fluid level switch = float + magnet + reed switch; integral to reservoir, not separately serviceable.
- Low fluid grounds through dual brake warning switch → disables ABS and lights ABS indicator; also lights red BRAKE lamp.
- Correct fill: between the MAX line and 4 mm (0.16 in) below MAX; use DOT 3 / Ford C6AZ-19542-AA.
- Normal (not a fault): level rises on apply / falls on release; trace fluid on booster shell; level drop as pads wear.
- Frequent top-ups indicate a leak — check all hydraulic connections.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Rear wheel brakes - are connected to the secondary outlet port... Front wheel brakes - are connected to the primary outlet port (nearest the dash panel)."

> "When a low fluid level condition occurs, a path to ground is provided through the dual brake warning switch to disable the Antilock brake system and illuminate the Antilock brake indicator."

> "Brake fluid level in the master cylinder reservoir should be between 4mm (0.16 inch) below the MAX line on the side of the reservoir and the MAX line."
