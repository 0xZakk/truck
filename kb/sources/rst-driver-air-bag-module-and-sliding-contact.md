## Summary

The driver-side air bag module is mounted in the center of the steering wheel and is serviced only as a
complete assembly — the inflator, air bag, mounting plate/retaining ring, and trim cover are all
non-serviceable components of it. When the sensors close signaling a crash, current reaches the inflator;
an igniter converts the electrical signal to heat, igniting the sodium azide / copper oxide gas generant,
which combusts to produce nitrogen gas that inflates the bag. The bag is neoprene-coated nylon, 711 mm
(28 in) in diameter, and fills to about 0.065 cu m (2.3 cu ft) in roughly 40 milliseconds. The mounting
plate and retaining ring attach and seal the bag to the inflator and mount the module to the wheel; the
injection-molded plastic trim cover has molded tear seams that split open to let the bag inflate.

The air bag sliding contact (clockspring / spiral cable) is mounted on the steering column behind the
steering wheel. It continuously transfers the SRS electrical signals between the column wire harness and
the contact in the steering wheel as the wheel rotates, keeping the deployment circuit to the module
connected through full steering travel. The column itself is an energy-absorbing type with two breakaway
mounting capsules and a bendstrap that allow controlled downward movement of the column and wheel on impact.

Torque specs: air bag module-to-steering wheel nuts 4-6 Nm (36-53 in lbs); air bag sliding contact screws
2-3 Nm (18-28 in lbs).

## Key Points

- Driver air bag module is serviced as a complete assembly; inflator, bag, plate, ring, and trim cover are non-serviceable.
- Inflator igniter ignites sodium azide / copper oxide generant, producing nitrogen gas.
- Bag: neoprene-coated nylon, 711 mm (28 in) diameter, ~0.065 cu m (2.3 cu ft) filled in ~40 ms.
- Trim cover has molded tear seams that split to allow inflation.
- Air bag sliding contact (clockspring) relays SRS signals between the rotating wheel and the column harness.
- Energy-absorbing column: two breakaway capsules and a bendstrap for controlled collapse.
- Module-to-wheel nuts: 4-6 Nm (36-53 in lbs). Sliding contact screws: 2-3 Nm (18-28 in lbs).
