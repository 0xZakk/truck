## Summary

TM10-3930-633-24P, May1993, covers the Clark MHE-228 warehouse tractor. PDF page36 (Figure13) and page37 (printed13-1) identify the C5AZ-6600-A oil-pump assembly and separate attachment groups. This is an identified industrial comparison, not proof of every1994 truck revision.

The exploded view shows a raised mounting flange, independent side inlet, cover and rotor set. The table lists two mounting bolts, four cover screws and two inlet screws, each with lockwashers. A separate pump-body pin is drawn as an annular piece; its dimensions and hydraulic function are not established. The pickup and gasket revision must remain distinct from truck-specific parts.
