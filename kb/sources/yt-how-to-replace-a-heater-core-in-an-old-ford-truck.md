## Summary

A short, hands-on walkthrough of replacing the heater core in an OBS Ford F-150 (the
narrator's is a 1992, but the procedure covers 1980–1996 trucks, including the 1994). The
point of the video is that the job is far less involved than its reputation suggests: on
these trucks the heater core lives in a plastic housing accessible from the passenger
footwell, so the whole dash does not have to come out.

The sequence is straightforward. The two heater hoses are worked off their tubes at the
firewall with pliers. Inside the cab, the glove box drops down (the hinge is a simple hook),
a control/blend module is unbolted (mostly **7 mm**), and the perimeter screws holding the
heater-core cover are removed (mostly **5/16"**, reachable with a ratchet and extension; one
hidden screw behind a cable needs a stubby/ratcheting driver). After releasing a wire and a
cable hook, the cover comes off and the heater core simply slides down and out.

The narrator notes the new core is dramatically lighter and "sounds thinner" than the
original, but it carries a lifetime warranty. He cleans out the disintegrated foam/rubber
seal from the cavity, transfers the foam, fits the new core and cover, reinstalls screws and
modules, pushes the hoses back on, then refills and **bleeds the cooling system** before
confirming hot air (and no steam) at the vents.

## Key Points

- On a 1980–1996 F-150 the heater core is reached from the **passenger footwell behind the
  glove box** — no full dash removal required.
- Disconnect the **two heater hoses** at the firewall first (pliers; try not to tear the
  rubber).
- Drop the **glove box** (hinge is a simple hook) to access the heater-core cover.
- A blend/control **module** unbolts with a **7 mm** socket; the cover **perimeter screws are
  5/16"**, with one hidden screw behind a cable.
- The heater core **slides straight down and out** once the cover, a wire, and a cable hook
  are released.
- Clean the **disintegrated foam/rubber sealing** from the housing cavity and transfer/replace
  the foam before fitting the new core.
- After reinstalling, **refill and bleed the coolant**, then verify warm air with no steam at
  the vents.
