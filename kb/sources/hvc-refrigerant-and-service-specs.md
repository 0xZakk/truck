## Summary

The 1994 F-150 (inventory id `hvac`) uses an R-134a air-conditioning system — one of the
early non-CFC systems — with PAG refrigerant oil. The FSM gives the refrigerant charge,
system oil capacity, fluid types, and the design of the R-134a service-port valves used to
connect gauges and recovery equipment.

This source consolidates the Specifications pages for Refrigerant (capacity and fluid type),
Refrigerant Oil (capacity and fluid type), and the Service Port HVAC Description/Operation
page.

## Key Points

- Refrigerant: R-134a, system capacity 2.0 lbs.
- Refrigerant oil: PAG (Motorcraft YN-12b or equivalent), system capacity 7.0 oz.
- R-134a service-port valves are a one-piece design — aluminum top threaded into a steel body
  on the suction accumulator/drier or refrigerant line, sealed with an O-ring.
- The high-side fitting is the larger of the two valves; both require special adapters.
- Service tools: high-side A/C fitting socket D94L-19703-A and low-side D94L-19703-B.
