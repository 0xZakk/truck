## Summary

The archived CSG649 industrial service manual provides connecting-rod and piston
dimensions on PDF page 100, printed page 8-03. The page was rendered and visually
inspected. Its industrial application and older specification set prevent treating
it as a verified drawing for the installed 1994 EFI engine.

The printed connecting-rod center-distance range supports the approximate length
used in the mechanism study. It does not establish the installed rod's identity,
forging outline or pin offset. Several entries on the page are internally suspect,
so they must not be normalized silently into plausible numbers.

## Key Points

- Rod center distance is printed as 6.2082–6.2112 inches.
- Piston pin length is printed as 3.150–3.170 inches.
- The pin-bushing entry conflicts with the piston-pin dimensions on this page.
- Applicability to the truck requires a separate cross-check.
