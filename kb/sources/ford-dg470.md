## Summary

Ford identifies DG470 as F7PZ12029AA, matching the coil service part in the archived 1994 4.9L manual. Manufacturer photographs show the exposed rectangular lamination frame, rounded molded case, four mounting holes, projecting high-voltage terminal and primary connector shroud.

The page confirms laminated steel and internal insulation, but does not provide a dimensional drawing or internal winding breakdown. The coil CAD study therefore retains provisional dimensions and explicitly illustrative winding packs. It does not establish the identity of the coil currently installed in the owner's truck.

## Key Points

- Cross-reference: DG470 / F7PZ12029AA.
- Exterior views: Ford product images 94421 and 94422.
- Bracket and radio interference capacitor are separately identified by the archived service procedure.
- Winding and lamination counts are unresolved; aggregate CAD volumes are not individual physical wire turns or sheets.
