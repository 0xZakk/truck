## Summary

This page consolidates the system-level diagnostic content for the 1994 F-150
wiper/washer system: the bench Park Test for the motor's park switch, the index of
symptom-related pinpoint tests (A through E), and the air bag service precautions
that apply whenever the steering column / multi-function switch is serviced.

The Park Test verifies that the wiper motor cycles once and stops in the Park
position when its park switch is properly fed. The FSM's symptom-related diagnostic
index routes the technician to one of five pinpoint test trees by symptom; each tree
is presented as a flowchart figure in the manual. The air bag precautions are
critical because the wiper/washer multi-function switch sits on the air-bag-equipped
steering column.

## Key Points

- Park Test: jumper the motor low-speed terminal to the park switch return terminal,
  ground both motor and park-switch ground terminals, and feed battery positive to
  the park feed terminal — the motor should cycle once then stop in Park.
- Symptom-based pinpoint tests in the FSM:
  - A: Windshield washer / wipers inoperative in all control positions (A1–A3)
  - B: Wipers inoperative or erratic on LOW, high speed works (B1–B7)
  - C: Wipers inoperative at INT, low & high speed OK (C1–C4)
  - D: Wipers continue to run when switch is turned OFF (D1–D4)
  - E: No wipe after wash, interval & low speed OK (E1–E2)
- Air bag DISARMING before column work: disconnect battery negative then positive,
  wait at least one minute for the back-up power supply to deplete, remove the air
  bag module, disconnect the driver air bag connector, and install an air bag
  simulator before reconnecting the battery.
- After reconnecting the battery the PCM must relearn its adaptive strategy; the
  truck may need 10+ miles of driving for normal drive feel to return.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Connect jumper lead from low speed terminal of windshield wiper motor connector
> to park return terminal of park switch connector... Windshield wiper motor should
> cycle once, then stop in Park position."

> "Wait at least one minute for back-up power supply to deplete its stored energy...
> Connect Rotunda Air Bag Simulator 105-00010, or equivalent, to vehicle harness at
> top of steering wheel."

> "After the battery has been disconnected and reconnected, abnormal drive conditions
> may be present while the Powertrain Control Module (PCM) relearns its adaptive
> strategy. The vehicle may need to be driven 10 or more miles."
