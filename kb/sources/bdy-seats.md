## Summary

The Seats section covers removal/installation of the standard and split bench seats, the seat
tracks, the seat-back frame, and the vacuum/pressure seat-back pad (lumbar) adjuster system.
Bench seats bolt to the floor pan through the seat tracks; the split bench adds a removable
center seat and folding seat backs with a safety-belt bracket. The FSM specifies sealing the
track-to-floor fastener locations with caulking cord on installation and gives torque values
for the track-to-floor and seat-to-seat-back-frame fasteners.

The optional power lumbar uses a vacuum/pressure pump that inflates a seat-back pad adjuster
through a hose; both the pump and the bladder-style adjuster are serviceable. These are
`body-cab` components.

## Key Points

- Standard/split bench removal: disconnect wiring if equipped, remove the screws and nuts
  (one of each per side) retaining the seat track to the floor pan, then lift the seat-and-
  track assembly out.
- On installation, apply Caulking Cord D6AZ-19560-A (or equivalent) around the track
  attaching screw/stud locations on the floor pan (water sealing).
- Seat track-to-floor-pan screws: 34-46 N-m (25-34 ft-lb).
- Split bench front seat-to-seat-back-frame nuts: 18-27 N-m (13-27 ft-lb).
- Seat Track service-and-repair page: track-to-seat-cushion-frame screws torque to
  17-23 N-m (13-17 ft-lb); handle the seat carefully — dropping or sitting on an unsecured
  seat can damage components.
- Seat-back pad adjuster (lumbar bladder) is retained by bend-over tabs on the seat-back frame
  and fed by an adjusting hose; removing it requires taking off the seat-back cover.
- The seat-back pad adjusting pump connects via an adjusting hose and an electrical connector
  and is reached by removing the seat, seat tracks, and seat-cushion cover.
