## Summary

The rear drum brakes are self-adjusting: they automatically take up clearance when the vehicle is driven in reverse and the brakes are applied sharply, then driven forward. This may need to be repeated several times to reach proper adjustment. Manual adjustment is only required after the shoes/linings have been relined or replaced, or if the adjuster (brake shoe adjusting lever kit, 2A176) has malfunctioned and been repaired or replaced.

The manual procedure (drum installed): place the transmission in neutral, raise and support the vehicle, remove the cover from the adjusting hole at the bottom of the backing plate, and turn the adjusting screw (part of brake shoe adjusting screw nut, 2047) with Brake Adjusting Tool D81L-1103-C until the shoes drag and lock the drum. Then back the adjusting screw off an additional 10 to 12 notches so the drum rotates freely without drag. If the drum still won't rotate freely, remove the wheel/drum and vacuum out lining dust, remove rust at shoe-to-backing-plate contact points, and lightly lubricate the backing plate contact pads (Disc Brake Caliper Slide Grease D7AZ-19590-A) without getting lubricant on the lining. Reinstall, adjust the other side, then apply the brakes — if the pedal travels more than halfway to the floor, clearance is excessive and the expand/back-off steps are repeated. Road test only when the brakes will apply safely. When adjusting the shoes, also check the rear parking-brake cable adjustment and that the equalizer operates freely.

## Key Points

- Self-adjust by reversing and braking sharply, then driving forward (repeat as needed).
- Manual adjustment needed only after reline/replace or adjuster failure.
- Expand the adjusting screw (2047) until the shoes lock the drum, then back off 10-12 notches for free rotation.
- Tools: Brake Adjusting Tool D81L-1103-C; Brake Adjustment Gauge D81L-1103-A (drum removed).
- Lubricate backing-plate contact pads with D7AZ-19590-A; keep lubricant off the lining.
- Pedal traveling more than halfway to the floor = too much shoe-to-drum clearance.
- Use vacuum (not compressed air/brushes) to remove brake dust; use non-asbestos parts.
- Also check parking-brake cable adjustment and free equalizer movement.

## Notable Excerpts

> "The brake drums are automatically adjusted while driving the vehicle in reverse and sharply applying the brakes and then driving forward. It may be necessary to do this several times to obtain the proper rear brake adjustment."

> "When the shoes are against the brake drum, loosen the adjusting screw... an additional 10 to 12 notches so that the brake drum rotates freely without drag."

> "Apply the brakes. If the pedal travels more than halfway to the floor, there is too much clearance between the brake shoes and the brake drums."
