## Summary

The 4.9L uses hydraulic lifters, so "valve clearance" is really a lifter collapsed-gap
check, not a feeler-gauge lash adjustment with shims. With the rocker bolts torqued and the
piston at TDC compression, the tappet is bled down with a special bottoming tool and the gap
between rocker and valve tip is measured. Desirable clearance is 0.125-0.175 in (allowable
0.100-0.200 in). If the gap is wrong, you change pushrod length — a shorter pushrod if the
gap is too small, a longer one if too large — working through the firing order 1-5-3-6-2-4,
one-third damper turn at a time.

Idle speed is not set by a simple stop screw. Curb and fast idle are controlled by the PCM
and the non-adjustable Idle Air Control-Bypass Air (IAC-BPA) valve. The throttle body uses
a sludge-tolerant design with an orifice in the throttle plate; its plate-stop screw must
never be backed off counterclockwise (it won't lower rpm and can make the plate stick), and
the bore must not be cleaned. Setting base idle requires a scan tool and the engine-running
Self-Test, after first ruling out vacuum leaks, wrong timing, PCV faults, and fuel-control
problems.

## Key Points

- 4.9L has hydraulic lifters; clearance is a collapsed-gap check, adjusted via pushrod length.
- Desirable clearance 0.125-0.175 in; allowable 0.100-0.200 in.
- Procedure: TDC compression per cylinder, torque rocker bolts, bleed tappet down with
  bottoming tool, measure rocker-to-tip gap; short pushrod if tight, long if loose.
- Work in firing order 1-5-3-6-2-4, one-third crank turn at a time.
- Idle is PCM/IAC-BPA controlled; the IAC-BPA valve is not adjustable.
- Sludge-tolerant throttle body: do not back the plate-stop screw out CCW, do not clean the bore.
- Setting base idle requires a scan tool running the engine Self-Test after eliminating
  vacuum leaks, timing, PCV, and fuel-control faults.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Slowly supply pressure to bleed down tappet until plunger is completely bottomed against
> tappet bleed down wrench tool ... If clearance is less than specifications, install shorter
> pushrod. If clearance is greater than specifications, install longer pushrod."

> "Allowable limits 0.100 - 0.200 in. Desireable limits 0.125 - 0.175 in."

> "The curb and fast idle speeds are controlled by the Powertrain Control Module (PCM) and the
> Idle Air Control-Bypass Air (IAC-BPA) valve. The IAC-BPA valve is not adjustable."
