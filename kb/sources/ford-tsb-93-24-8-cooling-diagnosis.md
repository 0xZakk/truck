## Summary

Ford's November 24, 1993 bulletin explicitly includes 1994 F-Series trucks. It explains that a thermostat stuck open or opening too early can contribute to poor heater output, rough idle, hesitation and poor fuel economy.

The bulletin diagnoses failure to reach normal operating temperature. It expressly excludes overheating diagnosis. Its test observes ECT voltage while retaining the sensor connection to the PCM; the full procedure and equipment requirements remain in the archived manual.

## Key Points

- Symptoms alone do not establish a failed thermostat.
- ECT trend is the diagnostic observation, not a substitute for checking coolant level and the prescribed test conditions.
- Disconnecting the PCM can erase learned adaptations and temporarily obscure a driveability complaint.
- The archived text contains a malformed temperature value; do not use it as a verified numerical specification.
