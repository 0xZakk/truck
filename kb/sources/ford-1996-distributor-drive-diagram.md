## Summary

The original scan of Ford service-manual page 03-07A-6 was visually reviewed. Its exploded diagram identifies the gear and roll pin separately, and calls out a thrust washer for the 4.9L engine. This supports separating the drive components in the model rather than incorporating them into the shaft mesh.

This is a 1996 comparison reference. It does not identify the installed 1994 distributor, supply manufacturing dimensions or verify the modeled tooth count/profile. The site's generated caption was not used as the authority; the original photographed page was inspected. The scan also covers other engines, whose collar details must not be silently assigned to the 4.9L.

## Key Points

- Item 9 identifies the 4.9L thrust washer.
- Item 10 identifies the roll pin; item 11 identifies the gear.
- The model's dimensions and gear mesh remain unresolved.
