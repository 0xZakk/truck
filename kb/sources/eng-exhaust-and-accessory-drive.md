## Summary

The exhaust uses a dual catalytic converter that combines a Three-Way Catalytic Converter
(TWC) with an Oxidation Catalyst (OC). Beyond oxidizing HC and CO, the TWC also reduces NOx,
working with the Secondary Air Injection (AIR) system. The catalyst is a ceramic substrate
coated with precious metals.

Two routine accessory items round out this source. The accessory drive belt runs on an
automatic tensioner, so no belt adjustment is required; instead you read the wear indicator
marks on the tensioner, and if the indicator falls outside its range you replace the belt
(a worn or wrong-length belt is what causes improper tension). The air filter element is a
chemically-treated pleated-paper element that filters dust, dirt, and debris from intake air.

This source backs the `intake-exhaust` and `engine` inventory systems.

## Key Points

- Dual catalytic converter = TWC + OC; oxidizes HC/CO and reduces NOx with the AIR system.
- Catalyst is precious-metal-coated ceramic substrate.
- Drive belt uses an automatic tensioner — no adjustment; check the tensioner indicator
  marks and replace the belt if the indicator is outside its range.
- Air filter element is a chemically-treated pleated-paper element.
