## Summary

This scanned industrial parts book gives exploded component views and service
quantities for older Ford 300 applications. It is a comparison source, not the
1994 truck's bill of materials. PDF pages 7, 9, 11 and 13 were rendered and visually
inspected. The drawings show details omitted from ordinary removal procedures,
including the cam thrust assembly, crank keys and individual bearing shells.

The text extractor produced no text for pages 7–14. Any claims must therefore be
based on the rendered page, not on an empty or assumed OCR transcription. The
industrial accessory drive, carburetion and earlier component revisions must not
be copied into the EFI truck assembly.

## Key Points

- PDF page 9 lists fourteen head bolts and two head locating dowels for its application.
- PDF page 13 lists four cam bearings and a 92-tooth industrial cam gear.
- These quantities and dimensions require application checks before transfer.
- The current educational timing gears remain explicitly illustrative.
