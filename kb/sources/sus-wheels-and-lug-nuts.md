## Summary

The F-150's road wheels are retained by lug nuts that the FSM specifies by thread size and
torque. The 5-lug wheel used on the 2WD half-ton uses a 1/2-20 stud torqued to 135 Nm
(100 ft lb); heavier 8-lug applications use a 9/16-18 stud at 190 Nm (140 ft lb). The manual
is explicit that the torque applies only to clean, dry, paint- and dirt-free threads and that
oil or grease must never be used on studs or nuts (which would over-tension the stud at a
given torque). This serves the `suspension` / wheels content.

## Key Points

- 5-lug wheel (2WD F-150): 1/2-20 lug, torque 135 Nm (100 ft lb).
- 8-lug wheel: 9/16-18 lug, torque 190 Nm (140 ft lb).
- Torque values assume clean, dry, dirt- and paint-free threads.
- Never use oil or grease on studs and nuts.
