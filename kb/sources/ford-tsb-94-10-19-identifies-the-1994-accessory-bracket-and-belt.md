## Summary

Ford's May 18, 1994 bulletin distinguishes the 1994 shared power-steering/A/C carrier from the earlier casting. Figure 4 depicts the tensioner on that same carrier. The supported engine attachment topology is a front-head bolt, a side-head bolt and two block-side nuts on studs; the older bracket's additional lower bolts do not carry over. These are architectural constraints, not dimensioned hole coordinates.

The belt table selects E8TZ-8620-T / Motorcraft JK6-984-B for the truck's 1994 F-Series, with-A/C and below-8500-GVW configuration. The 76 mm tensioner-pulley replacement procedure elsewhere in the bulletin applies specifically to Econoline with A/C and must not be applied to the F-150 model.

## Key Points

- The tensioner belongs on the shared carrier; an independent engine bracket is unsupported.
- Figure 4 supplies distinct 1994 fastener topology, and Figure 3 is the older comparison.
- The power-steering pulley hub should be flush with the pump shaft end.
- The bulletin does not provide pulley center coordinates, water-pump diameter or effective pitch radii.
