## Summary

On the 1994 F-150 the wiper switch and the windshield washer switch are the same
part: a single multi-function switch mounted on the steering column. The FSM lists
identical testing and service entries under both the "Wiper Switch" and "Windshield
Washer Switch" headings, both pointing at the multi-function switch component
testing procedure, terminal identification, and schematic.

Removal requires taking off the steering column shroud, removing the switch-to-
column attaching screws, disengaging the switch, and disconnecting its electrical
connectors. On tilt-column trucks the tilt lever must be unscrewed first, and care
must be taken not to damage the PRNDL (gear indicator) cable. The attaching screws
are torqued to 18–27 inch-lbs on reinstallation.

## Key Points

- The wiper switch and washer switch are one multi-function switch on the steering
  column.
- Testing is done via the multi-function switch component testing procedure with
  terminal identification and schematic (figure-based in the FSM).
- Removal: remove column shroud, remove switch attaching screws, disengage switch,
  disconnect connectors.
- Tilt-column models: unscrew and remove the tilt lever first.
- Do not damage the PRNDL cable during removal.
- Torque switch attaching screws to 18–27 inch-lbs.
