## Summary

EngineQuest identifies FSP300N for the Ford 4.9L 300 in truck and industrial applications through 1996. Its OEM interchange includes E7TZ-6519-A and the older C5AZ-6519-B appearing in the Ford industrial parts book. This strengthens the applicability of the older architectural reference without proving the owner's installed part identity.

The 2025 manufacturer catalog, PDF page 18, was rendered and visually reviewed. The Ford cover has six holes along its center row, repeated X-shaped stamped features, rounded corners and a continuous perimeter flange. The adjacent four-hole Chevrolet cover is a different part. The catalog does not supply the Ford cover's dimensions or hole stations.

## Key Points

- Use the actual six-hole Ford photograph for exterior construction.
- Retain a separate gasket and six grommets, cross-checking the applicable Fel-Pro catalog.
- Build a matching block opening and mounting bosses; an external cover alone does not provide access to the pushrod cavity.
- Older catalog dimensions found in search remain leads: the underlying scan returned 403 and has not been visually reviewed.
