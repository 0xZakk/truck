## Summary

The 4.9L (300 cu in) inline-six in the 1994 F-150 is a fuel-injected engine with a
4.00 x 3.98 in bore and stroke and an 8.8:1 compression ratio. Output depends on rear
axle ratio: 150 hp @ 3400 rpm and 260 ft-lb @ 2000 rpm in most trucks, dropping slightly
to 145 hp / 265 ft-lb with the 2.73 or 3.08 axle. Normal oil pressure with the engine
warm and running at 2000 rpm is 40-60 PSI.

The FSM consolidates fastener torque values and engine rebuilding clearances for this
engine. These cover the long block (cylinder head procedure is referenced separately,
main caps 60-70 ft-lb, rod caps 40-45 ft-lb), the rotating assembly, the cooling and
lubrication accessories, and the front-of-engine accessory brackets. Rebuilding specs
give wear limits and clearances for the head, valvetrain, camshaft, crankshaft, pistons,
rings, and oil pump.

This source backs the `engine` inventory system and supports head, valvetrain, block,
and accessory work on the 4.9L.

## Key Points

- Engine: 4.9L I6, bore/stroke 4.00 x 3.98 in, compression ratio 8.8:1, fuel injected.
- Output: 150 hp @ 3400 / 260 ft-lb @ 2000 (145/265 with 2.73 or 3.08 axle).
- Normal oil pressure: 40-60 PSI, warm, at 2000 rpm.
- Key torques: main bearing cap 60-70 ft-lb; connecting rod cap 40-45 ft-lb;
  flywheel-to-crankshaft 75-85 ft-lb; damper/pulley 130-150 ft-lb; intake manifold
  22-32 ft-lb; exhaust manifold 22-32 ft-lb; oil pan 15-18 ft-lb; oil drain plug
  15-25 ft-lb; spark plug 10-15 ft-lb; rocker cover 6-9 ft-lb; rocker shaft support
  17-23 ft-lb; water pump bolts 12-18 ft-lb; oil pump retaining bolts 10-12 ft-lb;
  engine-to-transmission bolts 40-50 ft-lb.
- Cylinder head torque: refer to the Cylinder Head tightening procedure (not a single value).
- Key rebuild clearances: main bearing 0.0008-0.0015 in; rod bearing 0.0008-0.0015 in;
  piston 0.0010-0.0018 in; camshaft bearing 0.0010-0.0030 in; cylinder head warpage limit
  0.007 in; valve seat angle 45 degrees; valve stem-to-guide clearance 0.0010-0.0027 in.
