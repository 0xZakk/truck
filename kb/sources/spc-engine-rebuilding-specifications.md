## Summary

This page is the factory overhaul ("rebuilding") specification table for the 4.9L (300
cu in) inline-six — the dimensional and clearance limits a machinist or engine builder
works to when the engine (inventory id `engine`) is torn down. Unlike the general engine
spec page (bore, stroke, compression ratio, power), this table covers the *internal* fits:
crankshaft and rod journal diameters, bearing clearances, piston and ring clearances,
cylinder bore limits, valve-train geometry, and oil-pump internal clearances.

These numbers define whether a worn part can be reused, machined, or must be replaced.
They pair with the engine torque-spec table (main caps, rod caps, etc.) used during
reassembly. Everything here is measured in inches.

## Key Points

- **Crankshaft main journal diameter:** 2.3982–2.3990 in; **rod journal diameter:**
  2.1228–2.1236 in. Main and rod **bearing clearance** are both 0.0008–0.0015 in.
- **Maximum journal taper:** mains 0.0005 in, rods 0.0006 in; crank **runout limit** 0.005 in.
- **Thrust bearing clearance** (crank endplay) 0.0040–0.0080 in; **connecting-rod side
  clearance** 0.0060–0.0130 in.
- **Cylinder bore (std.)** 4.0000–4.0048 in; out-of-round service limit 0.005 in, taper
  service limit 0.01 in, **deck warpage service limit** 0.006 in.
- **Piston clearance** 0.0010–0.0018 in; **compression ring end gap** 0.0100–0.0200 in;
  ring side clearance: top 0.0019–0.0036 in, second 0.0020–0.0040 in, oil ring snug fit.
- **Camshaft:** journal 2.0170–2.0180 in, bearing clearance 0.0010–0.0030 in, endplay
  0.0010–0.0070 in, runout max 0.008 in.
- **Lifters:** bore 0.8752–0.8767 in, lifter diameter 0.8740–0.8745 in, lifter-to-bore
  clearance 0.0007–0.0027 in.
- **Cylinder head warpage limit** 0.007 in; valve seat angle 45°, seat runout 0.002 in;
  valve stem-to-guide clearance 0.0010–0.0027 in; valve guide ID 0.3433–0.3443 in.
- **Valve springs:** free length intake 1.96 in / exhaust 1.78 in; out-of-square limit
  0.078 in; closed pressure 66–74 lb, open pressure 166–184 lb.
- **Oil pump:** rotor backlash 0.012 in, rotor-to-body clearance 0.0010–0.0130 in, rotor
  end clearance 0.0040 in, relief spring 20.6–22.6 lb @ 2.4900 in.
