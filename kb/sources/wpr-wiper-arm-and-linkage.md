## Summary

The wiper arms, pivot shafts, and linkage on the 1994 F-150 are reached by removing
the cowl grille. The FSM removal procedure first strips the wiper arms, blades, and
the upper antenna, then removes the cowl grille (after disconnecting the washer
nozzle hose), and finally releases the linkage clips and pivot body screws to free
the arm and pivot-shaft assemblies. The wiper motor removal procedure shares these
same cowl-grille steps because the motor sits under the grille.

## Key Points

- Disconnect the battery ground cable first.
- Remove both wiper arms, blade assemblies, and the upper antenna assembly.
- Remove cowl grille attaching screws, raise the grille, disconnect the washer
  nozzle hose, then remove the cowl grille assembly.
- Remove the clips securing the right and left linkage and the retaining clip from
  the wiper motor arm.
- Remove pivot body-to-cowl panel attaching screws, then withdraw the arm and pivot
  shaft assemblies.
- Reverse the procedure to install.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Remove cowl grille attaching screws and raise the grille slightly. Disconnect
> washer nozzle hose, then remove cowl grille assembly."

> "Remove clip securing right and left linkage and the retaining clip from wiper
> motor arm. Remove pivot body-to-cowl panel attaching screws, then the arm and
> pivot shaft assemblies."
