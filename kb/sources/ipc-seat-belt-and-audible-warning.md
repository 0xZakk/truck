## Summary

The audible warning device (buzzer/chime) control module drives the truck's reminder chimes
and the seat belt reminder lamp from several inputs. The Key-In-Ignition input sends a ground
signal to the module to chime when the keys are left in the ignition. The Lamps-On input,
fed when the main light switch is in PARK or HEAD, makes the chime sound when the driver's
door is opened until the door is closed or the lamps are turned off. The Seat-Belt-Buckled
input: with the belt unbuckled and power applied, a ground signal starts an electronic timer
that sounds the chime for six seconds. The Seat-Belt-Lamp output turns on the "fasten belts"
indicator for four to eight seconds in START/RUN regardless of belt state.

The seat belt reminder lamp/buzzer itself is a continuous-loop system with a warning
indicator switch, warning light, and chime. If the belt is unbuckled at key-ON, light and
chime run for 4-8 seconds; buckling during that interval shuts both off; if already buckled
at key-ON, the light shows for 4-8 seconds with no chime.

## Key Points

- Module inputs: Key-In-Ignition, Lamps-On, Seat-Belt-Buckled; output: Seat-Belt-Lamp.
- Key-in-ignition chime: ground signal when keys left in ignition.
- Lamps-on chime: sounds with driver's door open while lights are on (PARK/HEAD).
- Seat-belt chime: 6-second timer when belt unbuckled with power applied.
- Fasten-belts lamp: on for 4-8 seconds in START/RUN regardless of belt state.
- Reminder logic: unbuckled at key-ON = light + chime 4-8 s; buckling cancels both; buckled at key-ON = light only, 4-8 s.

## Notable Excerpts

> "With the seat belt unbuckled and the power applied to the warning buzzer/chime module, a
> ground signal is applied to an electronic timer to sound the warning buzzer/chime for six
> seconds."

> "If the safety belt is not buckled when the key is turned to the ON position, the light and
> chime will turn on for 4 to 8 seconds. ... If safety belt is buckled before the key is
> turned to the ON position, the light will come on for 4 to 8 seconds with no chime."
