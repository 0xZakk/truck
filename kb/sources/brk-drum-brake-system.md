## Summary

The rear brakes are internal-expanding, hydraulically actuated drum brakes (type DSSA). The brake drums (1126) use expanding brake shoes (2018) applied against the rotating drum by movable wheel-cylinder pistons (2196) in the rear wheel cylinders (2261). An anchor pin on the non-rotating brake shoe anchor pin guide plate (2028) acts as a stop, preventing the shoe from rotating with the drum. The shoes self-adjust automatically; manual adjustment is only required after relining/replacing shoes or after an adjuster malfunction.

Drum specifications: drum size 11-1/32 x 2.25 in; wheel cylinder bore diameter 0.9375 in (max allowable hone 0.003 in); maximum refinish (inside) diameter 11.090 in. Primary lining thickness 0.224 in, secondary 0.229 in; primary lining length 10.46 in, secondary 10.62 in. Total swept area per axle 155.27 sq in. The maximum drum diameter is stamped on the outside of the drum; replace the drum if the inside diameter exceeds it.

Inspection covers shoe lining thickness (at least 1 mm above rivets or 1.5 mm above the shoe surface, measured at all rivet/top/bottom/side locations using the minimum reading), overheating (glazing, bluish-purple webbing discoloration, warping), and contamination from wheel-cylinder fluid or axle grease. Hardware (springs, automatic adjuster), the backing-plate contact pads, and the wheel cylinder (leakage, mounting, free bleeder screw) are all inspected; contact pads get high-temperature silicone lubricant before reassembly.

## Key Points

- Internal-expanding drum brake, type DSSA; shoes applied by wheel-cylinder pistons.
- Anchor pin on the guide plate (2028) stops the shoes rotating with the drum.
- Self-adjusting; manual adjustment only after reline/replace or adjuster failure.
- Drum size 11-1/32 x 2.25 in; max refinish ID 11.090 in (max stamped on drum).
- Wheel cylinder bore 0.9375 in; max hone 0.003 in.
- Lining thickness primary 0.224 in / secondary 0.229 in; lengths 10.46 / 10.62 in.
- Swept area per axle 155.27 sq in; total lining area per axle 94.44 sq in.
- Shoe min lining: 1 mm above rivets / 1.5 mm above shoe surface; use the minimum measured spot.
- Replace shoes if overheated (glazed/blue/warped) or contaminated; find the root cause first.
- Grooved backing-plate contact pads cause drag/stick; replace the plate rather than grinding grooves out.
- Axle-seal grease contamination often traces to worn axle bearings with excess lateral play.

## Notable Excerpts

> "The brake drums (1126) utilize internal, hydraulically activated, expanding brake shoes that are applied against a rotating drum by movable wheel cylinder pistons (2196)."

> "An anchor pin, located on the non-rotating brake shoe anchor pin guide plate (2028), acts as a stop for the brake shoes prohibiting the shoe from rotating with the drum."

> "Grooved contact pads may cause the rear brakes to drag or stick. If you attempt to remove the grooves by grinding or filing, the new shoes/linings will not line up correctly with the drum."
