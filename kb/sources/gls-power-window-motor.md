## Summary

The power window motor and drive assembly is replaced as a unit from inside the door. The
FSM removal procedure is notable because three of the motor's retaining screws are not
directly accessible — two of them sit behind solid sheet metal, so the technician must drill
two access holes through the door inner panel at factory dimples to reach the hidden screw
heads.

After the motor and drive are disengaged from the regulator gear, the glass is propped in the
full-UP position and the assembly is removed through the door. Installation engages a new
motor to the regulator, torques the retainers, seals the drilled holes with waterproof tape,
and verifies window operation and door drain holes.

## Key Points

- Disconnect the battery ground cable first; on EEC vehicles the processor relearns its adaptive strategy and may need ~10 miles of driving afterward.
- Remove the door trim panel and watershield to access the motor.
- Drill TWO holes with a 12.7 mm (1/2-inch) bit at the drill dimples opposite the two unexposed motor drive retainer screws — check inside the door first so wires are not in line with the drill.
- Remove the three motor mount retainer screws using the two drilled holes plus the existing larger access hole.
- Push the motor toward the outer sheet metal to disengage it from the regulator gear, then prop the window full UP before removing the motor and drive from inside the door.
- Installation torque: tighten the motor retaining screws to 5.6-9.6 N·m (50-85 in-lb).
- Seal each drilled hole with a 25.4 mm (1-inch) square of Pressure-Sensitive Waterproof Tape D6AZ-19627-A (or equivalent body tape).
- Confirm door drain holes are open before reinstalling the trim panel.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Using a 12.7mm (1/2-inch) diameter drill bit, drill two holes in the door inner panel at the drill dimples located opposite the two unexposed motor drive retainer screws."

> "Push the motor toward the outside sheet metal to disengage the motor and drive from the regulator gear. After the motor and drive are disengaged, prop the window in full UP position."

> "Install a new motor and drive assembly and engage the motor to the regulator. Tighten motor retaining screws to 5.6-9.6 N-m (50-85 in-lb)."
