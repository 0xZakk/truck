## Summary

PDF page 10 (printed 8): MPE-107R contains five MPS-126 block plugs, one MPC-147 camshaft plug, one MPS-59A block plug and two MPP-554. Dimensional tables on PDF pages 3 and 7–9 describe these component plugs.

Kit application confirmed in live lookup for 1994 F-150 4.9L. Kit contents are not a complete engine BOM. Plug locations, cup wall thickness and seating depths require further evidence. MPS-59-A inch/metric OD columns disagree slightly; preserve original values.

## Key Points
