## Summary

The door-pillar (door-jamb) switch that turns on the interior courtesy and dome lamps when a
door opens is a small plunger switch threaded into the body pillar. The FSM gives one
identical procedure under both the "Interior Light Switch" and "Dome Lamp Switch" headings:
the switch is secured to the body by a hex sleeve nut and to the main wiring by a twist-lock
arrangement.

The notable detail is the locking mechanism: the switch attaches to the wiring with one of
three tabs spaced 120 degrees apart. If the engaged tab breaks off on disconnection, the
switch is rotated 120 degrees to engage a fresh tab; once all three tabs are broken the
switch must be replaced. Installation involves twisting the wiring and switch 4-1/2 turns
counterclockwise before pushing it back through the pillar. This component is part of the
truck inventory system `electrical-body`.

## Key Points

- Same switch and procedure serves the courtesy lamp and the dome lamp (door-jamb pillar switch).
- Held to the body by a hex sleeve nut; held to the main wiring by one of three tabs 120 degrees apart.
- If the engaged tab breaks, rotate the switch 120 degrees to a fresh tab; replace the switch when all three tabs are gone.
- Installation: twist the wiring and switch 4-1/2 turns counterclockwise, push wiring back through the mounting hole, then screw the switch clockwise into the pillar until seated.
- Verify by opening and closing the door to confirm the interior lamp operates.
