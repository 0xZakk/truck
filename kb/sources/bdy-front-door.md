## Summary

These pages cover servicing the front door's power window motor/drive and the window
regulator. Both jobs start with removing the door trim panel and watershield. The power
window motor is riveted/screwed behind the inner door panel, and because two of its retaining
screws are unexposed, the procedure has you drill two access holes through the inner panel at
factory drill dimples. The regulator itself is attached with rivets that must be drilled out
to remove and re-riveted (or bolted) on installation.

This is a `body-cab` component. The procedures are notable for the destructive-but-intended
drilling step and for the specific waterproofing required afterward (body tape over the
drilled holes and confirming the door drain holes are clear).

## Key Points

- Disconnect the battery ground cable first; the FSM warns the EEC/PCM may need ~10 miles to
  relearn its adaptive strategy afterward.
- Drill two 12.7 mm (1/2 in) holes at the inner-panel drill dimples opposite the two unexposed
  motor drive retainer screws — but first check inside the door that no wiring is behind them.
- After removing the three motor mount retainer screws, push the motor outboard to disengage
  it from the regulator gear, then prop the glass in the full UP position.
- Motor retaining screw torque: 5.6-9.6 N-m (50-85 in-lb).
- Cover the two drilled holes with pressure-sensitive waterproof body tape
  (D6AZ-19627-A or equivalent, 25.4 mm / 1 in square).
- Regulator removal: support glass full up, drill out the regulator attaching rivets (center
  pin out with a drift, then drill the head with a 1/4 in bit), disengage the regulator arm
  from the glass bracket.
- Regulator installation uses a Rotunda hydraulic rivet gun (107-00600); 1/4-20 x 1/2 in
  screw/washer + nut/washer assemblies may substitute if rivets are unavailable.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Using a 12.7mm (1/2-inch) diameter drill bit, drill two holes in the door inner panel at
> the drill dimples located opposite the two unexposed motor drive retainer screws."

> "Tighten motor retaining screws to 5.6-9.6 N-m (50-85 in-lb)."

> "Remove center pin from regulator attaching rivets with drift punch. Then, drill head from
> each rivet using a 6.35mm (1/4-inch) drill... Be careful not to damage sheet metal holes."
