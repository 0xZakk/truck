## Summary

The catalog describes a spring-actuated threaded cartridge with an extended pin, soft seating washer and external static seal. Depressing the pin opens the seated passage. The cap provides contamination protection and a secondary sealing boundary.

This supports a generic mechanism study, not identification of the truck's core. Materials, dimensions and pressure ratings are not transferred to the truck without a verified application match.

## Key Points

- The seating washer and external cartridge seal have different sealing roles.
- The cap is part of the sealing system.
- Exact installed construction remains unresolved.
