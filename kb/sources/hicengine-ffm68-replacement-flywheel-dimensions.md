## Summary

The HICENGINE replacement catalog row FFM68 cross-references LFW132, Z-288, FW167934 and 388019. PDF page 6 gives 164 teeth, 362 mm outside diameter, 44.5 mm inside diameter, 25 mm thickness, six 11 mm crankshaft holes on a 76 mm pitch circle, and a 295 mm clutch-cover pitch circle. Column headings were visually checked on PDF page 1.

These are replacement comparison dimensions, not a measurement of the installed Ford flywheel. The 270 mm disc-facing field does not settle the installed clutch diameter. LuK lists more than one relevant clutch option. The small front/rear product photographs help establish broad shape, but do not establish recess depths, angular hole indexing, ring width, pressure angle, balance drilling or dowels.

## Key Points

- LuK application reference: [[sources/luk-2012-ford-truck-clutch-and-flywheel-applications-102-107|LuK Ford truck applications]].
- Do not substitute the earlier LFW112 search lead for LFW132.
- Smooth fasteners and assumed ring tooth profiles must remain marked provisional.
