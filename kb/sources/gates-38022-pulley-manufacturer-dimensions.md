## Summary

Gates specifies a 90 mm outside diameter, 37.5 mm width and 17 mm bearing bore for steel pulley 38022. Its belt contact is smooth/backside. The application is supported separately by [[sources/gates-1994-ford-49l-belt-tensioner-and-hose-applications-473-473|the 1994 pickup application table]].

The page does not give the internal bearing identity, balls, races or pulley wall section. It warns that images are illustrative. These dimensions are useful for the envelope but do not establish a complete physical decomposition or installed location.
