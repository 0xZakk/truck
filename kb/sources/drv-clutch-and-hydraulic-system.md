## Summary

The 1994 F-150 manual uses a hydraulically actuated clutch. The clutch master cylinder
(pedal end) feeds a slave cylinder through a quick-connect coupling; on this application
the slave is a concentric (internal) slave cylinder that surrounds the input shaft inside
the bellhousing and acts directly on the release bearing. The FSM provides clutch and
release-system torque values, a detailed bleeding procedure for the concentric slave, and
a brief description of the clutch (pedal position) switch.

Because the hydraulic system is a closed circuit with the slave inside the bellhousing,
bleeding is done through the disconnect coupling and the slave bleed screw rather than by
pumping the pedal alone. Trapped air is the usual cause of incomplete clutch release. This
content supports the `driveline` inventory system.

## Key Points

- Clutch is **hydraulically operated** with a **concentric (internal) slave cylinder**;
  fluid is **DOT 3 brake fluid**.
- Torque values: Pressure plate/cover to flywheel **15-20 ft·lb** (bolt #382087) or
  **20-29 ft·lb** (bolt #387299); clutch housing to rear engine cover plate **40-50 ft·lb**;
  release lever seat to housing **12-18 ft·lb**; concentric slave cylinder bolt **14-19 ft·lb**;
  slave cylinder **bleed screw 2.2-3.7 ft·lb**.
- Bleeding requires disconnect tool **T88T-70522-A** to separate the hydraulic coupling at the
  transmission; the male coupling's internal valve is depressed to gravity/pressure bleed.
- The clutch master cylinder reservoir **must be kept full at all times** so air is not drawn in.
- After reconnecting, the pedal is cycled in sets of 5-10 quick strokes with 1-3 minute waits
  between sets, then the slave bleed screw is cracked with the pedal held down.
- The **clutch (pedal position) switch** prevents engine start unless the clutch pedal is
  depressed; depressing the pedal closes the switch and passes current to the starter relay.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Using coupling disconnect tool No. T88T-70522-A or equivalent, disconnect hydraulic coupling
> at transmission by sliding white plastic sleeve toward clutch slave cylinder while tugging on tube."

> "Clutch master cylinder must be kept full at all times to prevent air from entering system."

> "Loosen clutch slave cylinder bleed screw, then depress and hold clutch pedal while torquing
> bleed screw to 2.2-3.7 ft lbs."

> "If clutch does not release fully, indicating air has remained in system, repeat procedure."
