## Summary

The 1994 F-150 speed control (cruise control) system automatically maintains a
driver-selected vehicle speed above approximately 30 mph and below 125 mph. It is an
electronic-servo system: a throttle actuator with integrated electronics pulls on the
throttle linkage through an actuator (Bowden) cable to add or subtract throttle as needed
to hold the set speed. Supporting parts include the amplifier, the horn relay, the steering
wheel driver control switches, a brake lamp switch, a brake deactivator switch, and — on
manual-transmission trucks — a clutch switch.

The vehicle speed signal that the system regulates against comes from either the
Programmable Speedometer/Odometer Module (PSOM) or the Vehicle Speed Sensor (VSS); the VSS
is mounted on the transaxle. The electronic servo is mounted in the left-hand rear corner of
the engine compartment.

To operate the system the engine must be running and vehicle speed must exceed 30 mph. With
the On-Off switch actuated the system is ready to accept a set speed; pressing Set-Acc at a
stable speed of at least 30 mph captures that speed, which is then held until the brake pedal
is depressed, the system is turned off, or a new speed is set.

This page also consolidates the system-level Service Precautions, which require disarming the
driver air bag before steering-wheel/column work (the steering wheel carries the cruise
switches and clockspring on this truck).

## Key Points

- Operating window: maintains any set speed roughly 30-125 mph; will not engage below ~30 mph.
- Core actuator: electronic servo (throttle actuator + integrated electronics) in the
  left-rear corner of the engine compartment, linked to the throttle by an actuator cable.
- Speed reference: PSOM or transaxle-mounted VSS.
- Deactivation inputs: brake lamp switch, brake deactivator (pressure) switch, and the
  clutch switch on manual transmissions.
- Service precaution: record radio presets, disconnect battery negative then positive, wait
  at least one minute for the air bag back-up power supply to deplete, and install the
  Rotunda Air Bag Simulator 105-00010 before working on the steering wheel/column.
- After battery disconnect the PCM relearns its adaptive strategy; the truck may need to be
  driven 10+ miles before drive quality normalizes.

## Notable Excerpts

> "The speed control system is designed to automatically maintain vehicle speed at any
> driver-selected speed above approximately 30 mph and below 125 mph."

> "The electronic servo is mounted in the lefthand rear corner of the engine compartment and
> is connected to the throttle linkage with an actuator cable."

> "To operate the speed control system, the engine must be running and the vehicle speed must
