## Summary

Visually reviewed printed/PDF pages 34 and 37. The 4.9L listing pairs kit 152-6002 with head style M; the separate 240–300 listing pairs 152-6001 with G. The M photograph shows a rounded head perimeter, unlike the modeled regular hexagon. These are replacement comparisons, not identification of the truck’s installed bolts.
