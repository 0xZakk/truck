## Summary

A short, narrated walk-through of replacing both the **fender-mounted starter solenoid
(starter relay)** and the **starter motor** on an OBS Ford F-150 (the truck shown is a
'95). The host re-uploaded an older clip with commentary to call out the exact bolt/nut
sizes and the order of operations, which makes it directly applicable to a stock 1994
F-150. The job is presented as straightforward, especially the solenoid, which the host
calls one of the easiest he has worked on.

For the **solenoid**, the two large battery/cable connections are **9/16-inch nuts** and
the two mounting bolts are **8 mm**; you remove the electrical nuts first, then the
mounting bolts, then reverse to install. A small **push-on "elbow" connector** (the S/I
trigger wire) at the top of the solenoid simply pulls out and pushes back in — there is no
fastener holding it. For the **starter motor**, the two mounting bolts are **14 mm** and
the two electrical connections are **one 13 mm and one 10 mm** nut; again remove the
electrical connections first, then the mounting bolts, and install in the reverse order
(bolts first, then the electrical connections).

The commentary is mostly cautionary: always disconnect the battery negative terminal
before any electrical work; the aged electrical connectors are fragile (the host snapped
one and had to have a replacement terminal rigged up); the lower starter mounting bolt
started to strip because he skipped penetrating fluid; and you should wire-brush the
mounting surface clean before bolting on the new starter. He closes by replacing a PCV
valve and its valve-cover grommet — unrelated to the starter but shown at the end.

## Key Points

- **Disconnect the battery NEGATIVE terminal first** before any electrical work.
- **Solenoid (starter relay) fasteners:** two **9/16"** battery/cable nuts, two **8 mm**
  mounting bolts. Remove the electrical nuts first, then the mounting bolts.
- The **small "elbow" trigger connector** on top of the solenoid is **push-on / pull-off**
  — no bolt holds it.
- Many starters have the **solenoid built into the starter**; this OBS truck uses a
  **separate fender-mounted solenoid**, so it can be replaced on its own.
- **Starter motor fasteners:** two **14 mm** mounting bolts; electrical connections are
  **one 13 mm** and **one 10 mm** nut. Remove electrical first, then mounting bolts;
  install bolts first, then electrical.
- **Soak fasteners in penetrating fluid first** — the host stripped the lower mounting bolt
  and broke a brittle electrical connector by skipping this.
- **Wire-brush the mounting surface** before installing the new starter to clear dirt and
  ensure a clean ground/seat.
