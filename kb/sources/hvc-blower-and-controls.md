## Summary

The cabin airflow side of the 1994 F-150 HVAC system (inventory id `hvac`) is governed by the
dash Control Assembly working together with the blower motor switch, blower motor resistor,
and vacuum-actuated air doors. The control assembly controls the vacuum function of the air
doors (mode selection), runs the blower motor, and — together with the PCM — the A/C clutch.
The blower motor resistor limits blower motor current to give the LO, MED-LO, MED-HI, and HI
fan speeds. The blower motor switch and control assembly have component test, terminal-
identification, and schematic figures in the FSM.

This source consolidates the Control Assembly Description/Operation and Testing/Inspection
pages, the Blower Motor Resistor Description/Operation page, and the Blower Motor Switch
Testing/Inspection page.

## Key Points

- The Control Assembly controls air-door vacuum (mode), blower operation, and A/C clutch
  (with the PCM).
- The blower motor resistor limits blower speed so the motor runs at LO, MED-LO, MED-HI, or
  HI; the lower speeds drop voltage across resistor elements.
- HI speed typically bypasses the resistor (runs the motor at full voltage), which is why a
  failed resistor commonly leaves only HI working.
- Blower switch and control-assembly diagnosis relies on FSM component-test procedures,
  terminal-identification, and schematic figures.
