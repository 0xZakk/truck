## Summary

The windshield washer pump on the 1994 F-150 is tested by measuring its current
draw with a volt-ohmmeter while the pump is actively pumping fluid. The FSM gives a
specific acceptable window for that current.

## Key Points

- Test by attaching volt-ohmmeter leads and reading current draw while the pump
  pumps fluid.
- Current draw should not exceed 4 amps and should not be less than 1.7 amps while
  pumping.
- Readings outside that 1.7–4 amp window indicate a faulty pump (binding/high draw,
  or open/weak pump on low draw).

## Notable Excerpts

> "Current draw should not exceed 4 amps nor be less than 1.7 amps while windshield
> washer pump is pumping fluid."
