## Summary

The actual public full-size illustration places the air-charge sensor in a dedicated boss at the front terminal lower-intake runner. Numbered runner references2–5 and a front-of-engine arrow establish the end. A retained exact1994 service image, DM05Q313/ford10/147826554, independently shows the same local boss and identifies sensor12A697. The public page contextualizes a1987 intake-installation TSB. Its annotated image is a topology comparison, not an exact1994 metric drawing. Neither it nor the exact-year local diagram supplies port coordinates,3Daxis, gauge plane or engagement. The exact-year service text specifies16–24Nm and harness disconnection before removal, but does not say the hex bottoms against the boss.
