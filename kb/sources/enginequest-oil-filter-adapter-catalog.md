## Summary

The 2025 manufacturer catalog, page 23, lists EQ-OFA302 / EQ-OFA302I for a Ford engine group including the 4.9L/300, covering 1970–1996. Its OEM cross-reference is D7AZ6890A. The photograph shows a hollow stepped fitting with external threads at both ends, a shoulder and an internal drive feature. It supplies no dimensional drawing.

This catalog does not establish equivalence to the E4TZ6890A anti-drainback insert in Ford bulletin 95-8-8. Thread lengths and pitches, internal dimensions and drainback behavior remain unresolved. Preserve that distinction when reconstructing the filter-to-block connection.
