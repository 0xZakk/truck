## Summary

The wiper control module is the electronic brain of the wiper/washer system on the
1994 F-150. It receives signals from the multi-function (wiper) switch and performs
the washer, low-speed, high-speed, and interval wiper functions. The module is
mounted behind the right-hand side of the instrument panel, near the top of the
cowl panel.

The interval (INT) feature lets the wipers make single sweeps separated by an
adjustable pause. A control knob on the end of the wiper switch sets the pause
length from roughly 1 to 12 seconds. Because the module times the pause, the very
first wipe after selecting INT may not occur until up to 12 seconds have elapsed.

## Key Points

- The wiper control module receives multi-function switch signals and drives the
  washer, low-speed, high-speed, and interval functions.
- Module is located behind the RH side of the instrument panel near the top of the
  cowl panel.
- Interval mode = single sweeps separated by a pause, adjustable ~1–12 seconds via
  the knob on the wiper switch.
- The first interval wipe can lag by as much as 12 seconds — normal behavior, not a
  fault.
