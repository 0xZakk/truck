## Summary

The FSM's "Initial Inspection and Diagnostic Overview" page presents a Diagnosis Guide
chart for the rear axle and driveline. It groups complaints into five categories — Noise
Conditions, Vibration Conditions, Leakage Conditions, Inoperative Conditions, and
Non-Axle Noises — and lists the specific components or checks associated with each. Its
first job is to keep a technician from chasing the axle when the noise is really coming
from elsewhere: a Non-Axle Noises section explicitly calls out grille whistle, trim
moulding, roof rack, belt chirp/squeal, A/C system pulses, exhaust, tires, and aftermarket
add-ons (running boards, body-to-frame grounding, antennas, visors, bug deflectors).

Within the axle itself, the guide ties each characteristic sound to a probable cause:
gear howl/whine to gear-set and ring-gear setup, chuckle to differential gear clearance or
broken teeth, knock to gear-tooth mutilation or axle-shaft end play, clunk to driveline
backlash/u-joints/engine mounts, and bearing noise to specific bearings or limited-slip
operation. It also enumerates leakage points and the catastrophic "inoperative" failures
(broken shaft, pinion stem, welds, end yoke, u-joint, gear teeth, axle lock-up).

## Key Points

- Always rule out Non-Axle Noises first: grille whistle, trim moulding, roof rack, belt
  chirp, A/C pulses, belt squeal, exhaust, tires, and aftermarket add-ons.
- Gear Howl and Whine → inspect gear set, check gear-set backlash, check ring-gear run-out,
  check bearing preload.
- Chuckle Noise → differential gear clearance or broken gear teeth.
- Knock Noise → gear-tooth mutilation, ring-gear bolts, or axle-shaft end play.
- Clunk Noise → total axle backlash, worn driveshaft u-joints, loose/broken engine mounts,
  driveshaft splines needing lube, worn slip-yoke splines, axle-shaft spline fit, or an
  inoperative throttle damper.
- Bearing Noise → pinion bearing, wheel bearing, differential thrust washer/bearing,
  limited-slip operation, or click on engagement.
- Vibration Conditions → tires, driveline angle, universal joint, axle shaft, drive-pinion
  stem and flange, wheel nuts.
- Leakage Conditions → drive-pinion seal, drive-pinion nut, axle cover gasket, axle-shaft
  seals, casting porosity, weld leaks, axle vent/hose.
- Inoperative Conditions → broken axle shaft, broken pinion stem, broken welds, wheel
  bearing, axle lock-up, broken end yoke, broken u-joint, broken gear teeth.
