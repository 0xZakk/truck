## Summary

Two control devices decide when the A/C compressor clutch is allowed to engage on the
1994 F-150 (inventory id `hvac`). The Refrigerant Pressure Sensor/Switch cuts power to the
A/C clutch field coil when suction pressure is too low, protecting the system from running
without enough refrigerant. The Compressor Clutch Relay — the Wide Open Throttle A/C (WOT
A/C) relay — opens during wide-open-throttle so A/C is suspended and full engine power goes
to the wheels under heavy acceleration or load.

This source consolidates the Refrigerant Pressure Sensor/Switch and Compressor Clutch Relay
Description/Operation pages.

## Key Points

- The refrigerant pressure switch cuts off power to the A/C clutch field coil at low suction
  pressure.
- Pressure switch hysteresis: closes at 293 kPa (43.5 psi) with pressure rising; opens at
  169 kPa (24.5 psi) with pressure falling.
- The Compressor Clutch Relay is the WOT A/C relay; it opens at wide-open throttle to
  suspend A/C and free up engine power.
- Normal operation resumes (A/C re-enabled) once the truck returns to normal operation.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Cuts off power to the A/C Clutch Field Coil when there is low suction pressure. ... Closes
> at 293 kPa (43.5 psi), with pressure increasing; opens at 169 (24.5 psi), with pressure
> decreasing."

> "When the vehicle operator goes to Wide Open Throttle (WOT), the Wide Open Throttle A/C
> (WOT A/C) relay opens, suspending A/C operation until normal vehicle operation resumes.
> This is done to allow maximum power to the drive wheels under heavy acceleration or load."
