## Summary

Exact1994 Ford V5855-F shows a regulator toward the front rail portion and a second tube alongside the rail to paired rear connectors. Actual images of an earlier1987 4.9EFI rail corroborate this topology. They do not support the model's centered regulator and half-length return route.

The separate behind-rail hardline specimen supports formed bends and straight sealing leads only. Its identity is not sufficient to assign the inboard return route. Owner enginebay photos obscure the required correspondence.
