## Summary

The manufacturer lists PS-238 as an engine oil-pressure switch with one male stud terminal, screw-in connection and aluminum/plastic construction. Its features describe a polyimide-film diaphragm. The exterior photograph shows a threaded inlet neck, tapered metal body, broad wrenching region, crimped rim, blue insulator and axial terminal stud.

The buyers guide includes 1988–1996 F-150, but engine-specific applicability and the E9TZ9278A interchange remain unconfirmed by this manufacturer page. No dimensional drawing, switching threshold or internal contact arrangement was provided. The photograph is shape evidence rather than a measured drawing.
