## Summary

The Malfunction Indicator Lamp (MIL) alerts the driver to certain faults in the EEC-IV engine
control system and is also used to flash service (trouble) codes during self-test. On
trucks with a message center, "CHECK ENGINE" and/or "CHECK DLC" messages are displayed; the
Powertrain Control Module (PCM) sends the "CHECK ENGINE" message to the message center
through the Data Link Connector (DLC).

The "CHECK ENGINE" message is activated when the PCM switches to an alternate operating
strategy — Failure Modes Effect Management (FMEM). On entering FMEM the message appears with
a one-second tone every five seconds, suppressed after one minute. The message also displays
during Hardware Limited Operating Strategy (HLOS) operation, and "CHECK DLC" may accompany it.

## Key Points

- MIL warns of EEC-IV faults and flashes service codes during self-test.
- PCM sends "CHECK ENGINE" to the message center via the Data Link Connector (DLC).
- "CHECK ENGINE" triggers when the PCM enters Failure Modes Effect Management (FMEM).
- FMEM: message plus a one-second tone every five seconds; tone suppressed after one minute.
- Message also appears during Hardware Limited Operating Strategy (HLOS); "CHECK DLC" may also display.

## Notable Excerpts

> "The 'CHECK ENGINE' message is activated when the PCM switches to an alternate strategy of
> operation. This process is called Failure Modes Effect Management (FMEM)."

> "When the vehicle enters FMEM the 'CHECK ENGINE' message will be displayed and accompanied
> by a one second tone every five seconds. The tone will be suppressed after one minute."
