## Summary

PDF pages 17–18 (printed pages 14–15) were rendered and visually reviewed. The
parts book shows the pump, rotor-and-shaft service set, cover plate, intermediate
shaft and retainer, relief components, pickup and inlet gasket. The pump assembly
is C5AZ-6600-A, matching the service number in the project's 1994 truck catalog
source fsm-3ceaca21ab73. That match supports comparison, but does not establish
unchanged casting, internal revisions or installed pickup geometry.

The list specifies four plate-to-pump bolts. The new study uses four cover bolts
and a rounded rectangular cover/flange based on this comparison. Their locations
and dimensions remain assumed. The industrial pickup is C5TZ-6622-B; the truck
catalog instead lists E3TZ6622F, so the industrial tube path cannot be treated as
the truck's geometry.

## Key Points

- Pump service identity agrees across the two catalogs; pickup identity differs.
- Published industrial intermediate-shaft length is 4.52 inches. This has not been
  adopted as a verified installed truck dimension.
- The comparison lists relief plunger and spring dimensions substantially larger
  than the initial illustrative bore study. The model was revised to use those
  comparison envelopes; truck revision and detailed spring/valve geometry remain
  unverified.
- Cover hardware quantities can be checked independently of unverified geometry.
