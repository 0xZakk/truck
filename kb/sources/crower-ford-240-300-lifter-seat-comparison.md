## Summary

Catalog page119 (PDF page1) lists Ford240/300 six-cylinder hydraulic flat-tappet lifter66015J-12 with0.874in body diameter and1.715in pushrod-seat height. The introduction explicitly describes these heights as comparisons that may vary. The slow-leakdown66015JRX3-12 row uses1.660in instead.

These are replacement alternatives, not established1994Ford or owner-installed dimensions. A datum study must establish whether seat height refers to socket bottom or ball center, and the hydraulic-plunger condition. Those conventions are not established by the table.

## Key Points

- Standard comparison height1.715in converts to43.561mm.
- Do not freeze a production CAD datum from this comparison alone.
- A separate manufacturer [Howards91211-12 page](https://www.howardscams.com/hydraulic-flat-tappet-performance-lifters-ford-240-300-howards-cams-91211-12) reports2.018in seat height for its Ford240/300 product. Preserve this difference; do not combine the two products as one specification.
