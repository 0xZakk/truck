## Summary

The factory routing illustration covers regular-cab and SuperCab short-wheelbase F-Series trucks, showing a regular cab with dual tanks and noting that long-wheelbase arrangements are similar. Its key identifies supply connector N805526-S190 and return connector N805528-S190. The drawing also identifies the front fuel-line assembly, fuel-filter support and vapor-return routing.

These identifiers provide a starting point for subsequent replacement-part research. They do not establish fitting dimensions or the owner's exact tank configuration. Despite the page's vacuum/vapor title, the illustrations do not resolve the engine PCV hose connections.
