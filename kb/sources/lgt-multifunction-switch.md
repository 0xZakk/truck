## Summary

On the 1994 F-150 the turn signal switch, hazard warning switch, and headlamp high/low
beam dimmer are all the same steering-column "multi-function switch." The FSM gives the
identical removal/installation procedure and the identical attaching-screw torque for the
turn-signal, hazard, and dimmer entries, and its testing pages reference one "Multi-Function
Switch" terminal/schematic set. Diagnosis for all three functions is done by checking
continuity at the multi-function switch terminals against the schematic; the on-disk test
procedure itself is an image, so the operative content is the shared removal procedure and
terminal layout.

The turn signal flasher and hazard flasher are separate plug-in units, not part of the
switch. The turn signal flasher sits behind the left side of the instrument panel on the
front of the fuse panel. This component is part of the truck inventory system
`electrical-body`.

## Key Points

- Turn signal, hazard, and dimmer functions are combined in one column-mounted multi-function switch with a shared removal procedure.
- Removal: on tilt columns unscrew and remove the tilt lever; remove the steering column shroud; remove the switch-to-column attaching screws; disengage and disconnect the switch.
- Multi-function switch attaching screws torque: 18-27 inch-lbs.
- Caution during removal: do not damage the PRNDL cable on column-shift trucks.
- Turn signal flasher is a separate plug-in unit located behind the LH side of the instrument panel, on the front of the fuse panel.
- Testing is by continuity at the multi-function switch terminals per the FSM schematic (the step-by-step is supplied only as a manual image).

## Notable Excerpts

> "REMOVAL AND INSTALLATION 1. Remove steering column shroud. 2. Remove switch to steering
> column tube casting attaching screws, then disengage switch from column. 3. Disconnect
> switch electrical connectors. ... torque switch attaching screws to 18-27 inch lbs."

> "NOTE: On models with tilt column, unscrew the tilt lever and remove."

> "The Turn Signal Flasher is located behind the LH side of the instrument panel, on front
> of the fuse panel."
