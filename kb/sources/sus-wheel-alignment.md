## Summary

Front alignment on the Twin I-Beam F-150 is set with caster/camber adjuster sleeves and
specified to optimum-plus-range values. A single adjuster sleeve at each wheel sets BOTH
caster and camber, available in 1/2, 1, and 1 1/2 degree increments. Before any alignment
reading is trusted, the FSM requires checking front ride height (within 0 - 3/8 inch
side-to-side) and equal tire size/pressure, because alignment equipment only reads true when
the frame is horizontal.

This source consolidates the Alignment Specifications, the Caster/Camber service note, and
the Ride Height pre-check for the `suspension` system.

## Key Points

- Camber: optimum .25 deg +/- .5 deg.
- Caster: minimum 2.0 deg, maximum 6.0 deg.
- Total toe: optimum .06 deg +/- .25 deg.
- Camber split: .0 deg +/- .4 deg. Caster split: -.5 deg +/- .4 deg.
- One adjuster sleeve sets both caster and camber; offered in 1/2, 1, and 1 1/2 deg increments.
- Pre-check ride height: front side-to-side within 0 - 3/8 inch before reading caster/camber.
- If the frame is not level, modify the caster reading: front higher than rear, subtract; front lower, add.

> "Camber Optimum +/- Range  .25 deg +/- .5 deg... Caster Minimum 2.0 deg... Caster Maximum 6.0 deg...
