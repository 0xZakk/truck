## Summary

The remanufacturer's ALL-12952 listing supplies photographs of a Ford 300 EFI head described as a 1987–96, without-smog, heart-shaped-chamber application. The deck and spark-plug-side photographs were visually reviewed. They show six noncircular chamber recesses and six recessed plug wells, details absent from the earlier CAD head.

These are comparison photographs, not identification of the owner's installed casting. They establish visible feature topology, not bore angles, well depths, chamber volumes, port dimensions or casting identity. No dimensions were recovered from perspective photographs. The page's unsupported crossflow marketing language is excluded from the engine knowledge model.

## Key Points

- Photo 2 shows the deck, chamber outlines and valve faces.
- Photo 3 shows the recessed plug wells on the opposite side from the manifold ports.
- The CAD now shares provisional head/plug mounting coordinates to make those interfaces geometrically coherent.
- Collision clearance is a model check; it does not establish production fit or internal coolant-jacket geometry.
