## Summary

This source consolidates the factory fill capacities and fluid-type callouts most useful
when servicing the truck: fuel tank capacities by cab/wheelbase and tank location, cooling
system capacity, engine oil capacity, and manual-transmission fluid type and refill amount.
These support routine maintenance of the engine (inventory id `engine`) and its supporting
systems.

Capacities here come from the Capacity Specifications section; fluid types come from the
Fluid Type Specifications section of the FSM. Several of these (cooling capacity, oil
capacity, coolant type) are also captured in maintenance notes; this source adds the
fuel-tank and manual-transmission figures and keeps the capacity table together.

## Key Points

- **Fuel tank capacity** varies by cab and wheelbase, not by engine:
  - Regular Cab, 116.8 in WB: midship 62 L (16.3 gal), aft-of-axle 69 L (18.2 gal).
  - Regular Cab, 133.0 in WB: midship 72 L (19.0 gal), aft-of-axle 69 L (18.2 gal).
  - Super Cab, 138.8 in WB: midship 64 L (16.5 gal), aft-of-axle 69 L (18.2 gal).
  - Super Cab, 155.0 in WB: midship 72 L (19.0 gal), aft-of-axle 69 L (18.2 gal).
  - Aft-of-axle is the standard second tank on dual-tank trucks.
- **Engine oil refill:** 5.0 qt (4.7 L); note adds 1 qt with a filter change.
- **Cooling system capacity:** 13 qt (US) up to 15 qt (US) depending on transmission, A/C,
  and super-cooling package.
- **Coolant:** Ford Premium cooling system fluid (spec ESE-M97B44-A), mixed 50% with water;
  coolant types must not be mixed.
- **Manual transmission (M5OD-R2, the F-series 5-speed):** refill capacity 7.6 pints;
  fluid is Mercon V automatic transmission fluid (per TSB 06-14-4 supersession; the table's
  base 80W-EP applies to older 4-speeds).
- **Radiator cap relief pressure:** 13 psi.
