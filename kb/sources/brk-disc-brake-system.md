## Summary

The front brakes are a pin-sliding, single-piston caliper design. The caliper attaches to the front wheel spindle with two bolts passing through the slide pins. The ends of the brake pad assemblies (2001) are contained within the spindle, and the spindle abutments carry anti-rattle clips (2B164) at each end to give a low-drag surface for the pads to slide against. The caliper mounts over the top of the pads and bolts to the spindle on the inboard side; the outboard side of the caliper is secured to the outer pad with the outer shoe clip. By design, nearly all brake torque is transmitted directly to the spindle, not through the caliper housing.

A single wheel-cylinder piston rides in the caliper bore with the hydraulic inlet at the bottom. A square-section seal in an annular groove seals against pressure, while rubber boots keep road splash off the piston and bore. A bleeder screw above the bore bleeds air. The hub and rotor are an integrally cast assembly; a rotor shield bolted to the spindle protects the bearings and inboard rotor face. Disc brakes are self-adjusting.

Inspection covers pad lining thickness (minimum 1/8 in / 3 mm riveted, 1/16 in / 1.5 mm non-riveted), lining cracking/glazing, pad flatness (gap not over 0.13 mm / 0.005 in at the middle), caliper fluid leakage at the piston boot, cracked housings, uneven/side-to-side pad wear, slide-pin corrosion, and signs of overheating (bluish-purple discoloration or a thin brick-red oxidation layer). Pads carry a wear-indicator that emits a metallic squeal when replacement is due.

## Key Points

- Pin-sliding, single-piston caliper bolted to the spindle through the slide pins.
- Brake torque reacts into the spindle, not the caliper housing.
- Anti-rattle clips (2B164) give the pads a low-drag sliding surface.
- Hub and rotor are integrally cast; rotor shield protects bearings and inboard face.
- Pad wear indicator squeals when replacement is due.
- Pad min thickness: 1/8 in (3 mm) riveted, 1/16 in (1.5 mm) non-riveted.
- Pad flatness gap must not exceed 0.13 mm (0.005 in) at the middle.
- Rotor: min allowable thickness 0.96 in; max parallelism variation 0.0005 in; lateral runout 0.003 in; surface finish 0-65.
- Caliper-to-spindle bolts 22-26 ft lb; anchor bracket-to-spindle bolts 141-191 ft lb; front brake hose inlet block 10-15 ft lb.
- Out-of-adjustment rear brakes overwork the fronts, accelerating wear and overheating.

## Notable Excerpts
