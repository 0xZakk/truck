## Summary

Melling's dimensional chart complements the earlier stock-valve identification table. Page 8 lists V1504 with 1.559in head diameter, 4.750in overall length and 0.342in stem diameter. Page 11 lists V1505 with 1.783in head diameter, 4.749in overall length and 0.342in stem diameter. Both specify a 45° face and single groove.

These are manufacturer replacement dimensions, not measurements of the owner's installed valves. The earlier application-lookup anomaly remains unresolved. Geometry must preserve that distinction.

## Key Points

- V1504 overall length converts to120.65mm; V1505 to120.6246mm.
- The shared stem diameter converts to8.6868mm.
- The earlier teaching candidate's109mm overall valve length is about11.6mm short of these comparison parts.
- This table does not establish groove location, head profile, tip metallurgy or installed datum.
