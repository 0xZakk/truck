## Summary

The factory service manual lists electrical and mechanical specifications for the
Motorcraft permanent-magnet starters by starter diameter (4 in and 4-1/2 in versions).
These give the normal-load and no-load current draws used to judge the No-Load and Load
tests, plus cranking speed, brush wear limits, and brush-spring tension for rebuild.

The values vary by starter size, so identify the starter diameter before comparing
readings. The current-draw figures are the pass/fail reference for the bench No-Load test
and the in-vehicle Load Test.

## Key Points

- **4-1/2 in starter:** normal-load draw **150–180 A**; no-load draw **80 A @ 12 V**;
  cranking speed **150–290 RPM**; brush length **0.50 in**, wear limit **0.25 in**; brush
  spring tension **40 oz**.
- **4 in starter (variant A):** normal-load draw **150–200 A**; no-load **60–85 A @ 12 V**;
  cranking speed **180–250 RPM**; brush **0.50 in**, wear limit **0.25 in**; spring **40 oz**.
- **4 in starter (variant B):** normal-load draw **130–220 A**; no-load **60–80 A @ 12 V**;
  cranking speed **140–220 RPM**; minimum stall torque **11 ft-lbs @ 5 V**; brush **0.66 in**,
  wear limit **0.25 in**; spring tension **64 oz**.
