## Summary

The 1994 F-150 2WD uses an integral-carrier Ford **8.8-inch** rear axle, available as an
open differential or a **Traction-Lok** limited-slip unit. The FSM gives the fastener torque
specifications, the collapsible-spacer pinion-bearing preload targets, the rear-cover and
fill-plug torques, and the axle lubricant capacity and additive requirements. Most of the
gear-set technical data (gear ratios, backlash, shim charts) is presented as scanned tables.

The drive pinion preload is set with a **collapsible spacer**, which is a one-shot,
non-adjustable-back design: if you over-torque the pinion nut past the preload spec, the
spacer must be replaced. This source covers the `rear-axle` inventory system.

## Key Points

- Axle is the integral-carrier **Ford 8.8-inch** ring gear; limited-slip option is **Traction-Lok**.
- Torque: Differential bearing cap bolt **70-85 ft·lb**; ring gear attaching bolts **70-85 ft·lb**;
  differential pinion shaft lock bolt **15-30 ft·lb**; rear cover screws **25-35 ft·lb** (the
  Differential Cover page lists **15-20 ft·lb** for cover bolts — note the discrepancy between
  pages); oil filler plug **15-30 ft·lb**; axle vent **15-25 ft·lb**; brake backing plate nuts
  **20-40 ft·lb**; RABS sensor hold-down **25-30 ft·lb**.
- **Drive pinion nut: 217 ft·lb** — but only as needed to reach preload; if preload exceeds spec
  before that torque is reached, the collapsible spacer must be replaced.
- **Pinion-bearing preload (rotational torque):** new bearings **16-29 in·lb**, used bearings
  **8-14 in·lb**.
- **Rear axle lubricant capacity:** **5.5 pt (2.6 L)** hypoid gear lubricant; add **4 oz of
  friction modifier** (part C8AZ-19B546-A or equivalent) — required for Traction-Lok operation.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Pinion Shaft Nut — 217 ft lb. [1] If pinion bearing preload exceeds specification before torque
> is obtained, replace collapsible spacer."

> "PINION BEARING PRELOAD — Adjustment Method: Collapsible Spacer. New Bearings: 16-29 Inch Pounds.
> Used Bearings: 8-14 Inch Pounds."

> "Rear Axle Oil (Hypoid Gear Lubricant) 5.5 pt / 2.6L. Add 4 ounces of friction modifier additive
> part No. C8AZ-19B546-A or equivalent."
