## Summary

The speed control servo/amplifier assembly is the actuator that physically moves the
throttle to hold the set speed. Using the vehicle speed signal from the programmable
speedometer/odometer module (PSOM), it increases or decreases vehicle speed to maintain the
set speed by pulling on the throttle linkage through an actuator (Bowden) cable.

Testing the servo is largely a visual/connection check: inspect the servo assembly for loose
or unseated connector pins and broken wires at the connectors. The actuator cable is
inspected for improper adjustment (readjust as required) and for breakage.

Actuator cable adjustment is precise and deliberately leaves a small amount of slack: with
the cable clip removed and the throttle plate closed, pull the cable to take up slack, then
back off at least one notch to leave 0.04 inch of slack. The cable must not be pulled tight
or the system will not operate correctly.

## Key Points

- Function: servo/amplifier increases or decreases vehicle speed to maintain set speed using
  the PSOM speed signal.
- Servo test: inspect for loose/unseated connector pins or broken wires at the connectors.
- Cable test: inspect Bowden cable for improper adjustment and check for a broken actuator cable.
- Cable adjustment: throttle plate closed, take up slack, back off at least one notch to leave
  0.04 inch slack; do not pull the cable tight.
