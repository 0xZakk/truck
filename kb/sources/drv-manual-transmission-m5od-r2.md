## Summary

The 1994 F-150 with the 4.9L I6 uses the Mazda-built M5OD (M5OD-R2) 5-speed manual
transmission. The FSM groups overhaul-time inspection of the individual gear-train parts
(input shaft, output shaft, countershaft, synchronizer rings, shift forks, extension
housing, speedometer gear) under "Testing and Inspection," with several measurable
wear limits called out for go/no-go decisions during a rebuild.

The transmission's technical data (gear ratios, assembly tolerances) and torque data are
provided primarily as scanned tables/diagrams in the manual rather than as machine-readable
text. The text-bearing inspection pages, however, give concrete clearance and runout
limits that a rebuilder can act on directly. These checks belong to the `driveline`
inventory system.

## Key Points

- Transmission is the **M5OD 5-speed** ("Under 8500 GVW - With T-18 and M5OD Transmission").
- **Synchronizer ring to gear clearance** must be at least **0.031 inch**; below that, replace
  the synchronizer ring or gear. The limit is the same for 1st/2nd, 3rd/4th, and 5th/reverse.
- **Shift fork to clutch hub sleeve clearance** should be **0.031 inch**; check the fork
  contact surface for wear/damage.
- **Output shaft total runout** must not exceed **0.002 inch** (measured in V-blocks with a
  dial indicator).
- **Input shaft**: replace if splines are damaged, the needle-bearing bore surface is worn or
  rough, or the cone surface is damaged.
- **Countershaft**: replace if gear teeth or splines are bent, worn, or scored.
- **Extension housing**: check for cracks; the rear bushing is not serviceable — replace the
  housing as a unit if the bushing is bad. Replace the oil seal after the housing is installed.
- Synchronizer ring/gear cone contact is verified with Prussian blue; poor contact is
  corrected by lapping with compound.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Measure clearance between side faces of synchronizer ring and gear using a suitable feeler
> gauge. If clearance is less than 0.031 inch, replace synchronizer ring or gear... 1st/2nd,
> 3rd/4th, and 5th/reverse synchronizer to gear clearance specifications are the same."

> "Total output shaft runout should not exceed 0.002 inch."

> "Extension housing rear bushing cannot be serviced. If service is required, replace as a unit."

> "Check countershaft gear teeth and countershaft splines for wear or damage. If bent, worn or
> scored, replace countershaft."
