## Summary

The needle-bearing table on PDF150 (printed147) lists FC-65662 shaft diameter 0.6721 inch, OD 1.4400 inch and width 0.6700 inch; shaft limits 0.6710–0.6733 and housing limits 1.4300–1.4500 inch. Type is SPCL. PDF102 (printed99) instead lists bore0.671, OD1.450 and width0.669 inch. Both rows were visually reviewed. The discrepancy is retained explicitly; it is not silently reclassified as manufacturing tolerance.

Internal needle count, needle diameter, cage pockets, race section, closure and installed axial datum are not established. The generic sectional illustrations are not a specific FC65662 production drawing.
