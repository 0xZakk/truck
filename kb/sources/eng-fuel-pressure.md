## Summary

Fuel pressure on the injected 4.9L is checked at the Schrader (test port) fitting with a
fuel-pressure tester after relieving system pressure. The fuel pump is energized by
grounding the fuel-pump lead of the diagnostic connector and turning the key on with the
engine off. Specifications are 50-60 PSI (345-415 kPa) key-on/engine-off and 45-60 PSI
(310-415 kPa) key-on/engine-running at idle.

Maximum pressure is obtained with the vacuum hose removed from the fuel pressure regulator
or at wide-open throttle — the regulator references manifold vacuum, so idle (high vacuum)
pressure reads lower than KOEO pressure. The FSM emphasizes the fuel-system service
precautions before opening the Schrader fitting to avoid spillage, injury, or fire.

This source backs the `engine` and `intake-exhaust` inventory systems.

## Key Points

- Test at the Schrader test-port fitting with a fuel-pressure tester; relieve pressure first.
- Energize pump: ground the fuel-pump lead at the diagnostic connector, key on / engine off.
- Spec key-on/engine-off: 50-60 PSI (345-415 kPa).
- Spec key-on/engine-running (idle): 45-60 PSI (310-415 kPa).
- Idle pressure reads lower than KOEO because the regulator references manifold vacuum.
- Maximum pressure: remove the regulator vacuum hose or hold wide-open throttle.

## Notable Excerpts

> "Ground the fuel pump lead of the Diagnostic Connector with a jumper. Key On, Engine Off to
> operate the fuel pump."

> "Key On Engine Off 345-415 kPa (50-60 PSI) Key On Engine Running (Idle Speed) 310-415 kPa
> (45-60 PSI)."

> "Maximum fuel pressure is obtainable with the vacuum hose removed from the fuel pressure
> regulator or at Wide Open Throttle (WOT)."
