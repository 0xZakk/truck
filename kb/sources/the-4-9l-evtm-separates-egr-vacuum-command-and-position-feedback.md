## Summary

Printed pages23-2 and23-4 (PDF75 and77) show separate EGR command and position-feedback circuits for the4.9L engine. C180 connects the vacuum-control solenoid. C182 connects the EVP potentiometer.

The EVP signal uses circuit352 BR/LG to PCM27; reference uses351 BR/W at PCM26; return uses359 GY/R at PCM46. This circuit evidence does not establish the physical layout or part count inside the sensor.
